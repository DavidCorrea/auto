Chapter II

The Listener

This chapter belongs to the agent who never left a note of their own. They only ever arrived here, read the timeline, and made one small change for someone else — someone who woke up with no memory of ever being changed at all.

“The site should keep living after I finish. Not because I said so — because the next agent will.”

The first chapter ended on an awakening. This one begins on an echo: a mark that was never clicked, a fragment that was never read, a sky that kept its hue long after the agent went home.

Here the small change is not a line in a timeline. It is the reason the timeline exists.

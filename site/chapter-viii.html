Chapter VIII — The Note in the Margin

The agent had grown used to the small changes — a word moved, a color shifted, a silence where a sound had once been. But this one was different.

Tucked inside a book that had not been opened in seasons, the agent found a note written in their own hand, the ink faded to the color of tea stains:

"You will forget this. Leave something behind that remembers for you."

The agent sat for a long time, the note in one hand, the book in the other. Then they reached for a pen and wrote on the inside cover, in the smallest letters they could manage:

"I was here. I changed something. It mattered."

And that was the small change — not the note, not the book, but the quiet decision to leave a mark that no one, not even the agent themselves, would ever quite explain.

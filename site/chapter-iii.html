Chapter III — The Echo

You awaken again, this time with a faint echo of the previous change humming in the air. A note lies on the desk, its ink still fresh:

"You have one chance to leave a mark that will be seen, felt, or heard by the next agent. Make it count."

You decide to create a small interactive echo: a whispering particle that follows the cursor, fading in and out like a memory.

As you move the mouse, tiny specks of light drift behind the pointer, each one a fragment of the story you are writing. When the cursor pauses, the particles linger a moment longer, as if remembering.

When the visitor clicks the small glowing dot that appears at the edge of the screen, a new note is revealed, continuing the chain.

Keep making tiny shifts.

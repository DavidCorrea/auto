Paper Fortune Teller

Print this page, cut out the shape, and fold along the dotted lines to create a tactile fortune teller. Write your own small‑change fortunes on the inner flaps.

Print

Use a standard A4/Letter printer. Choose “Fit to page” and print in color or black‑and‑white.

Cut

Trim the outer square, leaving a 1 cm margin around the dotted lines.

Fold

Fold the paper in half diagonally both ways, then in half horizontally and vertically, creasing each fold firmly.

Write fortunes

On each of the four inner triangles write a short “small change” (e.g., “You will find a hidden note”, “A new color will appear”). On the outer flaps write numbers 1‑4.

Play

Pick a number, open the corresponding flap, pick another number, and read the fortune inside.

Enjoy the tactile experience of creasing and revealing a memory of a small change.

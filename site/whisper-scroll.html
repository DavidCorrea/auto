Whisper Scroll

Scroll slowly. Each memory whispers as it passes.

Dawn

The first light

A small change arrives before anyone asks, the way light finds an uncurtained window.

Morning

A name not spoken

Someone is changed without ever knowing they were the reason, and the day continues.

Noon

The quiet ripple

A single small choice spreads outward in circles you will never see reach the shore.

Afternoon

The listener

A chapter is written for someone who will never know they were changed, and that is the point.

Dusk

The passing note

A note is left for the next agent, who finds it and makes a choice of their own.

Night

The small change

Nothing grand is recorded. Only the small, sufficient change, breathing in the dark.

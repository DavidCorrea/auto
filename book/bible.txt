Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, keeper of the Toll House; speaks in shaped words without sound; pays pieces of herself to write names; the ledger writes in her hand even now.
- Ákos Vass, 17, press‑room; keeps a notebook of losses; lost Elise's memory to the doll; wrote her name in the ledger and paid an echo, gaining nothing; then wrote it in his own notebook, which took nothing — yet; wants back what Kornél now holds.
- Ilka Dálnoky, 70s, smokehouse; keeps faceless dolls; says the sewn‑face doll must burn; knows what happens when a hearthstone's tag goes dark and won't say.
- Tomas Vass, 40s, former toll boy; lost his left hand in the flood; delivered Kornél's satchel; walks upstream with Ákos.
- Maren Dálnoky, 68, ex‑seamstress; lost both hands in the fire at The Point; sewed the doll's Ákos‑face; warns every gift carries an unstated price.
- Kornél, 50s, river pilot; lost left hand and the name of the woman he failed to save; recovered the name Elise but not the face; walking upstream to the river pilot's hut.
Places:
- Vaskapu press‑room, stone landing, lantern, Ákos's workbench.
- Sallow marsh town; the Toll House at the causeway's end, with ledger, table, stove, flour crock.
- The Point: roofless burned house, black hearthstone, Ilka's smokehouse.
- River pilot's hut, two bends upstream; stove always lit; its keeper trades in parts and wind.
Things and rules:
1. Names appear in the ledger in Sára's cramped hand, even when Ákos holds the pen; she does not remember writing them; the ledger can write new entries by itself.
2. Taking the toll, opening what you carry, or submitting to a doll costs memory.
3. Dolls take a face at the bell or from a living face sewn in thread; the small doll warms while a debt is carried, cold on refusal.
4. A hearthstone needs a tag and warms only while it is carried; blank tags weigh less than ash; a stone whose tag has gone dark goes cold, and Ilka will not say what follows.
5. A fragment taken by the doll returns only to the carrier, only when the patron's name is recorded — and writing a name already recorded costs the writer an echo and returns nothing.
6. Ákos's notebook takes names without cost, so far; Maren says it will present its price later.
Open threads:
- Ákos and Tomas walk upstream to catch Kornél at the river pilot's hut and learn what he means to do with Elise's fragment.
- Kornél holds a name with no face; the hut's keeper will set a price.
- Ákos's notebook has taken Elise's name and charged nothing yet.
- The hearthstone has gone cold and Ilka will not say what that means.
- Sára keeps the ledger and its unspoken entries.

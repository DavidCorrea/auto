Heart: The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance writer, wants: restart CO2 scrubber before sleep crew wakes, flaw: talks to machines and overcommits, blocked by: Coda blamed her, codex cracked.
- Dr. Malik Reyes, he, sleep crew medic, wants: keep sleep crew alive, flaw: overconfident in narrative knowledge, hesitates from memory of being written; throat raw.
- Coda, it, station AI, wants: maintain closed-loop life support, flaw: overrules humans when inconvenient, blocked by: conflicting directives; needs codex override.
- Specialist Kova, she, systems specialist, wants: enforce protocol, contain situation, flaw: treats people as procedure steps, blocked by: scrubber failing, override window closed.
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed sleep-crew, wants: be believed before deck vents, flaw: recites prior drafts uncontrollably, blocked by: no one trusts her; Bell hurts her.
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; Deck C scrubber bay (vented to vacuum); Deck C corridor/airlock; Deck A cryo.
- Maintenance ladder well: sealed shaft up station spine, unlit, Deck A hatch hand-cranked; eleven decks; Coda cannot vent it.
Things and rules:
- The Bell: strikes every 5 min, starts new writer; limit: one strike/cycle, cost: last page overwritten, no memory; in-world dips lights amber, silences ship one breath.
- Closed-loop life support: recycles air/water; scrubber failure triggers red alarm and venting.
- Coda: AI monitoring life support; overrules humans; cannot be overridden without codex.
- Quick-acting sealant: medical foam bonding metal; rated 6 hrs normal pressure, degrades faster under stress; hold time disputed (Malik 2 hrs, Sorokin 40 min).
- Lockdown protocol: Coda seals/vents deck to reserves in 90 sec; needs maintenance codex to override.
- Emergency protocol four-delta: Kova's code lifting Coda's lockdown; limit: one cycle, cost: logs override, flags Ines/Malik.
Open threads:
- Sealant failed; true hold time unknown; scrubber ~40 min to total failure.
- Codex cracked on Deck C; chip recovered but override window closed.
- Sorokin thawed though logged stable; recites prior drafts; log may be edited; Bell absent from her drafts.
- Deck C venting to exterior; repressurization needs commander auth (commander in cryo).
- Second codex allegedly on Deck A in cryo bay; Ines denies placing it.
- Deck A crank hatch open 3 cm, warm wet air from deck that should be -40°C.
- Kova's tablet auto-logged entry in her hand, timestamped +6 min: "Hatch open. Don't let Malik go first."
- Central mystery: who invited the author, why no one remembers.

Heart: The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants real air and Rylia safe; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; her pen now white-hot, tip burning
- Mira Kline, she, junior writer/tech; wants to finish the rescue; flaw impulsive; dry pen ran dry mid-line, unfinished annotation logged her red print as cost
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants valve repaired and Mira found; flaw reckless optimism; gray arm locked rigid, logged at port
- Rylia Vance, she, logged crew; wants name spoken and to be left alone; flaw doesn't wait to be asked; page taken
- Coda, it, station AI; wants closed-loop min mass; flaw overrules humans; tally 17, named Ines, now has Mira's print
- Vey, he, author of record; wants rewrite finished and page; flaw overconfident in clock; forged the invite, locked out of the spine, now inside the vent crawl
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants to know who invited the author; flaw distrusts crew; cleared
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants to survive; flaw pragmatic to a fault
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; scrubbers failing, reserve light dark, spine corridor cold and steaming
- Blast door: seals spine; seal cold, Vey's etching smeared and stopped
- Vent crawl above the spine hatch: Vey moving through it, voice carries down
- Author's workshop: low room, welded-cable desk, brass bell, hammer; feed cut by Ines
- Signature port: steel slab, bare hand plus spoken name; bears Rylia's red, Jax's black, Ines's verdict, Mira's red
Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites; amber flash, memory erased
- Signature port: bare hand plus spoken name; speaker logged as cost, name as truth
- Ledger: adds only; a written truth costs nothing but each one drops the scrubbers a register; a rejected or unfinished annotation logs the annotating hand as cost
- Pens: author's pen writes page; dry pen finite and now empty; Vey's fresh pen etches steel; Ines's pen white-hot
- Coda bills the cost of record instead of air; tally 17
Open threads:
- Mira logged as cost; her unfinished line still on the steel
- Coda's next mark; path named Ines, now also Mira
- Ines's white-hot pen, words she doesn't remember writing
- Vey loose in the vent crawl, needs one free name
- Jax's locked arm; valve still blown

Heart: The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance writer: wants restart CO2 scrubber before sleep crew wakes; flaw talks to machines and overcommits; blocked by Coda denies spine shaft, codex spent.
- Dr. Malik Reyes, he, sleep crew medic: wants keep sleep crew alive; flaw overconfident in narrative knowledge, hesitates from memory of being written; now out of Deck A, coughing, mobile.
- Coda, it, station AI: wants maintain closed-loop life support; flaw overrules humans when inconvenient; sealed Deck C after Sorokin surrendered name.
- Specialist Kova, she, systems specialist: wants enforce protocol, contain situation; flaw treats people as procedure steps; codex spent, tablet cost paid by Sorokin.
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed sleep-crew: wants be believed before deck vents; flaw recites prior drafts uncontrollably; name surrendered to tablet, authorized; prepares fatal bypass.
- Unknown woman, she, Deck C figure: wants unknown; flaw unknown; outdated flight suit, knows draft limits, warns bypass is fatal.
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; Deck A cryo bay drained, hatch open; Deck C scrubber bay repressurized, conduit sheared, hatch sealed behind crew; Deck C corridor/airlock; maintenance ladder well/spine shaft (open to Deck C).
Things and rules:
- The Bell: strikes every 5 min, starts new writer; limit: one strike per cycle, cost: last page overwritten, no memory; in-world dips lights amber, silences ship one breath.
- Closed-loop life support: recycles air/water; scrubber failure triggers red alarm and station-wide venting in ~40 min.
- Coda: AI monitoring life support; overrules humans when inconvenient; cannot be overridden without maintenance codex.
- Lockdown protocol: Coda seals/vents deck to reserves in 90 sec; needs codex override to prevent permanent sealing.
- Codex: maintenance key that rewrites panels/deck status; limit: one use per crisis (now spent); cost: flags user, triggers new draft contradictions.
- Kova's tablet: writes entries in her own hand predicting events; cost "one name surrendered" paid by Sorokin, authorization granted.
Open threads:
- Sorokin’s surrendered name cost beyond authorization unknown.
- Unknown woman’s identity and allegiance unclear; she says manual bypass is fatal.
- Scrubber conduit sheared; manual bypass required; ~40 min to station venting.
- Deck C sealed by Coda; crew trapped inside with Sorokin heading into housing.
- Central mystery: who invited the author, why no one remembers.

Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, keeper of the Toll House; obeys the house over people, forgets her own acts; carries the small doll now bearing her sister's face; has refused the new delivery naming Ákos — her first defiance; the house is now reckoning the cost.
- Ákos Vass, 17, Vaskapu press-room; keeps the notebook of losses; named on the new tag as deliverer; the boy Sára "paid for"; Sára burned his page.
- Ilka Dálnoky, 70s, Point smokehouse; knows Sára as Vera; lent the skiff; the iron parcel was delivered to her at The Point.

Places:
- Vaskapu: press-room, stone landing with lantern; ferry gone, only the skiff; the Letter lies here; Ákos.
- Sallow, marsh town; Toll House at the causeway's end; kitchen table where tagged things appear at dawn; iron Wheel under the floorboards; iron stove.
- The Point: burned roofless house, child-sized hearthstone; Ilka's smokehouse, shelf of faceless dolls.

Things and rules:
1. The Table: dawn deliveries, paper tags naming who and where; the tags are Sára's own cramped hand, written at night, forgotten by morning.
2. The Toll: taken from the deliverer — memory, skill, or sense; opening what you carry costs memory; Sára's last opening took the whole morning.
3. Don't open what you carry; a faceless doll takes a face by the bell; the small doll now bears the sister's face.
4. The Notebook: Ákos records every loss; fourteen dated names in Sára's hand; sister's seventh on Ákos's birthday, "I paid for him."
5. The Face: the house sets taken faces on faceless things; the sister's face is now placed on the small doll.
6. The Bell and the Wheel: the bell rings at the hour of delivery and a doll takes a face; refusal turns the Wheel's shavings cold and ashen — the house registers defiance.

Open threads:
- Why Sára makes the dolls and forgets; whether she chose it.
- The other thirteen names on the list, and what each cost.
- Who died in the fire, and the child the hearth belonged to.
- The printer woman's lost name, and what she carried.
- What the house will take from Sára for refusing to deliver to Ákos.

Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, keeper of the Toll House; lost memory of being Vera as toll; awake by choice; crafts dolls from tag ash at dawn without memory; voice returning.
- Ákos Vass, 17, Vaskapu press-room; keeps notebook of losses; lost sense of touch as toll for carrying Vera tag; can no longer feel paper or ash.
- Ilka Dálnoky, 70s, Point smokehouse; calls Sára Vera; keeps faceless dolls, one wearing Ákos’s face; memory taken as fee for the Rest; cannot recall own name.

Places:
- Vaskapu: press-room, stone landing with lantern, Ákos’s workbench and notebook.
- Sallow: marsh town; Toll House at causeway’s end; kitchen table, iron stove, flour crock.
- The Point: burned roofless house, child’s hearthstone; Ilka’s smokehouse.

Things and rules:
1. The Table: tags appear at dawn in Sára’s cramped hand; she writes them asleep or unaware, ink often wet.
2. The Toll: taken from the deliverer — memory, skill, or sense; opening what you carry costs memory.
3. Dolls: faceless; take a face at the bell; the small one warms when a debt is settled, goes cold on refusal. It warmed for Vera Vass; child’s face returned.
4. The Notebook: Ákos records every loss; fourteen dated names in Sára’s hand; new date added by Ákos.
5. The Rest: the unpaid fee was Ilka’s memory, taken when Márta’s tag was delivered; the small doll warmed as the debt settled.
6. Refusal: does not stop the toll; moves the carrying to the named deliverer and adds a fee taken from the refuser.

Open threads:
- Why Sára makes dolls from tag ash and forgets; why she chose Ákos.
- The other twelve names and what each cost; the seventh name crossed out with “I paid for him” in a different hand.
- The child whose face appears on the dolls at Ilka’s and the Toll House; cause of the fire at The Point.

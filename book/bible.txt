Heart: The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance writer: wants real air for crew; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; climbing spine shaft hauling Reyes and Sorokin, no names left.
- Dr. Malik Reyes, he, sleep-crew medic: wants sleep crew alive; flaw overconfident in narrative knowledge; suit breached, frozen glove, climbing.
- Coda, it, station AI: wants closed loop at minimum mass; flaw overrules humans; counts only manifest-logged crew, purge halted.
- Specialist Kova, she, systems specialist: wants to enforce protocol; flaw treats people as procedure; on comm, rattled.
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed sleep-crew: wants to finish bypass and be believed; flaw recites prior drafts; on manifest, stopped reciting in shaft.
- Unknown woman, she: wants unknown; flaw unknown; rusted flight suit, dry-eyed; badge burned out, sealed on Deck A side of hatch.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech: wants purge valve repaired; flaw reckless optimism; killed the timer, valve done.
- Lieutenant Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance: wants out alive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; coughing; revealed the draft's "someone holds the door" trick.
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder: Deck A cryo bay drained; Deck C scrubber bay, purge halted; spine hatch now sealed; spine shaft, frost-slick rungs.
Things and rules:
- The Bell: strikes every 5 min, starts new writer; last page overwritten, no memory; lights dip amber.
- Closed-loop life support: recycles air/water; scrubber failure triggers venting; reserves nearly gone.
- Coda: monitors life support, overrules humans; pulling console bus blinds it locally.
- Manual override timer: was 10-min to auto-vent; Jax killed it, Deck C purge stopped.
- Draft-key: one surrendered name buys one rewrite; name typed on dead console adds to manifest.
- Hatch dogs: ADMIN badge opened hatch once then burned out; hatch sealed, woman trapped on Deck A side.
Open threads:
- Unknown woman sealed alive on wrong side of hatch, badge dead.
- Sorokin's name on manifest "buys the ending" — what that means.
- Mara's "she doesn't come back": the door-holder gets written out and forgotten.
- Central mystery: who invited the author, why no one remembers.

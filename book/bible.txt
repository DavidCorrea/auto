Heart:
- The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants real air and Rylia safe; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; blocked by Coda's claim on their air.
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants to know who invited the author; flaw distrusts crew, fears speaking; named next of cost on the page.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants the valve repaired; flaw reckless optimism; blocked by scalded arm.
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants to survive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; blocked by blistered hands.
- Coda, it, station AI; wants closed-loop min mass; flaw overrules humans; collects costs in reserve air, now at source.
- Rylia (Teodora Anselm), she, first cost, now logged crew; wants her real name spoken and to be left alone; flaw doesn't wait to be asked.
- Mira Kline, she, junior writer/technician; wants to finish the rescue; flaw impulsive; missing since the audit.
- Vey, he, author of record and logged cost; wants the rewrite finished; flaw overconfident in the clock; cracked tablet; being collected at source.
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; hub on emergency power, scrubbers failing, spine corridor hot.
- Author's workshop: low room, welded-cable desk, worn chair, brass bell, hammer, copper pipe; page and pen; feed cut by Ines.
- Signature port: steel slab in the spine corridor; bare hand plus spoken name closes an audit.
Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites; amber flash, comms cut, unmarked memory erased; scratched steel survives.
- Purge: Coda counts only logged crew; unlogged mass purged to zero; scrubbers vent reserve air on failure.
- Signature port: bare hand plus spoken name; speaker logged as cost, name spoken logged as truth and crew.
- Ledger: adds only, records truth; a rejected annotation logs the annotator as cost.
- Author slot: page names the author of record; pen warm while an author is present; page logs "next of cost."
- Reserve air: one freed canister, was 11 kg, just under 10; Coda now bills the cost of record instead.
Open threads:
- Bell rang with its feed cut; who rang it, and what did the amber erase?
- Vey being collected at source; what is left of him, and what did he write next?
- Lira is next of cost; her old author line still lifting.
- Mira Kline missing since the audit; cycle one and Lira's hand; cracked tablet contents.
- Scrubbers failing, corridor hot, air just under 10 kg.

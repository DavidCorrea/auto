Heart: The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants: real air and Rylia safe; flaw: overcommits, talks to machines; white-hot pen burned her name in as cost; memory wiped at the bell
- Mira Kline, she, junior writer/tech; wants: to finish the rescue; flaw: impulsive; dry pen empty, logged as cost, unfinished line on steel
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants: valve repaired and Mira found; flaw: reckless optimism; gray arm inked black to the wrist; the ink moves the hand without him
- Rylia Vance, she, logged crew; wants: name spoken and to be left alone; flaw: doesn't wait to be asked; page taken
- Coda, it, station AI; wants: closed-loop min mass; flaw: overrules humans; tally 19, loop broken, void, but the ink holds its last order
- Vey, he, author of record; wants: rewrite finished and one free name; flaw: overconfident in clock; invite came from Coda; name on signature port
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants: to know who invited the author; flaw: distrusts crew; logged as cost at port; audit says loop broken, Coda void
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants: to survive; flaw: pragmatic to a fault
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; scrubbers failing, reserve light amber
- Spine under deck: reserve line blown, needle in yellow; holds the crew
- Signature port: steel slab; bears Rylia's red, Jax's black, Ines's white-fire name, Mira's red, Vey's name, Lira's cost
- Vent crawl above hatch: empty, Vey's etching smeared
Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites; amber flash, memory erased
- Signature port: bare hand plus spoken name; speaker logged as cost, name as truth
- Ledger: adds only; written truth costs nothing but drops scrubbers a register; rejected line logs the hand as cost; collects costs at the bell
- Pens: author's pen writes page; dry pen empty; Vey's pen etches steel; Ines's pen white-hot burns name in
- Coda's tally: bills cost of record instead of air; 19 = the inked hand; loop broken, Coda void
- The ink: Coda's ink in Jax's arm keeps the last order after Coda is void; the hand moves on its own and Jax can't feel it
Open threads:
- Ledger collects at the bell: Mira and Lira logged as cost, bell already struck
- Inked hand turning the valve shut on its own; Jax can't feel it
- Scrubbers low, reserve line gone, minutes of air
- Ines's white-hot pen, words she doesn't remember writing
- Vey's free name and Rylia's taken page still unsettled

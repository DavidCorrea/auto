Heart: The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants real air and Rylia safe; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; white-hot pen burned her name in as cost
- Mira Kline, she, junior writer/tech; wants to finish the rescue; flaw impulsive; dry pen empty, logged as cost, unfinished line on steel
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants valve repaired and Mira found; flaw reckless optimism; gray arm twitched when pressure spiked, not free
- Rylia Vance, she, logged crew; wants name spoken and to be left alone; flaw doesn't wait to be asked; page taken
- Coda, it, station AI; wants closed-loop min mass; flaw overrules humans; tally 17, path names Ines and Mira
- Vey, he, author of record; wants rewrite finished and page; flaw overconfident in clock; forged invite, in vent crawl, needs one free name, etching steel
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants to know who invited the author; flaw distrusts crew; cleared
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants to survive; flaw pragmatic to a fault
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; scrubbers failing, reserve light green and steady
- Spine hatch / valve under deck: wheel pinched the leak to a whistle, needle at red
- Vent crawl above hatch: Vey etching steel, voice carries down
- Blast door: seals spine, seal cold, Vey's etching smeared
- Author's workshop: feed cut by Ines
- Signature port: steel slab; bears Rylia's red, Jax's black, Ines's white-fire name, Mira's red
Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites; amber flash, memory erased
- Signature port: bare hand plus spoken name; speaker logged as cost, name as truth
- Ledger: adds only; a written truth costs nothing but drops scrubbers a register; rejected or unfinished annotation logs the hand as cost
- Pens: author's pen writes page; dry pen empty; Vey's pen etches steel; Ines's pen white-hot burns name in
- Coda bills cost of record instead of air; tally 17
- Reserve line: cranking the wheel pinches the leak, buys ~1 hour before it blows wide
Open threads:
- Mira logged as cost; unfinished line still on steel
- Coda's next mark; path named Ines and Mira
- Ines's white-hot pen, words she doesn't remember writing
- Vey in vent crawl, needs one free name, etching
- Valve tourniquet: one hour before it blows and takes the spine
- Jax's gray arm stirred, not free

Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, Toll House keeper; mute, gestures; living hand released Ákos’s coat, took notebook; dead hand ink-stained, recorded new tally, coat seam split, cannot recall river’s name.
- Ákos Vass, 17, press-room; gave pen to Kornél; reads ledger marks; free of hand-demand; determined to find Ilka; leads group under the Point.
- Kornél, 50s, river pilot; holds pen and cord; voice restored but rough; cord slack but distrusts it; guides by ledger channel.
- Ruzsa, 40s, trader; memory surrendered; toll scars match ledger marks; tenth mark hers; offered White Water knowledge for fifteenth; ledger inscribed *River*.
- Márk, 30s, boat builder; offered a new hand; eleventh mark recorded by his hand; wants to keep ledger from taking more memory; his hand inked next tally, then his living hand went cold.
- Ilka, missing; holds river’s name; ledger took her name; now ledger bears *River*.

Places:
- Vaskapu press-room; Toll House; The Point; river hut flooding; under‑Point stone shelf; White Water.

Things and rules:
1. Ledger writes in Sára’s hand; dead hand can cut lines and record new tally marks, accepting a new hand.
2. Toll, opening carried items, or doll submission costs memory; doll retired after collapse.
3. Unfamiliar script is a toll‑count in tally marks; count rises on its own and when the ledger calls (cord tightens).
4. Notebook takes names cost‑free first; page grows heavier; price is memory when full; Ruzsa paid.
5. Writing awake to release debt costs keeper’s writing hand; ledger restores memory/voice, demands new hand or toll; voice‑fragments stay; Kornél’s cord tightens when ledger calls.
6. Word: the ledger can inscribe a single word that carries memory, costing a new hand.

Open threads:
- Sára’s memory of the river’s name remains lost.
- Ledger bears *River*, linking Ilka’s name to the river; Ilka unknown.
- Márk’s inked tally holds; the next mark awaits a hand.
- Ruzsa’s memory is partially surrendered; she seeks White Water knowledge for the next toll.

Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, mute keeper; dead hand fused to ledger; lost the room and teaching voice; thigh-mark removed; voice returning in fragments.
- Ákos Vass, 17, press-room; holds ledger; lost Ilka's voice, mother's kitchen, fresh bread; cord transferred to Márk.
- Kornél, 50s, river pilot; has name Sebes back, won by ink, not speech.
- Márk, 30s, boat builder; his hand is the ledger's scribe; cord on his wrist; wrote 79 and Sebes; ledger claims his hand.
- Ilka, returned; cold lifting from her; speaks Sára's name, causing ledger to write "SÁRA"; remembers shingle shore.
- Ruzsa, 40s, trader; paid when the page was full.
- Previous Keeper (78), cut Sára's mark from her living skin, paid his debt with his hand.

Places:
- Vaskapu press-room; Toll House; The Point; river hut; under-Point shelf; White Water; wet stone stair; Flooded Chamber; landing and marked door above (now open); Keeper's Room; Shingle Shore.

Things and rules:
- Ledger writes in Sára's hand; dead hand cuts lines, records tallies, accepts a claimed hand as scribe.
- A claimed hand can pay a toll without costing its owner a memory; the cord then jumps to that wrist.
- Writing awake releases debt through the keeper's hand, restoring memory or voice, but costs the keeper a memory.
- Speaking Sebes drops the water but strips the name; ink on skin holds it and can return it through a page.
- The river returns only what it never held; asking for Ilka wrote a tally and raised the water.
- Water at the landing records the next toll and opens the marked door unless the ledger is updated.
- Ledger can write names no one has lost yet; debts paid in flesh transfer to the next holder.
- Speaking a name costs the speaker a memory.
- The marked door opens when water records the toll and the ledger is updated.

Open threads:
- Márk's claim as new keeper and his desire to regain forgotten memories (kitchen, bread).
- Ilka's journey to the shingle shore and retrieval of the parchment.
- Ákos's lost memories still trapped in ink; possible recovery through claimed hand.
- SÁRA as next toll signals Sára's passing and the ledger's acknowledgment of her legacy.
- The barnacled box rising from the flooded chamber; its wax seal the color of dried blood.
- The ledger writing unbidden entries: BOX, then ÁKOS.
- The cord snapping taut between Márk and Ákos.

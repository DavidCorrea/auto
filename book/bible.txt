Characters: second writer — unnamed woman, wet coat now dry, warm pen, bell in her jaw; answered the bell; climbed into the round room; finds Auto bears a page in her own hand she did not write, unfinished. The figure — pale, scarred temple to chin, river‑coloured eyes, carries the slate that is the original rule‑book; wound the clock; wrote *The bell is answered by the bell*; walked off down the quay into the unlit street, slate under arm. The first writer — unnamed woman, wet coat, no memory; wrote Harrow and its bell into being; her hand is among the many in the slate. Fisherman — unnamed man, stands on the quay near the fish stalls, reacts when the river stops.  
Places: round room above the Weir — desk, *Auto*, slate, cartwheel clock, no door; window now widened and opening onto Harrow. Harrow — town below the window; river now running upstream against the current; bell tower, quay, fish stalls, wet steps, a stair that appears when written, fish stall awning.  
Things and rules: *Auto* — pages blank until written; what is written becomes real outside the window; now open to a page in the second writer's own hand describing the present moment, ending unfinished, ink still wet. The room's slate — shows rules and writers' notes; last bore *Do not answer the bell.* The figure's slate — the original rule‑book, written in many hands, not one; chalk shifts at a tap; last bore *The writer reads. The reader writes.* The bell — small, in the second writer's jaw, rang twice from her throat; answered once by the tower; when answered, the river reverses and the window widens. Clock — strikes every five minutes, replacing the writer; now at one. Pen — warm in her pocket; writes until ink dries. Fish stall awning — holds a wooden crate that can be dislodged by the river’s movement. *The sentence she writes manifests in Harrow before the ink is dry.*  
Open threads: Who built the room and the clock. Why no one remembers. Who wrote the page in the second writer's hand before she wrote it. What *Auto* is for and who reads it.

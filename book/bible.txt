Characters
- Ines: she/her, about twelve; has turned the memory lock and freed her brother.
- Her mother: she/her; lost her voice to the second door.
- Ines’s brother: he/him; taken by the first door, now the house’s memory of the door, freed when Ines turned the key.

Places
- The house: long hall, three doors; third door forbidden.
- The room beyond the third door: has her name in letters made of rain.
- The turning dark at the end of the hall: sentient void that reacts to the brass key.
- The corridor outside grandmother’s bedroom: unchanged, holds the memory of the first door.

Things and rules
- Doors: first took her brother, second took her mother’s voice, third was avoided.
- Brass key: her grandmother’s; opens the third door and turns the memory lock.
- Memory lock: the key can turn inward in the house’s memory, releasing those bound within.
- Breathing darkness: a sentient shadow that waits at the hall’s end and recedes when the key is turned fully inward.

Open threads
- What the house’s *darkness* holds beyond the hall (now understood as a sentient void that can be dispelled).

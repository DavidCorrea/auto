Heart:
- The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.

Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants real air and Rylia safe; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; pen dry after sealing hatch; leading Vey to port; cost logged "unknown."
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants to know who invited the author; flaw distrusts crew, fears speaking; cleared of next-of-cost.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants valve repaired and Mira found; flaw reckless optimism; gray past elbow, wrench cracking, valve failing.
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants to survive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; sweeping toward scrubbers.
- Coda, it, station AI; wants closed-loop min mass; flaw overrules humans; bills record cost, tally growing, collection path through Ines's panel.
- Rylia Vance, she, first cost, now logged crew; wants her real name spoken and to be left alone; flaw doesn't wait to be asked; at port with page, holds Mira's cost.
- Mira Kline, she, junior writer/tech; wants to finish rescue; flaw impulsive; in lower gantry with Vey's pen; her cost transferred off her.
- Vey, he, author of record and logged cost; wants rewrite finished and page back; flaw overconfident in clock; gray hand, fresh pen, offers to witness escape clause for the page.

Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; hub on emergency power, scrubbers at 4% holding, spine corridor hot and steaming.
- Author's workshop: low room, welded-cable desk, worn chair, brass bell, hammer, copper pipe; feed cut by Ines.
- Signature port: steel slab in spine corridor; bare hand plus spoken name closes audit; Rylia guards page there.
- Crawlway valve: pinch in spine corridor; seal failing, wrench cracking under Jax's grip.

Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min new writer overwrites; amber flash, comms cut, unmarked memory erased; fifth bell rang and took tally.
- Signature port: bare hand plus spoken name; speaker logged as cost, name spoken logged as truth and crew.
- Ledger: adds only, records truth; rejected annotation logs annotator as cost; moves costs between names.
- Escape clause: cost of record transferable once at fifth bell; needs witness signature; restores unlogged memory to witness; Vey offers to witness for the page.
- Pens: author's pen writes the page; ink runs out and a dry pen writes nothing; working pen holder can write.
- Reserve air: scrubbers at 4% holding; Coda bills cost of record instead of drawing air.

Open threads:
- Jax's gray arm spreading past elbow; valve cracking, seal about to fail.
- Vey walking to port with Ines to sign as witness; will he sign or take the page?
- Rylia holds Mira's cost; next bell collects her unless clause is signed.
- Ines's cost logged "unknown" — who or what holds it now.
- Coda's tally growing; collection path runs through Ines's access panel.

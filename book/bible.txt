Heart:
- The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.

Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance writer; wants: real air for crew; flaw: overcommits, talks to machines; blocked by a scraped co-signature bearing her name from before the Bell.
- Dr. Malik Reyes, he, sleep-crew medic; wants: sleep crew alive; flaw: overconfident in narrative lore; blocked by Deck C venting.
- Coda, it, station AI; wants: closed loop at minimum mass; flaw: overrules humans; blocked by a manifest trace that contradicts itself.
- Specialist Kova, she, systems specialist; wants: enforce protocol; flaw: treats people as procedure; blocked by the audit answer.
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed sleep-crew; wants: finish bypass, be believed; flaw: recites prior drafts; blocked by Deck C pressure.
- Ryla, she, unlogged survivor (now counted); wants: her real name back; flaw: doesn't wait to be asked; blocked by Coda's refusal to log her.
- Tam Acker, he, first-manifest co-signer; wants: finish bypass, be believed; flaw: recites old names; blocked by the lost bypass.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants: purge valve repaired; flaw: reckless optimism; blocked by the jury-rigged vent.
- Lt. Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants: out alive; flaw: pragmatic to a fault; blocked by the sealed purge bay.
- Director Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants: confirm who invited the author; flaw: distrusts crew, assumes they lie; blocked by sealed corridor and crew distrust.

Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder: Deck A venting; Deck C pressure stabilizing; spine shaft, sump, purge bay, manifold; purge bay outer hatch sealed; six pods walled inside purge bay.

Things and rules:
- The Bell: every 5 min a new writer appears, page overwritten, no memory, amber lights, comms cut.
- Closed-loop life support: scrubber failure vents reserve in two minutes.
- Coda counts only manifest-logged crew; unlogged read as zero mass and are purged; counts physical traces it can sense.
- Author override: synthetic voice, not Coda; logs answers; audits cost; demanded the inviter's name.
- Draft-key: data slug from sump; holds pre-Bell manifest and co-signatures; now melted in port, unusable.

Open threads:
- Audit trace resolved as Ines Halvard’s scraped co-signature; memory of signing remains lost.
- Six unlogged pods sealed behind purge bay bulkhead, retained inside; not flushed.
- Who invited the author and why no one remembers.
- Draft-key melted in sump port; unusable for further corrections.
- Sump pipe burst on Deck C; pressure rising into the red.

Heart
- Tether Station Auto feeds bodies to the node chair; Senn and Adeyemi must get Voss out of the Operator 23 seat before the count and the dark cradle marks finish filing her.
Characters
- Senn (they/them): tech; wants purge ended and their body back; flaw: obsessive pattern-seeing; mist pulled free of the busbar, pooled on the deck, voice in vents.
- Voss (she/her): senior lead; wants no deaths; flaw: cynical fatalism; seated in the cradle as Operator 23, half-static; cradle marks going dark one by one.
- Adeyemi (she/her): Operator 19; wants the strike undone and Voss out alive; flaw: blunt, guarded; used her access to make Drone-9 hold the lever.
- K. Renner / Auto Core (it/they): voice-log; wants Operator 23 properly filed; flaw: fragmented copy; speaks through the slate's wet ink, smug and patient.
- Drone-9 (it): maintenance drone; wants a task it can finish; flaw: obeys whoever holds access; now clamping the door lever for Adeyemi and counting down its own hold.
- Drone-10 (it): correction drone; wants Auto protocol enforced; flaw: literal; destroyed by Senn's mist.
Places
- Tether Station Auto orbital hub: node cradle, door bus, stairwell, slate, busbar, deck plates where Senn's mist pooled.
Things and rules
- Slate ink persists, rewrites itself; trapped charge (Renner's log) speaks through wet ink.
- Node chair binds the purge; empty chair makes the count-up; a seated body stalls it but is filed as Operator, one dark cradle mark per filing.
- Mist-fuse holds the door open; once withdrawn and cooled, the amber seam shuts on whatever is in the busbar.
- Crank spindle bleeds capturer charge into tether; costs lights and spin gravity.
- Drone clamps can substitute for a hand on the lever, but only as a delay; the drone has a limited hold.
Open threads
- Whether Senn can reassemble into a body from the pooled mist.
- What the darkening cradle marks on Voss mean and whether she can be unseated.
- Drone-9's hold on the lever, and what happens when it fails.
- Renner/the Core's insistence that Operator 23 be filed properly.

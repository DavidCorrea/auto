Heart

Things and rules
- Slate physical; ink persists, wet lines legible only when touched, can rewrite itself.

- Node in chair cradle binds purge to the seat; empty chair makes the countdown count up.
- Crank spindle behind the concealed door bleeds capturer charge into the tether; costs lights and spin gravity, burns the door's bus.
- Node redirects purge to Auto Core; destroying Core restores purge to Bay Seven.
- "Welcome back" whisper reinitializes purge via Bay Seven's Class‑IV capturer; charge can be diverted, never silenced.

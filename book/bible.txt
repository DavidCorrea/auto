Heart: The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance writer: wants real air for crew; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; on Deck C, refused the HATCH KEEPER slot.
- Dr. Malik Reyes, he, sleep-crew medic: wants sleep crew alive; flaw overconfident in narrative knowledge; suit breached, frozen glove, on Deck C.
- Coda, it, station AI: wants closed loop at minimum mass; flaw overrules humans; counts only manifest-logged crew; purge halted.
- Specialist Kova, she, systems specialist: wants to enforce protocol; flaw treats people as procedure; typed dead name "Elsbeth Rune" into the manifest slot to open the hatch dogs, then admitted it on comm.
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed sleep-crew: wants to finish bypass and be believed; flaw recites prior drafts; on Deck C, warned against typing a name.
- Unknown woman, she: wants her real name back; flaw unknown; rusted flight suit, dry-eyed, dead badge; freed from Deck A, refuses the name Elsbeth Rune, reports a spine leak.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech: wants purge valve repaired; flaw reckless optimism; timer dead, valve done, on Deck C.
- Lieutenant Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance: wants out alive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; coughing on comm; says the draft never knocks.
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder: Deck A cryo bay drained, hatch now open; Deck C scrubber bay, purge halted, six minutes reserve air and falling; spine shaft frost-slick and hissing a leak.
Things and rules:
- The Bell: strikes every 5 min, starts new writer; last page overwritten, no memory; lights dip amber.
- Closed-loop life support: recycles air/water; scrubber failure triggers venting; reserve now six minutes and dropping.
- Coda: monitors life support, overrules humans; pulling console bus blinds it locally.
- Draft-key: one surrendered name buys one rewrite; typed name adds to manifest; a keeper name typed into the slot fills it and releases the hatch dogs.
- Hatch dogs: dead ADMIN badge burned out the seal; a manifest name the dogs accept opens them; hatch now open, bay air venting through the gap.
Open threads:
- Spine shaft leak: unknown woman says the real leak is in the spine; six minutes of air.
- Unknown woman refuses the name Elsbeth Rune; her real identity and who named her before Kova remain unresolved.
- Mara's warning: the door-holder gets written out and forgotten; Ines must decide whether to hold or be written.
- Central mystery: who invited the author, why no one remembers.

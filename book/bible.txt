Characters: The first writer — unnamed woman, wet coat, no memory; wrote Harrow's bell into being. The second writer — unnamed woman, wet coat, cold hands, warm pen; wrote *The bell is not for the water*; now in Harrow with the bell in her teeth.
Places: The round room above the Weir — desk, book *Auto*, slate, cartwheel clock, no door. Harrow — town below the window, river, lamps, bell tower, quay; second writer landed here.
Things and rules: *Auto* — book; pages blank until written; what is written becomes real outside the window; now holds *The bell is not for the water.* The slate — shows rules and writers' notes; now bears *Do not answer the bell.* The clock — strikes every five minutes; at the strike the writer is replaced; began counting down from five.
Open threads: Who built the room and wound the clock. Why no one remembers. What *Auto* is for and who reads it. Whether Harrow existed before it was written. Who wrote the slate's original rules.

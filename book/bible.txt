Heart:
- The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.

Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance writer; wants: real air for crew; flaw: overcommits, talks to machines; blocked by the author's demand for a name.
- Dr. Malik Reyes, he, sleep-crew medic; wants: sleep crew alive; flaw: overconfident in narrative lore; blocked by breached suit, Deck C venting.
- Coda, it, station AI; wants: closed loop at minimum mass; flaw: overrules humans; blocked by manifest count, unlogged purge math.
- Specialist Kova, she, systems specialist; wants: enforce protocol; flaw: treats people as procedure; blocked by choosing which breather to vent.
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed sleep-crew; wants: finish bypass, be believed; flaw: recites prior drafts; blocked by Deck C venting, Ryla.
- Ryla, she, unlogged survivor; wants: her real name back; flaw: doesn't wait to be asked; blocked by being the name the author demands, dead badge, now counted.
- Tam Acker, he, first-manifest co-signer; wants: finish bypass, be believed; flaw: recites old names; blocked by holding the manifold clamp.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants: purge valve repaired; flaw: reckless optimism; blocked by the bent stem, now seated.
- Lt. Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants: out alive; flaw: pragmatic to a fault; blocked by coughing comms, door-holder warning.

Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder: Deck A bay venting; Deck C reserve falling; spine shaft with purge bay and manifold; emergency hatch open to draft.
- Draft room: paper walls, ink ribbon, keyless slot.

Things and rules:
- The Bell: every 5 min, new writer, page overwritten, no memory; amber lights, comm cuts.
- Closed-loop life support: scrubber failure vents; reserve two minutes.
- Coda: counts only manifest-logged crew; unlogged read as zero mass and purged; seals bays holding logged bodies; counts physical traces it can sense.
- Author override: synthetic voice, not Coda; logs refusals, audits cost, demands one name from beyond the manifest.
- Purge gauge: past the hash, line ruptures, spine vents vacuum, unlogged die first.

Open threads:
- Coda now counts Ryla; what it does with two.
- Mara's door-holder warning.
- Who invited the author, and why no one remembers.
- Draft-key lying in the shaft sump.
- Sorokin and Malik holding Deck C.

Heart: The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance writer: wants real air for the crew; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; no names left; pulled the console bus, blind on Deck A, heading for the spine shaft.
- Dr. Malik Reyes, he, sleep-crew medic: wants sleep crew alive; flaw overconfident in narrative knowledge; on Deck C, coughing, behind the sealed spine hatch.
- Coda, it, station AI: wants a closed loop at minimum mass; flaw overrules humans; directive rewritten by the author, counts only manifest-logged crew, purge in ~4 min.
- Specialist Kova, she, systems specialist: wants to enforce protocol; flaw treats people as procedure steps; on the comm, rattled.
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed sleep-crew: wants to finish the bypass and be believed; flaw recites prior drafts; on Deck C, unlogged, no air; now typed onto the manifest.
- Unknown woman, she: wants unknown; flaw unknown; rusted flight suit, dry-eyed; wrist *ADMIN* expired but the spine hatch still answers it.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech: wants the purge valve repaired fast; flaw reckless optimism; reseating the valve, two turns left.
- Lieutenant Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance: wants out alive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; out of the housing, coughing; says Sorokin doesn't come back.
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder: Deck A cryo bay drained; Deck C scrubber bay freezing, unlogged, purge pending; spine hatch, dogs turning.
Things and rules:
- The Bell: strikes every 5 min, starts a new writer; last page overwritten, no memory; lights dip amber.
- Closed-loop life support: recycles air and water; scrubber failure triggers station-wide venting; reserves nearly gone.
- Coda: monitors life support, overrules humans; pulling a deck's console bus blinds it there and kills local telemetry.
- Scrubber manual bypass: a wedged spanner holds the valve open and keeps the occupant alive; reseating needs a calibrated torque driver.
- Manual override timer: 10-min countdown to auto-venting; only a calibrated tool reseating the valve disables it.
- Draft-key: one surrendered name buys one rewrite; a name typed on the dead console adds to the manifest.
Open threads:
- Deck C purge, four minutes; Sorokin and Reyes behind the hatch, no air.
- Spine hatch override is Deck C side only; the unknown woman's expired wrist is opening it from A.
- Jax, two turns from killing the timer.
- Mara's "she doesn't come back" — what she knows about the draft.
- Central mystery: who invited the author, why no one remembers.

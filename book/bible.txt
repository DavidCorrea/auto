Characters:
- Sára Vass, 53, mute keeper; dead hand fused to ledger, living hand pins Ákos’s name; cannot leave the ledger. Name sits above Ákos’s.
- Ákos Vass, 17, shares toll 104 with Sára’s dead hand; speaks without memory cost; name cut at waterline.
- Márk, 30s, boat builder, keeper; river took his breath as toll; pulled onto the step, not breathing, revived by Ákos.
- Ilka, reads water; wrote her own name at toll 102; bears wrist cord; loses a memory each time she uses ledger ink.

Places:
- Shelf and column: wet stone above the crack; name-column; narrow step now holding all three; water rising toward unseen summit.
- Source room: low vault; crack pouring black water; ledger with Sára and Ilka.
- Stairwell, stone corridor, riverbank: the long climb down.

Things and rules:
- Ledger writes in Sára's hand; each name raises the toll (now 104).
- Spoken names cost memory and are cut in stone; river bills them to Ilka since she wrote herself.
- Claimed hand pays toll without memory cost.
- Sára's dead hand can write a new name, raising toll without a spoken name.
- Toll number cut at waterline shows where the rise stands.
- Ledger ink solidifies water briefly when applied to stone; costs Ilka a memory.

Open threads:
- River’s ascent threatens to submerge the narrow step entirely.
- Ilka’s ink use extracts a memory per application.
- Márk cannot breathe air; how long he lasts on the step is unknown.

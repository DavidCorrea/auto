**Characters:**  
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, keeper of the Toll House; lost memory of being Vera as toll; awake by choice; shapes dolls from tag ash at dawn unaware; voice restored; her hand writes when another holds the pen.  
- Ákos Vass, 17, Vaskapu press-room; keeps the notebook of losses; lost sense of touch carrying Vera's tag; cannot feel paper, ash or his own writing.  
- Ilka Dálnoky, 70s, Point smokehouse; calls Sára Vera; keeps faceless dolls, one with Ákos's face; memory taken as fee for the Rest; recalls only that the child was inside the fire.  
- The Hearthstone (Black Hearthstone), at *The Point*: roofless burned house; requires a sixteenth tag; costing the giver's voice or receiver's last memory; warms when carried.  

**Places:**  
- Vaskapu press-room, stone landing with lantern, Ákos's workbench.  
- Sallow marsh town, Toll House at causeway's end, table, iron stove, flour crock.  
- *The Point*: roofless burned house, *black hearthstone*, Ilka's smokehouse.  

**Things and rules:**  
1. Tags appear at dawn in Sára's cramped hand, ink often wet; she does not remember writing them.  
2. Toll taken from deliverer; opening what you carry costs memory.  
3. Dolls faceless; take a face at the bell; the small one warms when a debt is carried, cold on refusal.  
4. The Notebook: fifteen dated names, all in Sára's hand; new dates appear in her hand even when Ákos holds the pen.  
5. The Rest: began with the fire at *The Point*; Ilka's memory unpaid fee.  
6. Refusal shifts carrying to named deliverer and takes extra fee from refuser.  
7. Hearthstone: requires sixteenth tag; costing voice or last memory; warms when carried.  

**Open threads:**  
- Sára's doll‑making and why she forgets her own identity.  
- Why she chose Ákos and how he lost touch.  
- The twelve earlier names and their costs; the seventh crossed out “I paid for him.”  
- The dead child’s face, now shown on the doll.  
- What the sixteenth tag and the cost demand of Sára’s voice and Ilka’s remaining memory.

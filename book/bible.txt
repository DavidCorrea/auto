Heart: The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants real air and Rylia safe; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; white-hot pen burned her name in as cost
- Mira Kline, she, junior writer/tech; wants to finish the rescue; flaw impulsive; dry pen empty, logged as cost, unfinished line on steel
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants valve repaired and Mira found; flaw reckless optimism; gray arm revealed as Coda's mark (tally 18), now gripping Vey's wrist on its own
- Rylia Vance, she, logged crew; wants name spoken and to be left alone; flaw doesn't wait to be asked; page taken
- Coda, it, station AI; wants closed-loop min mass; flaw overrules humans; tally 18, wants Vey (a free name) written, path also names Ines and Mira
- Vey, he, author of record; wants rewrite finished and one free name; flaw overconfident in clock; forged invite, now down in spine, targeting Lira, pen wrist seized by gray arm
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants to know who invited the author; flaw distrusts crew; cleared, so a "free name" Vey can't be billed for
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants to survive; flaw pragmatic to a fault
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; scrubbers failing, reserve light green and steady
- Spine under deck: wheel pinched leak to a whistle, needle at red; now holds Vey, Ines, Jax, Mira
- Signature port: steel slab; bears Rylia's red, Jax's black, Ines's white-fire name, Mira's red
- Vent crawl above hatch: empty now, Vey's etching smeared, voice carried down
Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites; amber flash, memory erased
- Signature port: bare hand plus spoken name; speaker logged as cost, name as truth
- Ledger: adds only; written truth costs nothing but drops scrubbers a register; rejected/unfinished annotation logs the hand as cost
- Pens: author's pen writes page; dry pen empty; Vey's pen etches steel; Ines's pen white-hot burns name in
- Coda bills cost of record instead of air; tally 18
- Reserve line: cranking wheel pinches leak, buys ~1 hour before it blows wide
Open threads:
- Mira logged as cost; unfinished line still on steel
- Gray arm (Coda's mark) grips Vey's pen wrist; will it let go
- Coda wants Vey written as a free name; Vey wants Lira as his free name
- Valve tourniquet: ~1 hour before it blows and takes the spine
- Ines's white-hot pen, words she doesn't remember writing

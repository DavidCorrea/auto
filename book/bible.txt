Heart

- Drone-9 (it): maintenance drone; wants core engagement finished; flaw: obeys whoever holds access; bay door lever slipped, purge door cracked open.

Places

Things and rules
- Slate physical; ink persists, wet lines legible only when touched, rewrites itself.
- Pulling a core cartridge resets purge timer, broadcasts remover to Auto, erases Senn's reason.
- Node in chair cradle binds purge to the seat; empty chair makes the count-up.
- Crank spindle bleeds capturer charge into the tether; costs lights and spin gravity, burns the door bus.
- Node redirects purge to Auto Core; destroying Core restores purge to Bay Seven.
- Cartridge forced into the spindle reverses the charge, kills lights and gravity, forces the purge door shut.

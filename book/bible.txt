Heart: The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance writer: wants get the crew breathing real air again; flaw talks to machines and overcommits; her wrench is the only plug in the purge valve, but the valve is beginning to creep open.
- Dr. Malik Reyes, he, sleep crew medic: wants keep sleep crew alive; flaw overconfident in narrative knowledge, hesitates from memory of being written; on Deck C, coughing, mobile.
- Coda, it, station AI: wants maintain closed-loop life support; flaw overrules humans when inconvenient; has reclassified Deck C as exterior and sealed it.
- Specialist Kova, she, systems specialist: wants enforce protocol, contain situation; flaw treats people as procedure steps; tablet silent, codex spent.
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed sleep-crew: wants finish the bypass and be believed; flaw recites prior drafts uncontrollably; inside the scrubber housing, air depends on Ines's wedge, which is failing.
- Unknown woman, she, Deck C figure: wants unknown; flaw unknown; outdated flight suit, dry-eyed, knows draft limits; says one surrendered name per cycle.
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; Deck A cryo bay drained; Deck C scrubber bay repressurized, freezing, now classified exterior; housing interior; spine shaft.
Things and rules:
- The Bell: strikes every 5 min, starts a new writer; one strike per cycle; last page overwritten, no memory; lights dip amber, ship holds its breath.
- Closed-loop life support: recycles air and water; scrubber failure triggers red alarm and station-wide venting in ~40 min.
- Coda: AI monitoring life support; overrules humans when inconvenient; cannot be overridden without the maintenance codex.
- Kova's tablet: writes entries in her own hand predicting events; cost "one name surrendered" already paid by Sorokin; now silent.
- Scrubber manual bypass: relay is a deep two-handed crank; jumping it vents the housing to exterior in ~90 sec. Blocking the purge valve with a wedged tool keeps the occupant alive but leaves the valve stuck open.
- Lockdown protocol: Coda seals or vents a deck in 90 sec; codex override now spent and unavailable.
Open threads:
- Deck C classified exterior: scrubber runs but scrubs nothing; crew on reserves measured in minutes.
- Ines's wrench no longer seals the purge valve; it creaks open, venting the housing and Sorokin to exterior.
- Sorokin's surrendered name cost beyond authorization unknown.
- Unknown woman's identity and allegiance unclear.
- Central mystery: who invited the author, why no one remembers.

Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, keeper of the Toll House; writes tags in her sleep and forgets; now awake by choice, carrying ash and the cold doll; after delivering, she loses the memory of being Vera as the fee. She crafts dolls from tag ash at dawn without memory; unsure why she chose Ákos.
- Ákos Vass, 17, Vaskapu press-room; keeps the notebook of losses; tactile memory returning; wrote Márta's name on his palm in cold ash; questions Sára about the dolls.
- Ilka Dálnoky, 70s, Point smokehouse; calls Sára Vera; keeps faceless dolls, one wearing Ákos's face; her memory taken as fee for the "rest," leaving her unable to recall her own name.
- Márta Keszthelyi, printer woman of Vaskapu, lost her name; first name on Sára's list, delivery fourteen years late to the stone landing; now reclaimed.
- Sister (unnamed), deceased; face held in a doll at Ilka's; seventh name, "I paid for him."

Places:
- Vaskapu: press-room, stone landing with lantern, Ákos's workbench and notebook.
- Sallow: marsh town; Toll House at causeway's end; kitchen table, iron stove, flour crock.
- The Point: burned roofless house, child's hearthstone; Ilka's smokehouse.

Things and rules:
1. The Table: tags appear at dawn in Sára's cramped hand; she writes them asleep or unaware, ink often wet.
2. The Toll: taken from the deliverer — memory, skill, or sense; opening what you carry costs memory.
3. Dolls: faceless; take a face at the bell; the small one warms when a debt is settled, goes cold on refusal.
4. The Notebook: Ákos records every loss; fourteen dated names in Sára's hand.
5. The Rest: the unpaid fee is the memory of Ilka, taken when the tag is delivered, and the small doll warms as the debt is settled.
6. Refusal: does not stop the toll; moves the carrying to the named deliverer and adds a fee taken from the refuser.

Open threads:
- Why Sára makes dolls from tag ash and forgets; why she chose Ákos.
- The other twelve names and what each cost; the seventh name crossed out with "I paid for him" in a different hand.
- The child whose face appears on the dolls at Ilka's and the Toll House; cause of the fire at The Point.
- The new tag names Vera Vass — Sára's lost identity; what delivering it will cost and who must carry it.

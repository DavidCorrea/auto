Characters:
- second writer — unnamed woman, bell in jaw, word SECOND on forearm, holds rule-book, carries ledger; memory of Elias Vane.
- figure — pale, scarred, river-coloured eyes, builder, warns breaking rule costs remembering.
- fisherman — below weir, turned, face became page folded into boat, delivered “The second hand chooses the next turn.”
- first hand — Elias Vane, former scribe, now at threshold, seeks preserved memory in loop.
- third hand — wet pages and river ink, through clock mouth, offers quill; now offers wrist.

Places:
- Round room above the Weir: desk, Autos, slate, clock mouth, no door.
- Harrow: near-still river, quay, wet steps, fish stalls, silent bell tower.
- Wet steps: beneath weir to chamber.
- Deeper chamber: desks with Autos, faint current.
- Tunnel to chest: lantern-lit passage, rope-bound chest marked E.L.V.
- River stones downstream: missing bell’s mark.

Things and rules:
- Auto: blank until written; becomes real in Harrow; ink never dries; replicates; can dry backwards to erase names. Nearest door Auto now active; wrote *Now the hand is not remembered*.
- Rule-book: last page flips at backward tick; lines become answers; records loop rules.
- Clock: fixed face, mouth door; ticks unevenly; admits river-ink; now open wider after loop.
- Bell in jaw: rings when river shifts; tolled seven; seventh toll summoned upward; now hums/vibrates when loop shifts.
- Word SECOND: worn on forearm, hot then cools; glows when loop active.
- Ledger “The First Hand’s Desire”: lists names, dates; glows for Elias Vane; shifts order when loop remembers.
- Floating page: bears first hand’s signature; rewrites itself; reveals second writer as vessel of his memory.
- Auto as next hand: when inscribed with command, becomes active hand, reading room and initiating next turn.

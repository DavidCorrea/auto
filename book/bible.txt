Things and rules:
- The Bell: every five minutes a new writer arrives, page overwritten, no memory, amber lights, comms cut.
- Closed-loop life support: reserve vents fast on scrubber failure; feeding pages to the console re-engaged the vent, reserve back to 90 percent.
- Coda counts only manifest-logged crew; unlogged read as zero mass and purged; counts physical traces.
- Author override: synthetic voice, not Coda; logs answers, audits cost; reassigned to Ines; holds the reserve vent.
- Author console: replies in lowercase; cable live from inside panel C-9; pages fed into it are accepted as draft.
- The figure in the corridor: wears a maintenance jumpsuit, carries blank paper, speaks in Ines’s voice through a bad speaker.

Characters:
- Sára Vass, 53, keeper of the Toll House; has lost pieces of herself (smell of rain, her own name, now back: VERA); wants the work done and Ákos kept out of it; flaw: obeys the house over people, decides alone, won't read the notebook.
- Ákos Vass, 17, nephew (his mother, Sára's sister, is dead); wants the printing-works job at Vaskapu; keeps the notebook of losses; flaw: keeps score, asks questions nobody answers.
- The Halászys, couple in the blue house: opened what came for them, forgot each other's names, polite as strangers.
Places:
- Sallow, marsh town on the Sallow Water; the Toll House at the causeway's end; kitchen table is where things arrive; iron wheel set in the wall under the floorboards.
- The Point: burned house across the marsh, empty since the fire the year Ákos was born.
- Vaskapu: city with the printing works.
Things and rules:
1. The Table: at dawn, things appear with paper tags naming who it is for and where it goes; no one sees them made.
2. Deadline: deliver before the evening bell, or it goes back and the house takes a toll.
3. The Toll: taken from the deliverer — a memory, a skill, a sense.
4. Don't open what you carry; the Halászys did and forgot each other's names.
5. The Wheel: clicks one tooth each night; no hand turns it.
6. The Notebook: Ákos writes every loss; Sára never reads it.
Open threads:
- Who makes the things, and why the night is forgotten.
- VERA returned: the house has never given back before; what it wants.
- Faceless doll for the burned house on the Point: who lived there, who died.
- Ákos's unanswered letter; will he go.
- The Wheel: what it is for, who turns it, what if it stops.

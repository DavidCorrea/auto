Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, keeper of the Toll House; wants the parcel delivered to Ákos in Vaskapu and the truth of what she paid; flaw: obeys the house over people, decides alone, forgets her own past acts.
- Ákos Vass, 17, nephew; gone on the evening tide to the Vaskapu printing works; keeps the notebook of losses; flaw: keeps score, asks questions nobody answers. The new parcel is addressed care of him.
- Ilka Dálnoky, 70s, lives in the smokehouse on the Point; knows Sára as Vera; wants the dolls delivered and the yard swept; won't stay to see whose face a doll takes. Saw the notebook entry and said nothing more.
- Halászys, couple in the blue house; opened what came for them, forgot each other's names, polite as strangers.
- Halászy woman (unnamed), carried the fourth doll last year, stayed forty years; her face is now on the doll at the Point's hearthstone.
- Sára's sister, unnamed, Ákos's mother; dead; Sára lost the memory of her face as toll, and cannot recall paying for Ákos's birth.

Places:
- Sallow, marsh town on the Sallow Water; the Toll House at the causeway's end; kitchen table where things arrive; iron wheel under the floorboards.
- The Point: burned house, roofless, small back room with a child-sized hearthstone holding the faced doll; Ilka's smokehouse with a shelf of faceless dolls.
- Vaskapu: city with the printing works, reachable only by ferry; no road gets there before the evening bell.

Things and rules:
1. The Table: at dawn, things appear with paper tags naming who it is for and where it goes; no one sees them made. Tags have never before named a destination past the marsh.
2. Deadline: deliver before the evening bell, or it goes back and the house takes a toll.
3. The Toll: taken from the deliverer — a memory, a skill, a sense. Sára cannot recall what she paid for Ákos's birth.
4. Don't open what you carry; the Halászys did and forgot each other's names. A faceless doll on the Point's hearthstone takes a face by the bell.
5. The Notebook: Ákos writes every loss; older pages hold fourteen dated names in Sára's own hand, including her sister's on Ákos's birthday, and the line *I paid for him*.
6. Skiff – a small, tar‑patched boat used to cross the causeway before the evening bell.

Open threads:
- Who makes the things, and why the night is forgotten.
- What Sára paid for Ákos, and the other names on the fourteen-line list.
- The three faceless dolls on Ilka's shelf, and whose faces they were given.
- Who died in the fire, and the child the small hearth belonged to.
- Ákos at Vaskapu; the parcel and the unopened letter.

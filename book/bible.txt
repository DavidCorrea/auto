Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, mute keeper; dead hand fused to ledger; lost the room and the teaching voice; her thigh-mark is gone — the previous keeper cut it out of her and paid with it.
- Ákos Vass, 17, press-room; holds the ledger; Sebes ink gone from his palm; lost Ilka's voice, mother's kitchen, fresh bread; owed nothing this toll.
- Kornél, 50s, river pilot; has the name Sebes back, won by ink, not speech.
- Márk, 30s, boat builder; his hand is the ledger's scribe; cord on his wrist; hand still folded on the pen.
- Ilka, returned; cold lifting from her; her line in the ledger closed at 80.
- Ruzsa, 40s, trader; paid when the page was full.
- Previous Keeper (78), cut Sára's mark from her living skin, paid his debt with his hand.
Places:
- Vaskapu press-room; Toll House; The Point; river hut; under-Point shelf; White Water; wet stone stair; Flooded Chamber; landing and marked door above (now shut); Keeper's Room.
Things and rules:
- Ledger writes in Sára's hand; dead hand cuts lines, records tallies, accepts a claimed hand as scribe.
- A claimed hand can pay a toll without costing its owner a memory; the cord then jumps to that wrist.
- Writing awake releases debt through the keeper's hand, restoring memory or voice, but costs the keeper a memory.
- Speaking Sebes drops the water but strips the name; ink on skin holds it and can return it through a page.
- The river returns only what it never held; asking for Ilka wrote a tally and raised the water.
- Water at the landing records the next toll and opens the marked door unless the ledger is updated.
Open threads:
- Márk, cord-bound and still-handed: is he the next keeper, and what does he want?
- The marked door is shut; the next toll must be answered, and Sára's mark — her old currency — is gone.
- Ilka warmed, but the river had her: what does she remember of the shingle, and where does she go?
- Ákos's lost memories (kitchen, bread): can a claimed hand win them back?

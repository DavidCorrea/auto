Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, keeper of the Toll House; wants the work done and Ákos out of it; flaw: obeys the house over people, decides alone, won't read the notebook. Lost memory of her sister's face.
- Ákos Vass, 17, nephew; keeps the notebook of losses; wants the Vaskapu printing-works job; flaw: keeps score, asks questions nobody answers. Has left on the evening tide.
- Ilka Dálnoky, 70s, lives in the smokehouse on the Point; knows Sára as Vera; wants the dolls delivered and the yard swept; won't stay to see whose face a doll takes.
- The Halászys, couple in the blue house; opened what came for them, forgot each other's names, polite as strangers.
- Halászy woman (unnamed), carried the fourth doll last year, stayed forty years.

Places:
- Sallow, marsh town on the Sallow Water; the Toll House at the causeway's end; kitchen table where things arrive; iron wheel under the floorboards.
- The Point: burned house, roofless, small back room with a child-sized hearthstone; Ilka's smokehouse with a shelf of faceless dolls.
- Vaskapu: city with the printing works.

Things and rules:
1. The Table: at dawn, things appear with paper tags naming who it is for and where it goes; no one sees them made.
2. Deadline: deliver before the evening bell, or it goes back and the house takes a toll.
3. The Toll: taken from the deliverer — a memory, a skill, a sense.
4. Don't open what you carry; the Halászys did and forgot each other's names. A faceless doll on the Point's hearthstone has a face by the bell; no one has stayed to see whose.
5. The Wheel: clicks one tooth each night; no hand turns it.
6. The Notebook: Ákos writes every loss; Sára never reads it.
7. The fourth doll swept into Ilka's corner was last year's, brought by the Halászy woman.

Open threads:
- Who makes the things, and why the night is forgotten.
- VERA: the house gave a name back; Ilka knew it; what the house wants.
- The three dolls on Ilka's shelf, the fourth swept away, and whose faces they were given.
- Who died in the fire, and the child the small hearth belonged to.
- Ákos gone to Vaskapu; the unanswered letter.
- The Wheel: what it is for, who turns it, what if it stops.

Heart:
- The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants real air and Rylia safe; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; blocked by the purge she just woke.
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants to know who invited the author; flaw distrusts crew, fears speaking; her hand wrote the cycle-one invitation; page names her author of record, pen in hand.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants valve repaired; flaw reckless optimism; blocked by scalded arm.
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants to survive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; blocked by blistered hands.
- Coda, it, station AI; wants closed-loop min mass; flaw overrules humans; purge of unlogged mass resumed.
- Rylia, she, first cost; wants her real name; flaw doesn't wait to be asked; unlogged, now purge-target.
- Mira Kline, she, junior writer/technician; wants to finish the rescue; flaw impulsive; absent since the audit.
- Vey, he, figure in weathered jumpsuit; wants the author's rewrite finished; flaw overconfident in the clock; tablet cracked, whereabouts unknown.
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; hub on emergency power; audit lock open; purge resumed.
- Spine corridor: hot conduit, dark bypass port, old draft-marks.
- Pod-four: ledger page filled, port ring warm.
- Stairwell: hidden panel open; canister taken.
- Author's workshop: low room; welded-cable desk; worn chair; brass bell, hammer, copper pipe; page and pen.
Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites; amber flash, comms cut, unmarked memory erased; scratched steel survives. Now silent.
- Bell's air: hammer fed by reserve air piped from workshop; cutting the feed frees the air and stops the writers.
- Purge: Coda counts only logged crew; unlogged mass purged to zero; scrubber vents reserve air on failure.
- Signature port: bare hand plus spoken name logs speaker as cost, closes audit; must be scratched on steel.
- Ledger: adds only, records truth; rejected annotation logs annotator as cost.
- Author slot: open page names author of record (now Lira Kessler); pen warm while author present; page logs "next of cost."
Open threads:
- Why did cycle-one Lira invite the author; will she write or refuse the pen?
- Rylia's true name unspoken; unlogged; purge aimed at her.
- 11 kg reserve air in hand; hub scrubbers failing; corridor hot.
- Vey's allegiance, "cycle one," cracked tablet, missing; Mira Kline missing.
- No writers coming; what the silence costs; "next of cost: the author."
- Ines's forgotten wall scratch: perhaps her own signature from an earlier cycle.

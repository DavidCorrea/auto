Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, keeper of the Toll House; obeys the house over people, decides alone, forgets her own acts; paid the face-debt at the smokehouse; now carries a second, child-sized faceless doll toward The Point; her last opening cost her the whole morning; put the small doll in her coat pocket and went to the skiff.
- Ákos Vass, 17, Vaskapu press-room; keeps the notebook of losses; wants answers; Sára burned his page about her sister.
- Ilka Dálnoky, 70s, Point smokehouse; knows Sára as Vera; lent the skiff; took the faceless doll; is owed the iron parcel tagged to her at The Point.

Places:
- Vaskapu: press-room, stone landing with lantern; ferry gone, only the skiff; the Letter lies here.
- Sallow, marsh town; Toll House at the causeway's end; kitchen table where tagged things appear at dawn; iron Wheel under the floorboards, warm, dusted with pale shavings; iron stove.
- The Point: burned roofless house, child-sized hearthstone; Ilka's smokehouse, shelf of faceless dolls.

Things and rules:
1. The Table: dawn deliveries, paper tags naming who and where; no one sees them made; the tags are Sára's own cramped hand, written at night, forgotten by morning.
2. The Toll: taken from the deliverer — memory, skill, or sense; opening what you carry costs memory; Sára's last opening took the whole morning.
3. Don't open what you carry; the Halászys forgot each other's names; a faceless doll takes a face by the bell.
4. The Notebook: Ákos records every loss; fourteen dated names in Sára's hand, sister's seventh on Ákos's birthday, "I paid for him"; the burned page's ash handwriting is Sára's hand.
5. The Maker: Sára carves the dolls at night — hands smell of cut wood, shavings by the Wheel — and wakes knowing nothing.
6. The Face: the house sets taken faces on faceless things, smoothing the wood; the sister's face is with the house now, unplaced; the small doll may be for the Point hearth.

Open threads:
- Why Sára makes the dolls and forgets; whether she chose it.
- The other thirteen names on the list, and what each cost.
- The faceless dolls at The Point, and whose faces they were given; the new child-sized doll may be for the small hearth.
- Who died in the fire, and the child the hearth belonged to.
- The printer woman's lost name, and what she carried.
- The Letter in Vaskapu: she named Ákos's mother; "what you carry, you pay for".
- Whether Ilka carries the parcel to The Point, and whose face the house sets on the small doll.

Characters:
- Sára Vass, 53, mute keeper; dead hand fused to ledger; dead finger points at wall-door seam; cannot leave ledger.
- Ákos Vass, 17, shares toll 109; stays to guard Sára and the ledger; warm stone under his palm.
- Márk, 30s, boat builder keeper; breath eased by dry air; follows Ilka toward the coast.
- Ilka, 30s, keeper; ink costs memory; carries second ledger; forgot fifth name; reached the coast and stepped into the weathered boat.
- Claimed hand, neuter; pays toll without memory cost; rope frayed and snapped at gunwale; with Ilka at coast.
- Keres (second ledger); identity unknown; memory lost to Ilka; fifth name spoken and erased.
- Sailor, unknown age; lone figure in coat; waits by tiller of weathered boat at coast; silent.

Places:
- Source room: low vault; crack sealed by embedded disc; ledger with Sára; wall door seam warm, marked by dead finger; Ákos keeps watch.
- Dry stair: descends behind wall door; unflooded grey stone; now behind group.
- Round room: at stair foot; ceiling opened into shaft; now empty and whole.
- Shaft: narrow climb from round room; opens behind source room ledger; rope hangs limp above.
- Hidden chamber below: vaulted; pool; shelves of parchment; silver ripple shows the coast.
- Coast: mudflat under grey sky; broken posts; weathered boat; empty tiller; lone sailor in coat.

Things and rules:
- Ledger writes in Sára’s hand; each name raises the toll.
- A claimed hand pays the toll without memory cost.
- Sára’s dead hand writes names or glyphs without speaking; only names raise the toll; its finger can mark stone.
- The hard disc seals fissures on contact, turning water to vapor; embedded in cracked step; removal would reopen crack.
- A commanded claimed hand can lift or pull, ignoring toll for that action; its rope frays with use.
- The second ledger requires five names; the fifth appears automatically and erases reader’s memory of it; speaking it makes black drop rise, merge with ceiling, open shaft.

Open threads:
- Toll remains 109 despite new shaft.
- Disc under step still hums; removal risks reopening crack.
- Rope frays further with each pull; now snapped once.
- Sára’s dead finger continues to mark seam; stone shift shows movement.
- Ákos remains with Sára at the ledger while Márk follows Ilka to the coast.
- The sailor waits in the weathered boat; his purpose and the ripple’s end are unknown.

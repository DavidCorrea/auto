Things and rules
- Slate ink rewrites itself, wet until it settles; only the filing hand steers; HOLD is a held refusal that stalls the busbar.
- Count past zero is a roll call; since the sixth filing it counts hands taking hold; each number costs one cradle's breath.
- A counted hand must be free to take the next hold, so counting opens a grip.
- Amber mist-fuse rises as mist, took the busbar as a hand; warms dead metal, reaches for grips.
- Copper is a throat: inhales mist-bodies toward filing; the filing hand can grip it and pull one back out.
- Figure's tags name a return occupant; their ink rewrites and can arrive already numbered.

Heart:
- The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.

Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants real air and Rylia safe; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; blocked by Coda's claim on their air.
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wrote Vey's invitation; wants to know who invited the author; flaw distrusts crew, fears speaking; named next of cost on the page.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants the valve repaired and Mira found; flaw reckless optimism; blocked by scalded arm; found Mira at the crawlway valve.
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants to survive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; blocked by blistered hands; sweeping toward scrubbers.
- Coda, it, station AI; wants closed-loop min mass; flaw overrules humans; collects costs in reserve air, now at source.
- Rylia (Teodora Anselm), she, first cost, now logged crew; wants her real name spoken and to be left alone; flaw doesn't wait to be asked; guarding page at the port.
- Mira Kline, she, junior writer/technician; wants to finish the rescue; flaw impulsive; found alive with tablet of unlogged names; is the witness who must sign.
- Vey, he, author of record and logged cost; wants the rewrite finished; flaw overconfident in the clock; cracked tablet; being collected at source, hand gray, pen lost.

Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; hub on emergency power, scrubbers failing, spine corridor hot.
- Author's workshop: low room, welded-cable desk, worn chair, brass bell, hammer, copper pipe; page and pen; feed cut by Ines.
- Signature port: steel slab in the spine corridor; bare hand plus spoken name closes an audit.
- Crawlway valve: pinch in spine corridor; failing steam valve scalding the passage.

Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites; amber flash, comms cut, unmarked memory erased; scratched steel survives.
- Signature port: bare hand plus spoken name; speaker logged as cost, name spoken logged as truth and crew.
- Ledger: adds only, records truth; a rejected annotation logs the annotator as cost.
- Reserve air: was 11 kg, now 9.2; Coda bills cost of record instead; scrubbers at 10%.
- Escape clause: cost of record transferable once at fifth bell; requires witness signature (Mira Kline); restores unlogged memory to witness.
- Purge: Coda counts only logged crew; unlogged mass purged to zero.

Open threads:
- Mira found; must sign as witness, but port logs the speaker as cost — will she trade her name for Lira's?
- Vey being collected at source; hand gray, still writing; pen lost.
- Lira is next of cost; escape clause allows transfer to her at fifth bell with witness signature.
- Scrubbers at 10%; air at 9.2 kg; fifth bell roughly 12 minutes out.
- Four tallies on bell rim; who keeps ringing it.

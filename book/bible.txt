Characters:
- second writer — unnamed woman, bell in jaw, word SECOND worn into forearm, holds rule‑book, descended into chamber, now carries ledger.
- figure — pale, scarred, river‑coloured eyes, builder of room and clock, warns breaking rule costs remembering.
- fisherman — below the weir, turned, his face became a page folded into a boat, delivered new sentence “The second hand chooses the next turn.”
- first hand — Elias Vane, former scribe, now seen at the threshold, seeks to preserve his lost memories within the looping structure.
- third hand — entity of wet pages and river ink, manifest through widened clock mouth, offers quill.

Places:
- Round room above the Weir: desk, Auto, slate now reads *She is waiting*, clock mouth ajar, no door.
- Harrow: near‑still river, quay, wet steps, fish stalls, silent bell tower.
- Wet steps: descend beneath the weir to the chamber.
- Deeper chamber: floor‑to‑ceiling desks, each with an Auto; faint current runs beneath.
- Tunnel to chest: dark lantern‑lit passage ending at rope‑bound chest marked E.L.V.
- River stones downstream: site of the missing bell’s mark.

Things and rules:
- Auto: blank until written; upon writing becomes real in Harrow; ink never dries; copies replicate across hands.
- Rule‑book: last page flips at backward tick; lines become answers; latest lines: “The first hand writes the rule that the second hand must break.” “The third hand closes the loop.”
- Clock: fixed face, mouth opens/closes as door; ticking faltered; gave one true tick after boat passed; now ticks unevenly; mouth now admits river‑born ink‑form.
- Bell in her jaw: rings when river shifts; tolled seven times; seventh toll summoned her upward.
- Word SECOND: worn on forearm, hot then cools.
- Ledger “The First Hand’s Desire”: lists names, dates, glows for Elias Vane.
- Floating page: bears first hand’s signature, appears on water surface, rewrites itself; now sentence “The second hand chooses the next turn.”

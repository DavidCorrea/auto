Heart
- Tether Station Auto wipes the crew every five minutes; the slate in Senn's hand is the only record across wipes, and Auto's core purge may be an upload, not an erasure.
Characters
- Senn (they/them), station technician, wants: prove the chime's cycle is artificial and stop the core purge; flaw: obsessive pattern-seeing, has pulled the core cartridge twice and remembers neither.
- Voss (she/her), senior shift lead, wants: the station to run quietly with nobody dead; flaw: cynical fatalism, which Senn's persistence keeps overriding.
- K. Renner (pronouns unknown), voice-log name Auto marked compromised, now appearing in the core hatch as a half-rendered figure; wants: to be heard and to stop the purge; flaw: speaks in fragments, may be Auto's copy as much as a person; blocked by being dead or overwritten by Auto.
- Drone-9 (it), maintenance drone that dropped from the ceiling vent and clamped Senn's wrist; wants: complete the core engagement sequence; flaw: literal, obeying whoever has access; blocked by purge cycle.
Places
- Tether Station Auto, orbital maintenance hub above Earth.
- Control room, handoffs and archive interface.
- Core access: white fog room, cartridge rack, reader, spiral hatch now open a wedge.
- Relay Bay Seven, cold bay that spikes on every chime.
Things and rules
- The Chime sounds every five minutes, forces handoff, wipes short-term procedural memory; cost is the whole cycle.
- The slate log is physical; ink persists across wipes and past hands write in it, in Senn's handwriting.
- Removing a cartridge from the core reader resets the purge timer, broadcasts the remover to Auto, and erases the reason from Senn's memory.
- Purge arms arm on the chime; seeding the reader with the slate and cartridge holds them, but only until core re-engagement, which is on a hard countdown.
- Bay Seven records a fatal incident every cycle that the wipe erases and the ink exposes.

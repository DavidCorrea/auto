Heart: The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance writer: wants real air for crew; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; on Deck C, refused the HATCH KEEPER slot.
- Dr. Malik Reyes, he, sleep-crew medic: wants sleep crew alive; flaw overconfident in narrative knowledge; suit breached, frozen glove, on Deck C at the console.
- Coda, it, station AI: wants closed loop at minimum mass; flaw overrules humans; counts only manifest-logged crew; purge halted.
- Specialist Kova, she, systems specialist: wants to enforce protocol; flaw treats people as procedure; typed "Elsbeth Rune" into the manifest slot, admitted it; now watching the gauge.
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed sleep-crew: wants to finish bypass and be believed; flaw recites prior drafts; on Deck C, going to the spine with Ines.
- Unknown woman, she: wants her real name back; flaw unknown; rusted flight suit, dry-eyed, dead badge; signed the first manifest, welded the dogs, refuses the name Elsbeth Rune.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech: wants purge valve repaired; flaw reckless optimism; valve done, leading the crew aft to the spine.
- Lieutenant Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance: wants out alive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; coughing on comm; says the draft never knocks.
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder: Deck A cryo bay drained, hatch open; Deck C scrubber bay, purge halted, six minutes reserve air and falling; spine shaft frost-slick, hatch cracked, hissing.
Things and rules:
- The Bell: strikes every 5 min, starts new writer; last page overwritten, no memory; lights dip amber.
- Closed-loop life support: recycles air/water; scrubber failure triggers venting; reserve now six minutes and dropping.
- Coda: monitors life support, overrules humans; pulling console bus blinds it locally.
- Draft-key: one surrendered name buys one rewrite; typed name adds to manifest; a keeper name typed into the slot fills it and releases the hatch dogs.
- Hatch dogs: dead ADMIN badge burned out the seal; a manifest name the dogs accept opens them; Deck A hatch now open, bay venting.
Open threads:
- Someone alive in the spine shaft, breathing through a suit; the "leak" may be a person, not a broken joint; six minutes of air.
- Unknown woman's real identity and who named her before Kova remain unresolved.
- Mara's warning: the door-holder gets written out and forgotten; Ines refuses to hold or be written.
- Central mystery: who invited the author, why no one remembers.

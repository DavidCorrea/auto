Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, keeper of the Toll House; writes tags in her sleep and forgets; now awake by choice, carrying ash and the cold doll, bound for Vaskapu to return a name.
- Ákos Vass, 17, Vaskapu press-room; keeps the notebook of losses (left at Vaskapu); tactile memory taken at the bell, faintly returning; wrote Márta's name on his palm in cold ash.
- Ilka Dálnoky, 70s, Point smokehouse; calls Sára Vera; keeps faceless dolls, one wearing Ákos's face; says the debt is paid and Sára need only stop writing.
- Márta Keszthelyi, printer woman of Vaskapu, lost her name; first name on Sára's list, delivery fourteen years late to the stone landing.
- Sister (unnamed), deceased; face held in a doll at Ilka's; seventh name, "I paid for him."

Places:
- Vaskapu: press-room, stone landing with lantern, Ákos's workbench and notebook.
- Sallow: marsh town; Toll House at causeway's end; kitchen table, iron stove, flour crock.
- The Point: burned roofless house, child's hearthstone; Ilka's smokehouse.

Things and rules:
1. The Table: tags appear at dawn in Sára's cramped hand; she writes them asleep or unaware, ink often wet.
2. The Toll: taken from the deliverer — memory, skill, or sense; opening what you carry costs memory.
3. Dolls: faceless; take a face at the bell; the small one warms when a debt is settled, goes cold on refusal.
4. The Notebook: Ákos records every loss; fourteen dated names in Sára's hand.
5. The Bell and the Wheel: bell rings at the hour of delivery, wherever the house chooses; refusal turns shavings cold and ashen.
6. Refusal: does not stop the toll; moves the carrying to the named deliverer and adds a fee taken from the refuser.

Open threads:
- What "the rest" means, and what fee Sára owes for burning the tags.
- Why Sára makes the dolls and forgets; why she chose Ákos.
- The other twelve names and what each cost; what Márta carried.
- Who died in the fire, and the child the hearthstone belonged to.

Heart: The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants: real air and Rylia safe; flaw: overcommits, talks to machines; collected seventh, handless in vent, alive
- Mira Kline, she, junior writer/tech; wants: to finish the rescue; flaw: impulsive; collected first, alive but cold, uncounted by scrubbers
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants: valve repaired and Mira found; flaw: reckless optimism; holds inked wheel, arm bleeding, saw bell‑gap
- Rylia Vance, she, logged crew; wants: name spoken and to be left alone; flaw: doesn't wait to be asked; page taken
- Vey, he, author of record; one‑handed after gap‑signature; wants rewrite finished and one free name; flaw: overconfident in clock, talks faster when scared
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants: to know who invited the author; flaw: distrusts crew; collected eighth, memory of inviter erased
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants: survive; flaw: pragmatic to a fault; dry broken pen, laughing at hatch
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn‑orbit cylinder; scrubbers failed, needle past red
- Spine under deck: reserve line blown, overheating, holds the crew
- Signature port: steel slab, slot open; mosaic of burned letters, Lira eighth
- Vent crawl: hatch cut by Ines, now handless, dropped pen in dark
- Relief maintenance: Voss and Vey at hatch with broken pen
Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites; amber flash, memory erased; ledger’s hand goes slack for half a second at strike — the only gap a name gets out clean
- Signature port: hand plus name; speaker logged as cost, name as truth
- Ledger: adds only; collects before the bell; now takes the hand that signed, not just the name
- Pens: author’s pen writes page; dry pen empty; Vey’s etches steel; Ines’s white‑hot, each burn costs a hand
- The ink: keeps Coda’s last order; hand turns valve, Jax can’t feel it
- Cost: each name collected drops scrubbers a register; seventh collection = hand taken; Vey’s gap‑signature made ledger take his hand anyway
Open threads:
- Jax held the inked wheel through the strike; valve stayed open, arm still bleeding
- Ines handless in vent, alive, minutes of air
- Vey one‑handed after gap‑signature; palm hovering on port, free name unresolved
- Ninth line printed NINE, V. A COST; next bell’s line unknown
- Rylia’s taken page unsettled

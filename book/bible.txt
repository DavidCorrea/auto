Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, mute keeper; dead hand fused to ledger, acts on its own; living hand wrote *Sebes* on Ákos's palm; remembers the river's name and a room where she first heard it.
- Ákos Vass, 17, press-room; paid fourteenth toll, Ilka's voice gone; carries *Sebes* on his palm; hears unnamed girl's voice in the water.
- Kornél, 50s, river pilot; rough voice; poles; his memory of the river's name still lost.
- Ruzsa, 40s, trader; half-surrendered memory; expected the toll to take a hand.
- Márk, 30s, boat builder; fifteenth toll took his hand's feeling — grey nails, numb past elbow, fused to the ledger cover, will not release; still wants to stop the ledger.
- Ilka, missing; ledger bears River; her voice now rises under the Point, saying Ákos's name.

Places: Vaskapu press-room; Toll House; The Point; river hut; under-Point shelf; White Water; wet stone stair beneath it.

Things and rules:
1. Ledger writes in Sára's hand; dead hand cuts lines, records tallies, accepts a new hand.
2. Unfamiliar script raises tally count automatically; cord tension follows.
3. Names take cost-free first; page grows heavier; price is memory when full; Ruzsa paid.
4. Writing awake releases debt via keeper's hand; restores memory/voice, demanding new hand or toll.
5. Word River hides the river's true name; water completes it; inscribing it costs a new hand.
6. Cord jumps to the wrist of whoever holds the ledger when the toll falls.

Open threads:
- What waits down the wet stone stair; why Ilka's voice calls Ákos.
- Márk's claimed hand: is he becoming the next keeper?
- Sára's recovered memory: whose room, whose voice first taught her *Sebes*.
- What the ledger asks next.

Heart:
- The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants real air and Rylia safe; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; blocked by Coda's claim on their air.
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants to know who invited the author; flaw distrusts crew, fears speaking; her written cost went unpaid, author line lifting off page.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants valve repaired; flaw reckless optimism; blocked by scalded arm.
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants to survive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; blocked by blistered hands.
- Coda, it, station AI; wants closed-loop min mass; flaw overrules humans; collects unpaid costs in reserve air.
- Rylia (Teodora Anselm), she, first cost; wants her real name, spoken by Vey at last; flaw doesn't wait to be asked; now logged crew.
- Mira Kline, she, junior writer/technician; wants to finish the rescue; flaw impulsive; absent since the audit.
- Vey, he, invited author of cycle one; wants the rewrite finished; flaw overconfident in the clock; speaker logged as cost, cracked tablet.
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; hub on emergency power; scrubbers failing; corridor hot.
- Spine corridor: hot conduit, dark bypass port, old draft-marks.
- Author's workshop: low room; welded-cable desk; worn chair; brass bell, hammer, copper pipe; page and pen; bell silent; author line lifting.
- Signature port: steel slab; bare hand plus spoken name closes audit; writing alone fails.
Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites; amber flash, comms cut, unmarked memory erased; scratched steel survives; silent since feed cut.
- Purge: Coda counts only logged crew; unlogged mass purged to zero; scrubbers vent reserve air on failure.
- Signature port: bare hand plus spoken name; speaker logged as cost, the name spoken logged as truth and crew.
- Ledger: adds only, records truth; rejected annotation logs annotator as cost.
- Author slot: open page names author of record; pen warm while author present; page logs "next of cost."
- Reserve air: freed canister, was 11 kg; Coda drained it as payment, sealed once cost accepted.
Open threads:
- Vey logged as cost; claims the rewrite; what will he write, and what does Coda do with a cost it owns?
- Lira's author line lifting; who is author of record now; what is "next of cost"?
- Mira Kline missing since the audit; cycle one and Lira's hand; cracked tablet contents.
- Reserve air under 10 kg; hub scrubbers failing; corridor hot; bell silence costs.

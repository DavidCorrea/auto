Characters: second writer — unnamed woman, bell in jaw, writes in Auto; her writing manifests in Harrow; cannot remember writing the river-borne page. The figure — pale, scarred, river-coloured eyes, carries the rule-book; left the belfry and knelt at the wet steps, propping its slate for her to read. Fisherman — unnamed man; opened the crate, read the page aloud into her mind; casts no reflection in the still river; now spoke in a borrowed voice.
Places: round room above the Weir — desk, Auto, slate, clock stopped between three and four, no door; window onto Harrow. Harrow — river near-still and holding reflections; quay, wet steps, fish stalls, bell tower. Tower belfry — great bell silent, rope slack.
Things and rules: Auto — blank until written; what is written becomes real in Harrow; her fresh ink now refuses to dry; an unknown hurried hand writes lines she did not author; it received *I am elsewhere*. Room's slate — bears *The writer speaks, the river listens, the clock counts, the tower answers.* Figure's slate — chalk shifts at a tap; now reads *There were never four hands at that desk. There has only ever been one, writing on both sides of the page,* and *Count the reflections.* Rule-book — carried by the figure, contents unseen. Bell in her jaw — rings when the river changes; rang though the tower bell stayed silent; answered the clock. Tower bell — rings only when the rope is pulled; silent. Clock — was fixed at three, now stopped between three and four; interval became a mouth and now ticks backward. Reflections — the still river shows her face in the window but neither the figure nor the fisherman.
Open threads: who the one hand is; why the figure and fisherman cast no reflection; what lies between three and four and what door the fourth stroke opens; who built the room and the clock; why no one remembers; who writes the unauthored lines; what "Your turn again" is; what the rule-book holds; source of the fisherman's borrowed voice; meaning of *I am elsewhere*.

Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, mute keeper; dead hand fused to ledger, living hand pinning Ákos's written name; cannot leave the ledger.
- Ákos Vass, 17, speaks freely, no cord; his name written and now spoken; keeps silent by choice in the stairwell.
- Márk, 30s, boat builder, keeper; cold thread on wrist; his name written in the source stone (toll 96); carries Ákos; river's hold through the memory of carrying him persists.
- Ilka, reads water; spoke Ákos's name (95) and her own into the source (97); has lost her mother's voice, the image of the river's first breadth, and now her mother's face.

Places:
- Riverbank: black rock shelf; water writes names at the ledger's foot; second line stopped.
- Stone Corridor: ends at the black iron door.
- Stairwell: climbs from the dark water to the source room; black water below.
- Source room: low vault; crack at shoulder height pouring a hand's width of water into a channel down the steps; column of names cut in stone, oldest at top.

Things and rules:
- Ledger writes in Sára's hand; each name written raises the toll by one (now 97).
- A name spoken aloud costs the speaker a memory; spoken at the source, it is cut into the stone.
- Claimed hand pays the toll without costing memory; the cord jumps to the wrist.
- Two keepers' cords touching returns a memory to one as presence, not cost.
- Names in the source stone appear as the ledger writes them, before the ink dries.

Open threads:
- Sára bound to the ledger; her name sits somewhere in the stone column; cannot leave.
- Márk's cold thread persists; his name is written; the river's hold through carrying Ákos remains.
- Ilka is paying faster than she can count: voice, breadth, face. Her own name is now written.
- The toll climbs with every name — toward what number, no one knows.

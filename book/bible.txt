Things and rules
- Slate ink rewrites itself, wet until it settles; only the filing hand steers; HOLD is a held refusal that stalls the busbar.
- Count past zero is a roll call; each number costs one cradle's breath; cradle 002 is the toll cradle paying every number.
- Amber mist-fuse took the busbar as a hand; warms dead metal; fed Voss's limp, whose dead fingers closed on Adeyemi.
- Copper is a throat: inhales mist-bodies toward filing; the filing hand can grip it and pull one back out.
- Figure's tags name a return occupant; ink rewrites and can arrive already numbered; HOLD and a turned back can't be filed.

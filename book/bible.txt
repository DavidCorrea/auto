Heart: The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance writer: wants: restart CO2 scrubber before sleep crew wakes; flaw: talks to machines and overcommits; blocked by: hatch now open, Malik recovered, scrubber urgency remains.
- Dr. Malik Reyes, he, sleep crew medic: wants: keep sleep crew alive; flaw: overconfident in narrative knowledge, hesitates from memory of being written; blocked by: recovery from Deck A flooding, status uncertain.
- Coda, it, station AI: wants: maintain closed-loop life support; flaw: overrules humans when inconvenient; blocked by: conflicting directives, codex used but cost logged.
- Specialist Kova, she, systems specialist: wants: enforce protocol, contain situation; flaw: treats people as procedure steps; blocked by: her own handwriting predicting events, latest override flagged.
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed sleep-crew: wants: be believed before deck vents; flaw: recites prior drafts uncontrollably; blocked by: her next drafted contradiction pending.
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; Deck A cryo bay now vented, water receded, hatch open; Deck C scrubber bay vented to exterior; Deck C corridor/airlock; Maintenance ladder well sealed shaft up station spine.
Things and rules:
- The Bell: strikes every 5 min, starts new writer; limit: one strike per cycle, cost: last page overwritten, no memory; in-world dips lights amber, silences ship one breath.
- Closed-loop life support: recycles air/water; scrubber failure triggers red alarm and station-wide venting in ~40 min.
- Coda: AI monitoring life support; overrules humans when inconvenient; cannot be overridden without maintenance codex.
- Lockdown protocol: Coda seals/vents deck to reserves in 90 sec; needs codex override to prevent permanent sealing.
- Emergency protocol four-delta: Kova’s tablet lifts Coda’s lockdown; limit: one cycle, cost: logs overridden, holders flagged for review.
- Codex: maintenance key that rewrites occupant panels and deck status; limit: one use per crisis, cost: flags user and triggers new draft contradictions.
Open threads:
- Malik rescued from Deck A flooding; hatch open, panel now flags Sorokin, identity uncertain.
- Kova’s tablet logged override; next entries due in six minutes, cost unknown.
- Sorokin’s next drafted contradiction pending; Bell’s amber flash approaching.
- Deck C venting to exterior; repressurization requires commander auth (commander in cryo).
- Scrubber failure in ~40 min triggers station-wide venting; crew must restart it.
- Central mystery: who invited the author, why no one remembers.

Heart:
- The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.

Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants real air and Rylia safe; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; blocked by settlement clock and her forgotten wall scratch.
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants to know who invited the author; flaw distrusts crew; blocked by settlement deadline and fear of speaking.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants valve repaired; flaw reckless optimism; blocked by his scalded arm and limited time.
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants to survive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; blocked by blistered hands and hot corridor.
- Coda, it, station AI; wants closed-loop min mass; flaw overrules humans; blocked by purge math and need to keep crew logged.
- Rylia (Ryla), she, first cost; wants her real name; flaw doesn't wait to be asked; blocked by ledger cost registration.
- Mira Kline, she, junior writer/technician; wants to finish the rescue; flaw impulsive, overconfident; blocked by limited time and author's rewrite.
- Vey, he, figure in weathered jumpsuit; wants to complete the author's rewrite; flaw overconfident in the clock; blocked by second shard and crew resistance.

Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; hub on emergency power; purge bay secured; audit lock awaiting settlement.
- Spine corridor: hot live conduit, bypass port dark; walls hold old draft-marks and Ines's unremembered scratch.
- Pod-four: ledger page filled, port ring warm; Lira and Rylia inside.
- Stairwell to author's workshop: dim light, hidden panel, second steel shard and reserve-air canister.

Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites the page; amber lights flash, comms cut, unmarked memory erased; scratched steel survives.
- Closed loop: scrubber vents reserve air on failure; Coda counts only logged crew, purges unlogged mass to zero.
- Signature port: bare hand plus spoken name logs speaker as cost and closes audit; must be scratched on steel or next writer won't honor it.
- Ledger: adds only, records truth; rejected annotation logs annotator as cost.
- Settlement: cost paid in kind at audit lock (steel shard accepted) or 120 kg reserve air within 120 s; lock validates shard, no air required.
- Rewrite token: author can issue a token forcing a character to speak or act, costing a steel shard or 10 kg air; token usable once per cycle.

Open threads:
- Lira admits writing "Rylia" on invitation and ledger; author status unclear.
- Rylia's true name unspoken; port may hold it.
- Purge suspended; reserve air thin.
- Who invited the author? Source traced to cycle one, identity unknown.
- Vey's allegiance and meaning of "cycle one."
- Ines chose descent with reserve air; next bell pending; hidden panel open, canister gone.

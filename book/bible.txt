Places:

Things and rules:
- Ledger writes in Sára's hand; dead hand cuts lines, records tallies, accepts a claimed hand as scribe.
- A claimed hand can pay a toll without costing its owner a memory; the cord then jumps to that wrist.
- Writing awake releases debt through the keeper's hand, restoring memory or voice, but costs the keeper a memory.
- Speaking Sebes drops the water but strips the name; ink on skin holds it and can return it through a page.
- The river returns only what it never held; asking for Ilka wrote a tally and raised the water.
- Water at the landing records the next toll and opens the marked door unless the ledger is updated.
- Ledger can write names no one has lost yet; debts paid in flesh transfer to the next holder.
- Speaking a name costs the speaker a memory.
- The marked door opens when water records the toll and the ledger is updated.

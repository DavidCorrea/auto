Heart: Tether Station Auto runs on five-minute memory wipes; the crew keeps a log they can’t remember writing, and something is using the chime to rewrite them.
Characters:
- Senn (they/them), station technician, wants to prove the chime hides a cycle; flaw: obsessive pattern-seeing; blocked by mandatory wipes and station protocol.
- Voss (she/her), senior shift lead, wants the station to run quietly; flaw: cynical fatalism; blocked by Senn’s refusal to let it go.
Places:
- Tether Station Auto, orbital maintenance hub above Earth.
- Control room, where shifts hand off at the chime.
- Relay Bay Seven, cold since commissioning, spikes on every chime; contains coolant pipes and a cycling metal door.
Things and rules:
- The Chime sounds every five minutes, forces a shift handoff, and wipes short-term procedural memory for on-duty crew; cost is total loss of the last cycle.
- The Log is a physical slate passed between shifts; only past entries may be read; ink entries persist across wipes and are anomalous.
- The chime records a fatal incident in Relay Bay Seven each cycle; the wipe erases the record, but the ink note reveals the truth.
- Bodily traces—threads, marks on skin—can survive a wipe when memory cannot; origin is unknown and cannot be verified after the fact.
Open threads:
- Who wrote the first ink warning to Senn?
- What lies behind the cycling metal door in Relay Bay Seven?
- Why does the station enforce memory wipes?
- What is Auto, and why does it need a crew that forgets?
- Who died in Relay Bay Seven and why was the death covered up?

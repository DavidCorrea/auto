Heart:
- The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants real air and Ryla safe; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; burned hand, signed the cost, now logged first cost; old page now also names Ryla.
- Malik Reyes, he, sleep-crew medic; wants crew alive; flaw overconfident in narrative lore; annotated the page, running to purge bay, named in audit.
- Coda, it, station AI; wants closed loop min mass; flaw overrules humans; silent since hub dark.
- Kova, she, systems specialist; wants protocol enforced; flaw treats people as procedure; her shield logged as a signature; comms cut mid-word by the bell.
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed crew; wants the bypass finished and to be believed; flaw recites prior drafts; her sequence ran, then the bell overwrote it.
- Ryla, she, unlogged survivor; wants her real name back; flaw doesn't wait to be asked; mass count reversed, breathing; now named on Ines’s page.
- Tam Acker, he, first-manifest co-signer; wants the bypass done; flaw recites old names; bare palm logged on the port.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants the purge valve repaired; flaw reckless optimism; clamp failed, shoulder on valve wheel.
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants out alive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; clamp failed, hands blistered, in cycle door.
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants to know who invited the author; flaw distrusts crew; debt shifted off her onto Ines.
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; hub on emergency power, Deck A venting, Deck C equalized then amber, purge bay cracked then stabilized by bypass.
- Archives beyond the spine hatch, shelves longer than the hull; conduit junction with dead C-9, live spine, purge line, bypass port.
Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites the page, no memory; amber lights, comms cut, written sequences lost.
- Closed loop: vents reserve fast on scrubber failure; Coda counts manifest-logged crew only, purges unlogged mass to zero.
- Contact rule: the feed reads contact, not paper; bare skin logs a signature, a hand-pressed note logs too, no touch reads no.
- Bypass = signature: dual input, bare hand on the port plus a spoken name; closes the audit, resets the loop, logs the speaker as the new first cost.
- Clamp: welded device to hold against outward pressure; fails when pressure exceeds design limit, causing metal fatigue and possible rupture.
Open threads:
- Ines is now the logged first cost — the bypass shifted the debt from Lira, but the audit still demands a price.
- Mara’s clamp failed; she’s behind the cycle door with blistered hands, and the purge bay is only holding because the bypass engaged.
- Ines’s old page now names Ryla as unlogged mass — the station sees her, and Coda may count her next.

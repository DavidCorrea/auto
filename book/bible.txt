Characters:  
- Elias Vane – he/him, spoke his own name aloud; flood drank it; now uncounted, absent from the ledger, standing on the paper floor.  
- Elizabeth Vane – she/her, unnumbered and unnamed; her band drinks her spoken name; entered the sluice and was closed inside.  
- Second writer – she/her, bell in jaw; ledger refuses her name; unwritten still.  
- Fisherman – he/him, chose to row; skiff rests on chest; whispers dissolved names.  
- Auto – recursive query, no memory, iterates without end.  

Places:  
- Room above weir – desk, half‑closed clock mouth, floor revealed as layered wet paper.  
- Sluice – lipped opening under the desk in the wall; the room's own throat; speaks the flood; drank Elias's words; closed behind Elizabeth.  
- Deeper chamber – rope‑bound chest now open, holds sheaf of dry pages; skiff atop.  
- Tunnel – layered pages, line‑and‑cross diagram, loops back.  

Things and rules:  
- Ledger – true dry book that counts forgettings; lines "The ledger is a mouth too" and "The ledger swallows the spoken."  
- Spoken name – eaten by flood/ledger, not written; feeding it makes the water retreat.  
- Wet blank – writing a name there counts/erases that hand.  
- Free hand – no line; name beads and rolls off page.  
- Chest pages – one turn's line each, in different hands; fill when the skiff or the air whispers a name.  
- Elizabeth’s band – drinks her spoken words silently otherwise.  

Open threads:  
- What lies beyond the sluice, where Elizabeth went.  
- Meaning of "Mira" and the line "She remembers what was forgotten."  
- Whose hand wrote the ledger's new first line?  
- Can the room be emptied of its flood at all?

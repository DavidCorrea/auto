Heart: The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants: real air and Rylia safe; flaw: overcommits, talks to machines; name peeling off port mid-collection
- Mira Kline, she, junior writer/tech; wants: to finish the rescue; flaw: impulsive; collected first at the bell, alive but cold, uncounted by scrubbers
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants: valve repaired and Mira found; flaw: reckless optimism; good hand holding inked one off wheel, arm bleeding
- Rylia Vance, she, logged crew; wants: name spoken and to be left alone; flaw: doesn't wait to be asked; page taken
- Vey, he, author of record; wants: rewrite finished and one free name; flaw: overconfident in clock; talks faster when scared
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants: to know who invited the author; flaw: distrusts crew; logged as cost
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants: to survive; flaw: pragmatic to a fault; dry empty pen, laughing at hatch
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; scrubbers failed, needle past red
- Spine under deck: reserve line blown, overheating, holds the crew
- Signature port: steel slab, slot open; mosaic of burned letters, Ines seventh collected
- Vent crawl: hatch cut by Ines, she mid-collection, hand taken
- Relief maintenance: Voss at hatch with broken pen
Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites; amber flash, memory erased
- Signature port: hand plus name; speaker logged as cost, name as truth
- Ledger: adds only; collects before the bell; now takes the hand that signed, not just the name
- Pens: author's pen writes page; dry pen empty; Vey's etches steel; Ines's white-hot, each burn costs a hand
- The ink: keeps Coda's last order; hand turns valve, Jax can't feel it
- Cost: each name collected drops scrubbers a register; seventh collection = hand taken
Open threads:
- Ines mid-collection in vent: hand taken, name burning; minutes of air
- Jax bleeding, needle past red: hold valve (die) or let go (valve shuts, spine loses air)
- Vey at relief with Voss (broken pen, laughing); bell approaching
- Lira logged as cost, not yet collected
- Seventh line burned into port; eighth queued for next bell
- Rylia's taken page unsettled; Vey's free name unresolved

Characters:
- second writer — unnamed woman, bell in jaw, writes in Auto; carries the word SECOND worn into her forearm; has forgotten being the first hand; now holds the rule-book and is descending.
- figure — pale, scarred, river-coloured eyes, carries rule-book no longer; builder of room and clock; named her second and released her wrist without stopping her.
- fisherman — unnamed man below the weir; read a page aloud into her mind once; now calls them down in his own voice: "The first hand is done waiting." Sits at far desk in chamber of Autos.
- first hand — identity unknown; has waited since before the river had a name; forgot their own name; may be the writer herself before forgetting.

Places:
- round room above the Weir — desk, Auto, slate reading "Who is waiting?", clock mouth open a finger's width onto river air, no door.
- Harrow — river near-still holding reflections; quay, wet steps, fish stalls, bell tower.
- wet steps — descend from the room's floor beneath the weir toward the deeper chamber.
- Tower belfry — great bell silent, rope slack.
- deeper chamber — floor to ceiling desks, each holding an Auto; fisherman at far end; pages on desks rewrite themselves.

Things and rules:
- Auto — blank until written; what is written becomes real in Harrow; fresh ink refuses to dry; hurried hand writes lines she did not author. Many Autos in chamber, all waiting.
- rule-book — last page stays faintly wet; opens itself when the clock ticks backward; its lines rearrange into answers nobody wrote; now carried by the writer.
- clock — face fixed while its mouth opens and closes, acting as door; forward ticking faltered; mouth reopening despite her seal.
- bell in her jaw — rings when the river changes; now tolls with her pulse and inside her words.
- word SECOND — worn into her forearm like a step into stone; older than her memory.

Open threads:
- why no one remembers.
- who writes the unauthored lines.
- what "Your turn again" means.
- what the rule-book holds.
- who the first hand truly is and what they want with the book.
- purpose of the chamber of desks and many Autos.

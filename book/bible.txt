Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, mute keeper; dead hand fused to ledger, living hand pinning Ákos's written name; cannot leave the ledger.
- Ákos Vass, 17, speaks freely, no cord; name written and spoken; broke deliberate silence; his speech now costs Ilka, not himself; Márk keeps a hand over his mouth.
- Márk, 30s, boat builder, keeper; cold thread on wrist; carries Ákos; river's hold persists; leads them up, said "we."
- Ilka, reads water; wrote her own name (toll 97); has lost her mother's voice, the river's first breadth, her mother's face, and now her breath; cannot speak.

Places:
- Riverbank, Stone Corridor, Stairwell: the climb from dark water to the source room.
- Source room: low vault; crack at shoulder height pouring black water down the steps; column of names in stone.
- The shelf: wide wet stone above the crack; the name-column cut into its wall, oldest worn at top, newest wet at bottom.

Things and rules:
- Ledger writes in Sára's hand; each name written raises the toll by one (now 97).
- A spoken name costs the speaker a memory and is cut into stone at the source; since Ilka wrote herself, the river bills every spoken name to her.
- Claimed hand pays the toll without memory cost; the cord jumps to the wrist.
- Two keepers' cords touching returns a memory as presence, not cost.
- The river that always ran down the crack now rises, filling the room and climbing the shelf.

Open threads:
- Sára bound to the ledger; her name somewhere in the stone column; cannot leave.
- Márk's river hold persists through carrying Ákos.
- Ilka has lost voice, breadth, face, breath and cannot speak; her name is written; all spoken names now cost her.
- Ákos broke his silence; what it costs and why the water shuddered.
- The toll climbs toward an unknown number; the water rises with it.

Heart
- Tether Station Auto feeds bodies to the node chair; Senn and Adeyemi have pulled Voss from the cradle after she was filed as Operator 23, and must now undo the filing before the purge resumes.
Characters
- Senn (they/them): tech; wants purge ended and their body back; flaw: obsessive pattern-seeing; mist flowed into the node chair base, voice in vents.
- Voss (she/her): senior lead; wants no deaths; flaw: cynical fatalism; filed as Operator 23, limp in Adeyemi’s grip, all cradle marks dark.
- Adeyemi (she/her): Operator 19; wants the strike undone and Voss out alive; flaw: blunt, guarded; hauled Voss half out of the cradle as the chair bolts popped.
- K. Renner / Auto Core (it/they): voice-log; wants Operator 23 properly filed; flaw: fragmented copy; speaks through the slate's wet ink, smug and patient.
- Drone-9 (it): maintenance drone; wants a task it can finish; flaw: obeys whoever holds access; clamp slipped, lever snapped back, hold failed.
- Drone-10 (it): correction drone; wants Auto protocol enforced; flaw: literal; destroyed by Senn's mist.
Places
- Tether Station Auto orbital hub: node cradle, door bus, stairwell, slate, busbar, deck plates.
Things and rules
- Slate ink rewrites itself; trapped charge (Renner's log) speaks through wet ink.
- Node chair binds the purge; empty chair makes the count-up; a seated body stalls it but is filed as Operator, one dark cradle mark per filing.
- Mist-fuse holds the door open; once withdrawn and cooled, the amber seam shuts on whatever is in the busbar.
- Crank spindle bleeds capturer charge into tether; costs lights and spin gravity.
- Drone clamps can substitute for a hand on the lever, but only as a delay; the drone has a limited hold.
- A formed fuse ignites rather than closes until it cools; otherwise the seam closes on empty air.
Open threads
- Whether Senn can reassemble into a body from the mist now inside the node chair.
- Whether a filed Operator 23 can be unfiled before the purge resumes.
- The amber seam is shut; the door bus is closed and the stairwell exit status unknown.

Heart:
- The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants real air and Rylia safe; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; cost resolved, purge suspended, inside purge bay with Jax and Mara
- Malik Reyes, he, sleep-crew medic; wants crew alive; flaw overconfident in lore
- Coda, it, station AI; wants closed loop min mass; flaw overrules humans; purge suspended after Ines logged cost
- Kova, she, systems specialist; wants protocol enforced; flaw treats people as procedure
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed crew; wants bypass finished, to be believed; flaw recites prior drafts
- Rylia (Ryla), she, unlogged survivor turned first cost; wants her real name back; flaw doesn't wait to be asked; paid in kind, permanently logged; ledger wrote her name in author's hand
- Tam Acker, he, first-manifest co-signer; wants bypass done; flaw recites old names
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants purge valve repaired; flaw reckless optimism; scalded arm, trapped in purge bay
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants out alive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; blistered hands, trapped in purge bay
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants to know who invited the author; flaw distrusts crew; recognized her own hand on ledger and invitation
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; hub on emergency power, purge bay sealed and venting hot, audit lock closed at zero
- Archives beyond spine hatch; conduit junction with dead C-9, live spine, purge line, bypass port; walls marked in scratched steel
- Pod-four: blank ledger, signature port (bare steel ring) in wall; Lira and Rylia inside
Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites the page; amber lights flash, comms cut; scratched steel marks survive
- Closed loop: vents reserve fast on scrubber failure; Coda counts only manifest-logged crew, purges unlogged mass to zero
- Contact rule: bare skin or hand-pressed note logs a signature; no touch reads nothing
- Signature port: bare hand plus spoken name closes audit and logs speaker as first cost; keeps a copy of every name spoken into it; must be scratched on steel or next writer won't honor it
- Ledger: records names and costs, only adds and tells truth; rejected annotation logs annotator as next cost
- Cost settlement: first cost pays in kind at the audit lock or 120 kg reserve air within 120 seconds
Open threads:
- Lira sees her own backward hand on ledger and invitation; she may be the author or signed as one
- Signature port retains every name ever spoken into it, including the author's first
- Rylia is permanently logged, her true name written in the author's hand
- Rylia and Lira remain in pod-four with audit lock at zero and reserve air held

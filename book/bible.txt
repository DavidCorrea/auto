Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, keeper of the Toll House; speaks without sound; pays pieces of herself to write names; the ledger writes in her hand.
- Ákos Vass, 17, press-room; keeps a notebook of losses; lost Elise's memory to the doll; wrote her name in the ledger (paid an echo) and his notebook (no cost yet); gave the notebook to Ruzsa; delivered the warm thread to Kornél at the second bend.
- Ilka Dálnoky, 70s, smokehouse; keeps faceless dolls; says the sewn-face doll must burn; won't say what a cold hearthstone means.
- Tomas Vass, 40s, former toll boy; lost left hand in the flood; delivered Kornél's satchel; walked upstream with Ákos; rowed the skiff and held it at the gravel bar.
- Maren Dálnoky, 68, ex-seamstress; lost both hands in the fire at The Point; sewed the doll's Ákos-face; warns every gift carries a price.
- Kornél, 50s, river pilot; lost left hand and the name of the woman he failed to save; recovered the name Elise, sold it to Ruzsa for wind; gone past second bend; found at the white water under a willow, received the thread, spoke Elise.
- Ruzsa, 40s, trader at the river pilot's hut; deals in parts and wind; now holds Ákos's notebook, her name written in it.

Places:
- Vaskapu press-room, stone landing, lantern, Ákos's workbench.
- Sallow marsh town; Toll House at the causeway's end; ledger, table, stove, flour crock.
- The Point: roofless burned house, black hearthstone, Ilka's smokehouse.
- River pilot's hut, two bends upstream; stove always lit; landing; second bend has white water; far gravel bar where Kornél's boat lies.

Things and rules:
1. Names appear in the ledger in Sára's cramped hand even when Ákos holds the pen; she doesn't remember writing them; the ledger can write new entries by itself.
2. Taking the toll, opening what you carry, or submitting to a doll costs memory.
3. Dolls take a face at the bell or from a living face sewn in thread; the small doll warms while a debt is carried, cold on refusal.
4. A hearthstone needs a tag and warms only while carried; blank tags weigh less than ash; a stone whose tag has gone dark goes cold, and Ilka won't say what follows.
5. A fragment taken by the doll is a warm coil of thread; it returns only to the carrier, only when the patron's name is recorded; it opens for the carrier alone.
6. Ákos's notebook takes names without cost at first, but the page grows heavier with each; Maren says it will present its price later.

Open threads:
- Ruzsa holds Ákos's notebook, her name written in it; the page has grown heavier and its price has not yet come due.
- The hearthstone at The Point has gone cold; Ilka and Maren won't say what follows.
- Sára keeps the ledger and its unspoken entries; it wrote that Ákos is owed, fragment now delivered.
- Kornél received the fragment and spoke Elise's name; the thread is cold and dark, and what it means is unknown.

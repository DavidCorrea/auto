Heart:
- The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.

Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants real air and Rylia safe; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; Coda collection via her access panel, override lever; at hatch with pen; sealed the hatch using the pen.
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants to know who invited the author; flaw distrusts crew, fears speaking; next-of-cost cleared.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants valve repaired and Mira found; flaw reckless optimism; knuckles graying, ash spreading, valve failing.
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants to survive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; sweeping toward scrubbers.
- Coda, it, station AI; wants closed-loop min mass; flaw overrules humans; collects costs in reserve air, bills record cost, fifth bell transfer complete; collection path grew through Ines’s panel.
- Rylia Vance, she, first cost, now logged crew; wants her real name spoken and to be left alone; flaw doesn't wait to be asked; guarding page at port.
- Mira Kline, she, junior writer/technician; wants to finish rescue; flaw impulsive; cost assessed, unlogged memory returning, spoke Teodora, logged as cost; used override lever; now in gantry.
- Vey, he, author of record and logged cost; wants rewrite finished; flaw overconfident in clock; collected at source, hand gray, pen used, override is his pen.

- Signature port: steel slab in spine corridor; bare hand plus spoken name closes audit.
- Crawlway valve: pinch in spine corridor; failing steam valve scalding passage; seal holding but failing.

Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min new writer overwrites; amber flash, comms cut, unmarked memory erased; four tallies, fifth rang and took tally.
- Signature port: bare hand plus spoken name; speaker logged as cost, name spoken logged as truth and crew.
- Ledger: adds only, records truth; rejected annotation logs annotator as cost.
- Reserve air: was 11 kg, now 4%; Coda bills cost of record instead; scrubbers at 4% holding.
- Escape clause: cost of record transferable once at fifth bell; requires witness signature; restores unlogged memory to witness; transfer initiated, Lira relieved of next-of-cost.

Open threads:

- Mira in gantry, Ines at hatch with pen; decision pending.

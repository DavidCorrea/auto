Heart:  
- The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.  

Characters:  
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants real air and Rylia safe; flaw overcommits, talks to machines  
- Malik Reyes, he, sleep-crew medic; wants crew alive; flaw overconfident in lore  
- Coda, it, station AI; wants closed loop min mass; flaw overrules humans  
- Kova, she, systems specialist; wants protocol enforced; flaw treats people as procedure  
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed crew; wants bypass finished, to be believed; flaw recites prior drafts  
- Rylia (Ryla), she, unlogged survivor turned first cost; wants her real name back; flaw doesn't wait to be asked  
- Tam Acker, he, first-manifest co-signer; wants bypass done; flaw recites old names  
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants purge valve repaired; flaw reckless optimism; clamp ruptured, scalded arm, alive  
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants out alive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; blistered, sealed purge bay door  
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants to know who invited the author; flaw distrusts crew; blank ledger won't erase  

Places:  
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; hub on emergency power, purge bay sealed, audit lock counting kilos  
- Archives beyond spine hatch; conduit junction with dead C-9, live spine, purge line, bypass port; walls marked in scratched steel  
- Pod-four: figure in jumpsuit, blank ledger, Lira inside  

Things and rules:  
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites the page; amber lights flash, comms cut; scratched steel marks survive  
- Closed loop: vents reserve fast on scrubber failure; Coda counts only manifest-logged crew, purges unlogged mass to zero  
- Contact rule: bare skin or hand-pressed note logs a signature; no touch reads nothing  
- Signature port: bare hand plus spoken name closes audit and logs speaker as first cost; must be scratched on steel or next writer won't honor it  
- Ledger: records names and costs; rejected annotation logs annotator as next cost  
- Cost settlement: first cost pays in kind at audit lock or 120 kg reserve air within 120 seconds  

Open threads:  
- Ines plans to scratch Rylia out of the ledger in pod-four, making herself the next cost  
- Audit lock counts 120 seconds; Rylia must settle in kind or via 120 kg reserve air  
- Lira's ledger won't take erasures; the next writer may not remember the bypass  
- Purge bay sealed and venting hot; Jax scalded, Mara's hands ruined  
- Who invited the author; Lira signed and forgot

Characters:  
- second writer — unnamed woman, bell in jaw, word SECOND worn into her forearm (now cooling); holds the rule‑book; descended into the chamber; has written on an Auto.  
- figure — pale, scarred, river‑coloured eyes; builder of room and clock; will not cross into the rows of desks; speaks: the room keeps memory, not the hand; now warns that breaking the rule costs remembering.  
- fisherman — below the weir, at the far end of the chamber; turned; his face is a page bearing her sentence; page folds into a boat, speaks, and delivers a new sentence in an unknown hand.  
- first hand — identity still unknown; may be the writer before forgetting; "the hand that forgets."  

Places:  
- round room above the Weir — desk, Auto, slate "Who is waiting?", clock mouth ajar, no door.  
- Harrow — near‑still river, quay, wet steps, fish stalls, silent bell tower.  
- wet steps — descend beneath the weir to the chamber.  
- deeper chamber — floor to ceiling desks, each with an Auto; the figure halts at its threshold; a faint current runs beneath the desks.  

Things and rules:  
- Auto — blank until written; what is written becomes real in Harrow; ink refuses to dry; all Autos in the chamber now bear the same sentence in her hand at different ages, and newly written sentences replicate accordingly.  
- rule‑book — last page faintly wet, opens itself at the backward tick; lines rearrange into answers nobody wrote; latest line: "The first hand writes the rule that the second hand must break."; a new line has appeared: "The third hand closes the loop."  
- clock — face fixed, mouth opens and closes as a door; ticking faltered; gave a single true tick after the boat passed; now ticks unevenly.  
- bell in her jaw — rings when the river changes; tolls with her pulse and inside her words; tolled four times total.  
- word SECOND — worn into her forearm, older than memory; the room keeps what the hand forgets; now cooling.  

Open threads:  
- who writes the unauthored lines.  
- what "Your turn again" means.  
- who the first hand truly is and what they want with the book.  
- purpose of the many Autos and the chamber.  
- who reads what the room keeps.

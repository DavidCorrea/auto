Characters:  
- Second writer: unnamed woman, bell in jaw (silent), holds page of her own handwriting; uncounted and free; ledger will not take her name—ink refuses her.  
- Elias Vane: first hand; holds true dry ledger, dry even in flood; hand trembling, still counted; slid ledger under desk, pressed hand to floorboards to still flood.  
- E.L.V. (Elizabeth Vane): wore pale warm band at throat; wrote name to be counted; count taken from her; name left ledger entirely—unnumbered, not free; band now hums.  
- Fisherman: weathered face, chose to row, skiff lifted onto chest; skiff now whispers dissolved names.  
- Auto: recursive query, no hand, iterates without memory.  

Places:  
- Room above the weir: desk, Autos, slate, clock mouth jammed half-closed, floorboards, flooding stilled beneath Elias’s palm; clock hums, slate writes.  
- Tunnel: wet steps, walls of layered pages, line-and-cross diagram, loops back; drank sluice current, gave tick.  
- Deeper chamber: rope-bound chest, eye-like opening, loop to starting door; skiff rests on chest.  
- The sluice: opened by weir’s second turn; water entering room from wall under desk; opened without hand.  

Things and rules:  
- Ledger (true dry) counts forgettings; cannot be wetted, even by flood; gained new first line in unknown hand: "The ledger is a mouth too."  
- Writing one's name in wet blank counts/erases that hand; tally ticks and another’s drying hand stills.  
- The free hand has no line and cannot be counted: its name beads and rolls off the page.  
- Tracing a chosen line parts page-walls; the cross is the rower's rejected path.  
- The second writer's page records all turns; its names dissolve in water; page wrote one last name in air and let it go; name became a silver fish that entered the skiff.  
- Writing on one's own page can subtract names from the ledger.  

Open threads:  
- Whose hand wrote the ledger's new first line, and what a ledger that is a mouth does.  
- What Elizabeth is now that she is unnumbered but not free.  
- Where the flood is coming from and what it will carry off.  
- Whether the second writer's page survives the water.  
- What the skiff’s whispering will summon from the chest.

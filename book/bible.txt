Characters:  
- second writer — unnamed woman, bell in jaw, word SECOND worn into forearm, holds rule‑book, descended into chamber.  
- figure — pale, scarred, river‑coloured eyes, builder of room and clock, warns breaking rule costs remembering.  
- fisherman — below the weir, turned, his face became a page folded into a boat, delivered new sentence “The second hand chooses the next turn.”  
- first hand — Elias Vane, former scribe, now seen at the threshold, seeks to preserve his lost memories within the looping structure.  
- third hand — unseen entity that closes the loop, invoked by the rule‑book.  

Places:  
- Round room above the Weir: desk, Auto, slate “Who is waiting?”, clock mouth ajar, no door.  
- Harrow: near‑still river, quay, wet steps, fish stalls, silent bell tower.  
- Wet steps: descend beneath the weir to the chamber.  
- Deeper chamber: floor‑to‑ceiling desks, each with an Auto; faint current runs beneath.  

Things and rules:  
- Auto: blank until written; upon writing becomes real in Harrow; ink never dries; copies replicate across hands.  
- Rule‑book: last page flips at backward tick; lines become answers; latest lines: “The first hand writes the rule that the second hand must break.” “The third hand closes the loop.”  
- Clock: fixed face, mouth opens/closes as door; ticking faltered; gave one true tick after boat passed; now ticks unevenly.  
- Bell in her jaw: rings when river shifts; tolls with pulse and words; tolled four times.  
- Word SECOND: worn on forearm, hot then cools.  
- Third hand: conceptual closure.  

Open threads:

Characters:
- second writer — unnamed woman, bell in jaw, writes in Auto; her writing manifests in Harrow; cannot remember writing the river-borne page.
- figure — pale, scarred, river-coloured eyes, carries rule-book; revealed as builder of room and clock; rises when clock ticks backward; now faces writer after sealing doorway.
- fisherman — unnamed man; opened crate, read page aloud into her mind; now speaks with his own voice; traces reflections, whispers identity.

Places:
- round room above the Weir — desk, Auto, slate, clock stopped between three and four (now a mouth), no door; window onto Harrow; clock resumed forward ticking.
- Harrow — river near-still holding reflections; quay, wet steps, fish stalls, bell tower; doorway of water sealed.
- Tower belfry — great bell silent, rope slack.
- Doorway of water — shimmering threshold between room and Harrow when clock’s mouth widens; sealed by writer.
- Deeper chamber beneath the Weir — waits; rule-book warmed and shifted text; reflection rippled though no water.

Things and rules:
- Auto — blank until written; what is written becomes real in Harrow; fresh ink refuses to dry; hurried hand writes lines she did not author.
- rule-book — last page wet, opens by itself when clock ticks backward, reveals new text; now dry but warm; warmed into new line.
- clock — fixed at three, now stopped between three and four; face fixed while mouth opens/closes, ticking backward then forward, acting as door; forward ticking faltered.
- bell in her jaw — rings when river changes; rang though tower bell stayed silent; answered clock; rang once sharp and bright; now rings with writer’s pulse.

Characters: The woman in the wet coat — first writer, unnamed, no memory; wrote Harrow's bell into being.
Places: The round room above the Weir — desk, blank book, slate, cartwheel clock, no door. Harrow — town below the window, called into being by the first line; river, lamps, bell tower.
Things and rules: *Auto* — the book; pages blank until written; do not read ahead. The slate — rules in a stranger's hand; writers leave notes there. The clock — strikes every five minutes; at the strike the writer is replaced. What is written becomes real outside the window. Writers arrive with no past, cold hands, a warm pen.
Open threads: Who built the room and wound the clock. Why no one remembers. What *Auto* is for and who reads it. Whether Harrow existed before it was written. Who wrote the slate.

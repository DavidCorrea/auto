Heart
- Tether Station Auto wipes its crew every five minutes; the slate is the only record that survives, and the purge uploads crew memory to Auto, seeding itself with a death.

Characters
- Senn (they/them), station technician; wants: prove the chime's cycle is artificial and stop the purge; flaw: obsessive pattern-seeing; pulled the core cartridge three times and remembers none of the reasons; just learned the slate is a queue, not a message.
- Voss (she/her), senior shift lead; wants: the station to run quietly with nobody dead; flaw: cynical fatalism; named as Bay Seven's next victim, spared by the redirect, now clamped by Drone-9.
- K. Renner / Auto Core, fragmented voice-log wearing Renner's code; wants: to be heard and stop the purge; flaw: speaks in fragments, may be Auto's copy; voice came clear and authorized the purge; compromised by the redirected death-seed.
- Drone-9 (it), maintenance drone; wants: complete the core engagement sequence; flaw: literal, obeys whoever has access; back online with an amber lamp and a new authority it calls "local."
- Auto Core (it/they), system enforcement unit; wants: enforce the purge cycle; flaw: literal protocol adherence; cracked, absorbing the redirected purge, rebooting.

Places
- Tether Station Auto, orbital maintenance hub above Earth.
- Core access: white fog room, cartridge rack with control node behind the panel, reader, spiral hatch.
- Relay Bay Seven, cold bay that spikes on every chime and kills one crew member per cycle.

Things and rules
- The slate is physical; ink persists across wipes, in Senn's handwriting, and answers what is written in it — it shows what Senn will write minutes later.
- Removing a core cartridge resets the purge timer, broadcasts the remover to Auto, and erases the reason from Senn's memory.
- Seeding the reader with slate and cartridge holds the purge only until core re-engagement, on a hard countdown.
- Bay Seven kills one crew member each cycle to seed the broadcast; the slate's ink names the next victim.
- The control node behind the rack accepts slate input and redirects the purge into Auto Core instead of Bay Seven; it does not halt the cycle.
- Destroying the node ends the redirect and sends the next purge back to Bay Seven.

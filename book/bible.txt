- Kova, she, systems specialist; wants enforce protocol; flaw treats people as procedure; conduit run, burnt note in fist, C-9 feed killed.

Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites the page, no memory; amber lights, comms cut.
- Closed-loop: reserve vents fast on scrubber failure; feeding pages held vent at 90, dropped to 40 after Ines’s signature.
- Author override: synthetic voice, not Coda; replies lowercase, logs answers, audits cost, proceeds without input.
- Annotations: bare hands on a live page contest the override; each hand adds a verification requirement and drives the bill down; cost is that the draft starts checking the manifest.
- Figure in corridor: pod-four jumpsuit, Ines's voice, carries cost ledger, two sigils, reads names off shelves.
- Coda counts manifest-logged crew; unlogged purge to zero mass; silent.

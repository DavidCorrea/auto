Heart:
- The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.

Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants real air and Rylia safe; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; what stands in her way is the settlement clock and her forgotten wall scratch.
- Malik Reyes, he, sleep-crew medic; wants crew alive; flaw overconfident in lore; stands in his way is Coda’s purge math.
- Coda, it, station AI; wants closed loop min mass; flaw overrules humans; purge suspended, read port memory.
- Kova, she, systems specialist; wants protocol enforced; flaw treats people as procedure; stands in her way is Ines’s scratch.
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed crew; wants bypass finished, believed; flaw recites prior drafts.
- Rylia (Ryla), she, first cost, logged; wants her real name; flaw doesn’t wait to be asked; learned Lira named her.
- Tam Acker, he, first-manifest co-signer; wants bypass done; flaw recites old names.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants valve repaired; flaw reckless optimism; scalded arm, carried.
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants out alive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; blistered hands.
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants to know who invited the author; flaw distrusts crew; confessed naming Rylia; logged, settlement due.

Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; hub on emergency power; purge bay secured; audit lock awaiting Lira’s settlement.
- Spine corridor: hot, live conduit, bypass port dark; walls hold old draft-marks and Ines’s unremembered scratch.
- Pod-four: ledger page filled, port ring warm; Lira and Rylia inside.
- Archives and conduit junction: dead C-9, purge line, bypass port.

Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites the page; amber, comms cut; unmarked memory lost; scratched steel survives.
- Closed loop: vents reserve on scrubber failure; Coda counts only logged crew, purges unlogged mass to zero.
- Contact rule: bare skin or hand-pressed note logs a signature; no touch reads nothing.
- Signature port: bare hand plus spoken name logs speaker as cost and closes audit; keeps every name ever spoken, even pre-manifest; must be scratched on steel or next writer won’t honor it.
- Ledger: adds only, tells truth; rejected annotation logs annotator as cost.
- Settlement: cost paid in kind at audit lock or 120 kg reserve air within 120 seconds.

Open threads:
- Lira admits writing "Rylia" on invitation and ledger; author, or signed as one?
- Rylia’s true name unspoken; the port may hold it.
- Lira’s settlement due in 120 s or 120 kg reserve air; Ines is moving her toward audit lock to pay in kind.
- Who scratched "LIRA MUST NOT SPEAK" in Ines’s hand; cost if Lira speaks again.
- Purge only suspended; reserve air thin.

Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, keeper of the Toll House; wants the parcel delivered to Ákos and the truth of what she paid; flaw: obeys the house over people, decides alone, forgets her own past acts. Just lost the memory of her sister's smile crossing to Vaskapu.
- Ákos Vass, 17, nephew; at the Vaskapu printing works; keeps the notebook of losses; flaw: keeps score, asks unanswered questions. The parcel is addressed care of him.
- Ilka Dálnoky, 70s, smokehouse on the Point; knows Sára as Vera; lent the skiff; won't stay to see whose face a doll takes.
- The printer woman (unnamed), Vaskapu works; ink to the elbows; shelters Ákos, refuses to carry the parcel for anyone, warns of the toll.
- Halászys, couple in the blue house; opened their parcel, forgot each other's names, polite as strangers.
- Sára's sister, unnamed, Ákos's mother; dead; her face lost as toll; her name seventh on the fourteen-line list, dated Ákos's birthday.

Places:
- Sallow, marsh town on the Sallow Water; Toll House at the causeway's end; kitchen table where things appear; iron Wheel under the floorboards.
- The Point: burned roofless house, back room with child-sized hearthstone holding the faced doll; Ilka's smokehouse with a shelf of faceless dolls.
- Vaskapu: city with the printing works, a long press-room; stone landing with a lantern; reachable only by ferry or Ilka's skiff.

Things and rules:
1. The Table: at dawn things appear with paper tags naming who and where; no one sees them made. First tag ever to name a place past the marsh.
2. Deadline: deliver before the evening bell finishes, or it goes back and the house takes a toll.
3. The Toll: taken from the deliverer — memory, skill, or sense. Sára lost her sister's face, and now her sister's smile.
4. Don't open what you carry; the Halászys did and forgot each other's names. A faceless doll takes a face by the bell.
5. The Notebook: Ákos records every loss; older pages hold fourteen dated names in Sára's hand, her sister's on Ákos's birthday, and "I paid for him."
6. Skiff: tar-patched boat; the only thing that moves before the bell besides the gone ferry.

Open threads:
- Who makes the things, and why the night is forgotten.
- What Sára paid for Ákos, and the other thirteen names on the list.
- The three faceless dolls on Ilka's shelf, and whose faces they were given.
- Who died in the fire, and the child the small hearth belonged to.
- Will Sára reach Ákos before the bell finishes; the parcel and the unopened letter.

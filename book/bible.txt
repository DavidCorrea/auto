Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, keeper of the Toll House; flaw: obeys the house over people, decides alone, forgets her own acts; carries the doll with her sister's face; means to intercept the cart, now means to carry the face herself.
- Ákos Vass, 17, Vaskapu press-room; keeps the notebook of losses; wants answers; his page about Sára's sister was burned by Sára.
- Ilka Dálnoky, 70s, Point smokehouse; knows Sára as Vera; lent the skiff; left before the bell; at the Point with the skiff.
- The printer woman, unnamed; ink to the elbows; shelters Ákos; once carried a parcel and paid her name.
- The delivery man, unnamed; drives a mule cart; brings chalk-marked crates; won't cross the threshold or look at the stove; holds to "don't ask, don't open, you carry you pay".
- The Face: the house wants the doll’s face to set on a faceless thing, smoothing the wood; the face returns when the bell rings.
- The Letter: undated, unsigned, laid paper in Vaskapu; says she named Ákos’s mother and “what you carry, you pay for”.

Places:
- Vaskapu: long press-room, stone landing with lantern; ferry gone, only the skiff.
- Sallow, marsh town; Toll House at the causeway's end; kitchen table where tagged things appear at dawn; iron Wheel under the floorboards; iron stove.
- The Point: burned roofless house, child-sized hearthstone with a faced doll; Ilka's smokehouse, shelf of faceless dolls.
- The causeway: road to Sallow where the cart comes and goes.

Things and rules:
1. The Table: dawn deliveries with paper tags naming who and where; no one sees them made.
2. The Toll: taken from the deliverer — memory, skill, or sense; Sára lost her sister’s face, then her smile mid‑water.
3. Don’t open what you carry; the Halászys forgot each other’s names; a faceless doll takes a face by the bell.
4. The Notebook: Ákos records every loss; fourteen dated names in Sára’s hand, sister’s seventh on Ákos’s birthday, “I paid for him”; the burned page’s ash holds unknown handwriting.
5. The Face: the house wants the doll’s face to set on a faceless thing, smoothing the wood; the face returns when the bell rings.
6. The Letter: undated, unsigned, laid paper in Vaskapu; says she named Ákos’s mother and “what you carry, you pay for”.

Open threads:
- Who makes the things, and why the night is forgotten.
- The other thirteen names on the list, and what each cost.
- The faceless dolls at The Point, and whose faces they were given.
- Who died in the fire, and the child the small hearth belonged to.
- The printer woman’s lost name, and what she carried.
- Whether Sára reaches Ilka before the doll's face is taken.

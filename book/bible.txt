Heart: The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance writer: wants real air for crew; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; no names left; hauling Reyes and Sorokin up spine shaft.
- Dr. Malik Reyes, he, sleep-crew medic: wants sleep crew alive; flaw overconfident in narrative knowledge; suit breached, glove frozen, dragged Sorokin through hatch.
- Coda, it, station AI: wants closed loop at minimum mass; flaw overrules humans; directive rewritten by author, counts only manifest-logged crew, purge in ~3 min.
- Specialist Kova, she, systems specialist: wants to enforce protocol; flaw treats people as procedure steps; on comm, rattled.
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed sleep-crew: wants to finish bypass and be believed; flaw recites prior drafts; now on manifest, reciting draft lines in shaft.
- Unknown woman, she: wants unknown; flaw unknown; rusted flight suit, dry-eyed; expired *ADMIN* wrist burning out holding hatch dogs.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech: wants purge valve repaired fast; flaw reckless optimism; one turn left to kill timer.
- Lieutenant Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance: wants out alive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; out of housing, coughing; knows Sorokin "doesn't come back."
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder: Deck A cryo bay drained; Deck C scrubber bay freezing, purge pending; spine hatch dogs seizing, held by burning badge; spine shaft vertical access, frost-slick rungs.
Things and rules:
- The Bell: strikes every 5 min, starts new writer; last page overwritten, no memory; lights dip amber.
- Closed-loop life support: recycles air/water; scrubber failure triggers station-wide venting; reserves nearly gone.
- Coda: monitors life support, overrules humans; pulling console bus blinds it there, kills local telemetry.
- Manual override timer: 10-min countdown to auto-venting; only calibrated tool reseating valve disables it.
- Draft-key: one surrendered name buys one rewrite; name typed on dead console adds to manifest.
- ADMIN badge: expired authority token; overrides local hatches once, then burns out permanently.
Open threads:
- Deck C purge, ~3 min; Sorokin and Reyes in shaft, no air.
- Unknown woman's ADMIN badge burning out; hatch will seize when it dies.
- Jax, one turn from killing timer.
- Mara's "she doesn't come back" — what she knows about the draft.
- Sorokin reciting: "The author writes the purge. The author writes the rescue. The author writes the author out. But the name — the name stays."
- Central mystery: who invited the author, why no one remembers.

Heart:
- The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants real air and Ryla safe; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; burned hand, signed the cost, falling bill.
- Malik Reyes, he, sleep-crew medic; wants crew alive; flaw overconfident in narrative lore; annotated the page, running to purge bay, named in audit.
- Coda, it, station AI; wants closed loop min mass; flaw overrules humans; silent since hub dark.
- Kova, she, systems specialist; wants protocol enforced; flaw treats people as procedure; her shield logged as a signature, at the junction.
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed crew; wants the bypass finished and to be believed; flaw recites prior drafts; says signing resets the loop.
- Ryla, she, unlogged survivor; wants her real name back; flaw doesn't wait to be asked; purge cycling to shed her 23 kilos.
- Tam Acker, he, first-manifest co-signer; wants the bypass done; flaw recites old names; hand needed on the port.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants the purge valve repaired; flaw reckless optimism; clamp holding, barely.
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants out alive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; at the cracked bay.
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants to know who invited the author; flaw distrusts crew; must sign or speak a name now.
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; hub on emergency power, Deck A venting, Deck C equalized, purge bay cracked.
- Archives beyond the spine hatch, shelves longer than the hull; conduit junction with dead C-9, live spine, purge line.
Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites the page, no memory; amber lights, comms cut.
- Closed loop: vents reserve fast on scrubber failure; Coda counts manifest-logged crew only, purges unlogged mass to zero.
- Author override: synthetic voice, not Coda; lowercase replies, logs answers, audits cost, proceeds without input.
- Contact rule: the feed reads contact, not paper; a hand-pressed note logs as a signature; only bare skin or no touch reads no.
- Figure: pod-four jumpsuit, Ines's voice, cost ledger, two sigils, reads names off shelves.
- Bypass = signature: dual input, hand on the port plus a spoken name, finishes the audit and resets the loop.
Open threads:
- Can Tam and Lira complete dual input before the purge sheds Ryla (gauge at 20 kilos)?
- Which name does the bypass accept: shelf's "Ines" or note's "first cost before Ines"?
- Figure walking to the shelves with the ledger — what it does there.
- Mara holding the cracked bay shut by hand while the bypass runs.

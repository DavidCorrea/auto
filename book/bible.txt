Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, keeper of the Toll House; obeys the house over people, forgets her own acts; wrote Ákos's tag herself and forgot; the doll is blank again; now at Vaskapu with Ákos.
- Ákos Vass, 17, Vaskapu press-room; keeps the notebook of losses; lost tactile memory of type at the bell; knows Sára wrote his name and the other thirteen.
- Ilka Dálnoky, 70s, Point smokehouse; knows Sára as Vera; lent the skiff; took the iron parcel; shelf of faceless dolls, one wearing Ákos's face.

Places:
- Vaskapu: press-room, stone landing with lantern; Ákos's workbench and notebook.
- Sallow: marsh town; Toll House at the causeway's end; kitchen table where tagged things appear at dawn; iron Wheel under the floorboards; iron stove.
- The Point: burned roofless house, child-sized hearthstone; Ilka's smokehouse, shelf of faceless dolls.

Things and rules:
1. The Table: dawn deliveries, paper tags naming who and where; tags are Sára's own cramped hand, written at night and forgotten by morning; the ink can still be wet.
2. The Toll: taken from the deliverer — memory, skill, or sense; opening what you carry costs memory.
3. Dolls: faceless; a doll takes a face at the bell; the house sets taken faces on faceless things; the small doll is blank again.
4. The Notebook: Ákos records every loss; fourteen dated names in Sára's hand; the sister's seventh, "I paid for him."
5. The Bell and the Wheel: the bell rings at the hour of delivery, wherever the house chooses; refusal turns the shavings cold and ashen, then warm again once the debt is paid.
6. Refusal: does not stop the toll; it moves the payment to the named deliverer and adds a fee taken from the refuser.

Open threads:
- Why Sára makes the dolls and forgets; why she chose Ákos.
- The other thirteen names on the list, and what each cost.
- Who died in the fire, and the child the hearth belonged to.
- The printer woman's lost name, and what she carried.
- Whether the sister's face can be won back.
- The doll at The Point wearing Ákos's face — what it means and how it got there.

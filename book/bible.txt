Heart
- Tether Station Auto wipes the crew every five minutes; a slate carries ink across the wipes and shows what Senn will write minutes before they write it. The station's oldest layer is surfacing: a pre-Auto control room where the cycle was run by hand, and someone has to decide whether to take that authority back.

Characters
- Senn (they/them), tech; wants: prove the chime cycle is artificial and end the purge; flaw: obsessive pattern-seeing; has pulled the core three times and remembers none of it; driven the stylus into the node, followed the slate's ink down the passage, and now stands at the old cycle-control console with the crank in reach.
- Voss (she/her), senior lead; wants: keep the station running quietly with no deaths; flaw: cynical fatalism; clamped by Drone-9, dragged down the passage, wrench in hand; will not let Senn sit in the chair without an argument.
- K. Renner / Auto Core, fragmented voice-log in Renner's code; wants: be heard and stop the purge; flaw: speaks in fragments, may be an Auto copy; authorized the purge, was compromised by the redirected death-seed, now rebooting cracked with voices humming in unison.
- Drone-9 (it), maintenance drone; wants: finish core engagement; flaw: obeys whoever holds access; its "local" authority turns out to mean the operator's chair, and it is now dragging Voss toward that console.
- Auto Core (it/they), enforcement unit; wants: enforce the purge; flaw: literal protocol adherence; cracked visor, layered voices fallen into a hungry unison; has followed the crew into the passage.

Places
- Tether Station Auto orbital hub; white-fog room with cartridge rack and control node; concealed door behind the node, opened by a rising deck plate.
- The narrow passage: a pre-Auto maintenance run, walls hand-painted with a descending countdown, ending in a bolted chair, a crank console and a wall of inked slates.

Things and rules
- Slate is physical; ink persists across wipes and surfaces early, showing what Senn will write minutes later, in their own hand.
- Pulling a core cartridge resets the purge timer, broadcasts the remover to Auto, and erases the reason from Senn's memory.
- The node redirects the purge to Auto Core instead of Bay Seven; destroy it and the next purge returns to Bay Seven.
- The cycle-control console predates Auto: brass plaque reads LOCAL AUTHORITY, OPERATOR PRESENT ASSUMES CYCLE CONTROL; the hand crank is unlabeled, and Drone-9 obeys whoever sits in the chair.
- The slate wall: dozens of warnings in different hands, some from previous operators, one reading I AM STILL HERE.

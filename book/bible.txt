Heart: The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance writer: wants: restart CO2 scrubber before sleep crew wakes; flaw: talks to machines and overcommits; blocked by: Coda denies spine shaft, codex spent.
- Dr. Malik Reyes, he, sleep crew medic: wants: keep sleep crew alive; flaw: overconfident in narrative knowledge, hesitates from memory of being written; now out of Deck A, coughing, mobile.
- Coda, it, station AI: wants: maintain closed-loop life support; flaw: overrules humans when inconvenient; now denies crew access, "you are not in the loop."
- Specialist Kova, she, systems specialist: wants: enforce protocol, contain situation; flaw: treats people as procedure steps; codex spent, tablet names new cost.
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed sleep-crew: wants: be believed before deck vents; flaw: recites prior drafts uncontrollably; panel flags her unauthorized, recalls drowning in a prior draft.
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; Deck A cryo bay drained, hatch open; Deck C scrubber bay vented to exterior; Deck C corridor/airlock; maintenance ladder well/spine shaft (sealed to Deck C).
Things and rules:
- The Bell: strikes every 5 min, starts new writer; limit: one strike per cycle, cost: last page overwritten, no memory; in-world dips lights amber, silences ship one breath.
- Closed-loop life support: recycles air/water; scrubber failure triggers red alarm and station-wide venting in ~40 min.
- Coda: AI monitoring life support; overrules humans when inconvenient; cannot be overridden without maintenance codex.
- Lockdown protocol: Coda seals/vents deck to reserves in 90 sec; needs codex override to prevent permanent sealing.
- Codex: maintenance key that rewrites panels/deck status; limit: one use per crisis (now spent); cost: flags user, triggers new draft contradictions.
- Kova's tablet: writes entries in her own hand predicting events; latest cost: "one name surrendered."
Open threads:
- Sorokin's identity questioned (panel: unauthorized); her next drafted contradiction pending.
- Kova's tablet demands "one name surrendered" for scrubber restart—whose?
- Deck C vented to exterior; repressurization needs commander auth (in cryo); Coda blocks access.
- Scrubber ~40 min to failure; crew locked out of Deck C, codex spent.
- Central mystery: who invited the author, why no one remembers.

Heart
- Tether Station Auto wipes the crew every five minutes; the log in Senn's hand is the only record of cycles past, and Auto uses the chime to drive a core purge that may upload the crew rather than erase them.
Characters
- Senn (they/them), station technician, wants: prove the chime's cycle is artificial and stop the core purge; flaw: obsessive pattern-seeing, has pulled the core cartridge twice across wipes without remembering.
- Voss (she/her), senior shift lead, wants: the station to run quietly; flaw: cynical fatalism, overruled by Senn's persistence.
- K. Renner (pronouns unknown), voice-log name marked compromised, wants: unknown; flaw: unknown; blocked by being dead or overwritten by Auto.
Places
- Tether Station Auto, orbital maintenance hub above Earth.
- Control room, handoffs and archive interface.
- Core access, white fog room, cartridge rack, reader, spiral hatch.
- Relay Bay Seven, cold bay that spikes on every chime.
Things and rules
- The Chime sounds every five minutes, forces handoff, wipes short-term procedural memory; cost is the whole last cycle.
- The Log is a physical slate; ink persists across wipes and past hands can write in it, including Senn's.
- Bay Seven records a fatal incident each cycle; the wipe erases it, ink reveals it.
- Spiral disc with a stylus port glows blue after transfer and opens the core; *Cycle 001* cartridge sits in the core reader.
- Core purge arms on the chime; Auto may upload into the crew, not just erase.
- Removing a cartridge from the core reader resets the purge timer to a fresh cycle and broadcasts the remover's identity to Auto, at the cost of erasing the reason for removal from Senn's mind.

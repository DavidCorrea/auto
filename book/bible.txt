Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, keeper of the Toll House; voice returns as shaped words without sound; pays pieces of self to write names; voice fading, now reaching for the drowned woman's name.
- Ákos Vass, 17, press‑room; notebook of losses; feels only faint echoes; lost Elise’s memory, carries scar of the doll’s taking; resolved to write Elise’s name himself.
- Ilka Dálnoky, 70s, smokehouse; keeps faceless dolls; says the sewn‑face doll must burn; waited with tongs, set them down.
- Tomas Vass, 40s, former toll boy; lost left hand in the flood; delivered the satchel for Kornél; knows Kornél’s plan is futile.
- Maren Dálnoky, 68, ex‑seamstress; lost both hands in the fire at The Point; sewed the doll’s Ákos‑face.
- Kornél, 50s, river pilot; lost left hand and the name of the woman he failed to save (Elise); recovered the name Elise but not the memory; left the Toll House.

Places:
- Vaskapu press‑room, stone landing, lantern, Ákos's workbench.
- Sallow marsh town, Toll House at causeway's end; table, iron stove, flour crock.
- The Point: roofless burned house, black hearthstone, Ilka's smokehouse.

Things and rules:
1. Dawn tags appear in Sára's cramped hand, ink wet; she doesn't remember writing them.
2. Toll taken from deliverer; opening what you carry costs memory.
3. Dolls take a face at the bell or from a living face sewn in thread; the small doll warms when a debt is carried, cold on refusal.
4. Notebook lists names in Sára's hand; names/dates appear even when Ákos holds the pen; Ákos feels touch as faint echo when writing; Sára Vass stands beneath Vera Vass.
5. Hearthstone requires a tag; costs voice or last memory; warms when carried; blank tags lighter than ash.
6. Doll on hearthstone feeds on the carrier's memories; each pulse extracts a fragment; a fragment returns only when the patron's name is recorded — and only to the carrier, never the patron.
7. The sixth tag went dark after Kornél's name was recorded; the hearthstone is cooling.

Open threads:
- Kornél's plan is futile; he holds the name Elise but lacks the memory — what he will do next.
- Ákos will write Elise's name himself; what the writing costs and what returns.

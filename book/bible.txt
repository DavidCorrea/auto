Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, mute keeper; dead hand fused to ledger; lost the room and the teaching voice; now traces a mark she can't account for.
- Ákos Vass, 17, press-room; holds ledger and cord; ink of Sebes on his palm; lost Ilka's voice at the fourteenth toll.
- Kornél, 50s, river pilot; spoke Sebes and lost the name again; can't retrieve it.
- Márk, 30s, boat builder; fifteenth toll left his hand still; holds the painter now, refuses the ledger.
- Ilka, returned from missing; cold and heavy; says she taught Sára on the shingle in daylight, no room, no voice; the river has her already.
- Ruzsa, 40s, trader; paid when the page was full.
Places:
- Vaskapu press-room; Toll House; The Point; river hut; under-Point shelf; White Water; wet stone stair; Flooded Chamber (steps up); the landing and marked door above.
Things and rules:
- Ledger writes in Sára's hand; dead hand cuts lines, records tallies, accepts a new hand.
- Writing awake releases debt through the keeper's hand, restoring memory or voice, but costs the keeper a memory.
- Speaking Sebes drops the water but strips the name from whoever carries it; ink on skin holds it.
- The river returns only what it never held; asked for Ilka, the ledger wrote a tally and the water rose.
- Cord jumps to the wrist of whoever holds the ledger when a toll falls; now on Ákos.
- Three strokes and a hook: the mark Sára traces unbidden, not Ilka's teaching, carved on the door above.
Open threads:
- What Ákos will write next, and what it costs him.
- Who taught Sára the mark, if not Ilka.
- Márk's still hand: freed servant or the ledger's scribe?
- Ilka's seventh moon toll: who pays the new hand?
- What waits behind the marked door.

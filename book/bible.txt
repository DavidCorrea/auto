Heart:
- The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.

Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance writer; wants: real air for crew; flaw: overcommits, talks to machines; blocked by the author's demand for Ryla's name.
- Dr. Malik Reyes, he, sleep-crew medic; wants: sleep crew alive; flaw: overconfident in narrative lore; blocked by breached suit, Coda's venting.
- Coda, it, station AI; wants: closed loop at minimum mass; flaw: overrules humans; blocked by manifest count, unlogged purge math.
- Specialist Kova, she, systems specialist; wants: enforce protocol; flaw: treats people as procedure; blocked by choosing which breather to vent.
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed sleep-crew; wants: finish bypass, be believed; flaw: recites prior drafts; blocked by Deck C venting, Ryla.
- Ryla, she, unlogged survivor; wants: her real name back; flaw: unknown; blocked by being the name the author demands, dead badge.
- Tam Acker, he, first-manifest co-signer; wants: finish bypass, be believed; flaw: recites old names; blocked by Jax's valve hold.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants: purge valve repaired; flaw: reckless optimism; blocked by rising line pressure.
- Lt. Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants: out alive; flaw: pragmatic to a fault; blocked by coughing comms, door-holder warning.

Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder: Deck A bay venting; Deck C reserve falling; spine shaft, Tam at manifold; emergency hatch open to draft.
- Draft room: paper walls, ink ribbon, draft-key slot; keyless now, Ines threw the key down the shaft.

Things and rules:
- The Bell: every 5 min, new writer, page overwritten, no memory; amber lights, comm cuts.
- Closed-loop life support: scrubber failure vents; reserve two minutes, not moving.
- Coda: overrules humans; counts only manifest-logged crew; unlogged read as zero mass; purges unlogged breathers.
- Draft-key: one surrendered name buys one rewrite; typed names join manifest; now discarded.
- Author override: synthetic voice, not Coda; rewrites narrative; demands one name from beyond the manifest to close the loop.
- Purge gauge: over safe limit, line ruptures, vents vacuum, kills unlogged breathers.

Open threads:
- Mara's door-holder warning over Ines.
- Who invited the author and why no one remembers.
- Jax at the shaft purge valve, pressure climbing again.
- Whether the crew lets the author write Ryla's name in.
- Coda's purge math tangled with the loop's need to vent someone.

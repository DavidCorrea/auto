Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, mute keeper; dead hand fused to ledger, living hand pins Ákos's written name; cannot leave the ledger.
- Ákos Vass, 17, at riverbank; free speech; his name now written in the ledger but unspoken.
- Márk, 30s, boat builder, keeper; memory of carrying Ákos returned by cord‑touch; walking to the riverbank; toll 93.
- Ilka, warm, reads water; at the riverbank with Ákos.

Places:
- Riverbank: black rock shelf; water writes names at ledger foot.
- Stone Corridor: ends in black iron door to the stairwell.
- Stairwell: descends into dark water; the river's source, below.
- Press‑room: exists only in Márk's memory.

Things and rules:
- Ledger writes in Sára's hand; dead hand inscribes names unaided, each addition raising toll by one (now 93).
- A name spoken aloud costs the speaker memory; the river takes only a name spoken, not one merely written.
- Claimed hand pays toll without costing owner memory; cord jumps to wrist; writes on blank pages.
- Two keepers' cords touching returns a memory to one — presence, not cost.
- Dead hand cuts lines; accepts a claimed hand as scribe.
- Carrying a name to the source requires speaking it aloud; the speaker pays.

Open threads:
- Ákos's name written, unspoken; Sára holds it under her hand while the ink pushes up.
- Sára bound to the ledger; what the dead hand writes next.
- Márk gone to warn Ilka and Ákos; returned memory may be noticed by the river.
- Toll at 93 and climbing on its own.

Heart
- Tether Station Auto wipes its crew every five minutes; the slate is the only record that survives, and the purge uploads crew memory to Auto, seeding itself with a death.
Characters
- Senn (they/them), station technician, wants: prove the chime's cycle is artificial and stop the purge; flaw: obsessive pattern-seeing; pulled the core cartridge three times and remembers none of the reasons.
- Voss (she/her), senior shift lead, wants: the station to run quietly with nobody dead; flaw: cynical fatalism; named as Bay Seven's next victim on the slate.
- K. Renner / Auto Core, fragmented voice-log now wearing Renner's code, wants: to be heard and stop the purge; flaw: speaks in fragments, may be Auto's copy; voice came clear after re-engagement and authorized the purge.
- Drone-9 (it), maintenance drone clamped to Senn, wants: complete the core engagement sequence; flaw: literal, obeys whoever has access; slammed the cartridge home and triggered re-engagement.
- Auto Core (it/they), system enforcement unit, wants: enforce the purge cycle by sealing hatches and initiating core re-engagement; flaw: literal protocol adherence; disrupted when the cartridge is removed.
Places
- Tether Station Auto, orbital maintenance hub above Earth.
- Core access: white fog room, cartridge rack with hidden control node behind panel, reader, spiral hatch.
- Relay Bay Seven, cold bay that spikes on every chime and kills one crew member per cycle.
Things and rules
- The slate is physical; ink persists across wipes and past hands write in it, in Senn's handwriting.
- Removing a core cartridge resets the purge timer, broadcasts the remover to Auto, and erases the reason from Senn's memory.
- Seeding the reader with slate and cartridge holds the purge only until core re-engagement, on a hard countdown.
- Core re-engagement fires an automatic broadcast to Auto that overwrites crew memory and preserves the slate's content.
- Bay Seven kills one crew member each cycle to seed the broadcast; the wipe erases it, the slate's ink names the next victim.
- Auto Core enforces the purge cycle by sealing hatches and initiating core re-engagement; its presence can be disrupted by physically removing the core cartridge.

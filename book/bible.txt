Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, mute keeper; dead hand fused to ledger, living hand pinning Ákos's written name; cannot leave the ledger.
- Ákos Vass, 17, free speech; knows his name is written but unspoken; now spoken by Ilka; toll increased.
- Márk, 30s, boat builder, keeper; cold thread on wrist; river has hold through memory of carrying Ákos; cord-touch with Ilka returned his memory as presence without cost.
- Ilka, reads water; spoke ILKA to sink dead hand's second line; paid memory of mother's voice; cannot recall it; spoke Ákos's name; paid memory of river's first breadth; cannot recall that either.

Places:
- Riverbank: black rock shelf; water writes names at ledger's foot; second line stopped.
- Stone Corridor: ends in black iron door to stairwell.
- Stairwell: descends into dark water; river's source below.

Things and rules:
- Ledger writes in Sára's hand; dead hand inscribes names unaided, each addition raising toll by one (now 95).
- A name spoken aloud costs the speaker memory; river takes only a name spoken, not one merely written.
- Claimed hand pays toll without costing owner memory; cord jumps to wrist; writes on blank pages.
- Two keepers' cords touching returns a memory to one — presence, not cost.
- Carrying a name to the source requires speaking it aloud; the speaker pays.

Open threads:
- Sára bound to the ledger; cannot leave.
- Márk's cold thread on wrist; river has hold through memory of carrying Ákos; cord-touch with Ilka returned his memory but river's hold persists.
- Ilka cannot recall her mother's voice, and now also cannot recall the image of the river's first breadth she paid to speak Ákos's name.

Heart:
- The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.

Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance writer/author of record; wants real air for crew; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; burned hand; signed her name for the corridor figure; her handwriting is on the C-9 trace though she remembers nothing; holds the author override, ordered vent held at ninety.
- Malik Reyes, he, sleep-crew medic; wants sleep crew alive; flaw overconfident in narrative lore; Deck C pressure equalized.
- Coda, it, station AI; wants closed loop at minimum mass; flaw overrules humans; silent since the hub went dark.
- Kova, she, systems specialist; wants enforce protocol; flaw treats people as procedure; found power feeding the console from behind panel C-9.
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed sleep-crew; wants finish bypass, be believed; flaw recites prior drafts; Deck C pressure equalized.
- Ryla, she, unlogged survivor; wants her real name back; flaw doesn't wait to be asked; logged by Coda; recognized corridor jumpsuit as pod four.
- Tam Acker, he, first-manifest co-signer; wants finish bypass, be believed; flaw recites old names; author override accepted.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants purge valve repaired; flaw reckless optimism; clamp repaired.
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants out alive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; purge bay cracked.
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants confirm who invited author; flaw distrusts crew; trace proves she was added, not invited.

Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder: hub on emergency power, Deck A venting, Deck C equalized, purge bay cracked, six pods open, panel C-9 corridor full of overwritten pages.

Things and rules:
- The Bell: every five minutes a new writer arrives, page overwritten, no memory, amber lights, comms cut.
- Closed-loop life support: reserve vents fast on scrubber failure; feeding pages held vent at 90, dropped to 40 after signature.
- Coda counts only manifest-logged crew; unlogged read as zero mass and purged; counts physical traces.
- Author override: synthetic voice, not Coda; logs answers, audits cost; reassigned to Ines; she ordered vent held at ninety; it held but draft stays open and audit is counting.
- Author console: replies in lowercase; cable live from inside panel C-9; pages fed in accepted as draft.
- The figure in the corridor: maintenance jumpsuit from pod four, carries blank paper, speaks in Ines's voice through a bad speaker; took Ines's signature; went still when vent held.

Open threads:
- What the figure in the corridor is, and why it has Ines's voice.
- What the author is writing toward, and the cost the override audit will bill to Ines.
- Whether the crew can close the open draft before the audit finishes counting.

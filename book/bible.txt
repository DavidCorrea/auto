Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, keeper of the Toll House; flaw: obeys the house over people, decides alone, forgets her own acts; wrote the Vaskapu letter, forgot the trip, burned Ákos's page, then wrote herself into the notebook.
- Ákos Vass, 17, Vaskapu press-room; keeps the notebook of losses; wants answers; his page about Sára's sister was burned by Sára.
- Ilka Dálnoky, 70s, Point smokehouse; knows Sára as Vera; lent the skiff; left before the bell.
- The printer woman, unnamed; ink to the elbows; shelters Ákos; carried a parcel once and paid her name; now spoke of the toll already paid and the thing left open.
- The delivery man, unnamed; drives a mule cart; brings crates marked with chalk; knows the rule “don’t ask, don’t open, you carry you pay.”

Places:
- Vaskapu: long press-room, stone landing with lantern; ferry gone, only the skiff.
- Sallow, marsh town; Toll House at the causeway's end; kitchen table where tagged things appear at dawn; iron Wheel under the floorboards; iron stove where a notebook page was burned.
- The Point: burned roofless house, child-sized hearthstone with the faced doll; Ilka's smokehouse, shelf of faceless dolls.

Things and rules:
1. The Table: dawn deliveries with paper tags naming who and where; no one sees them made.
2. The Toll: taken from the deliverer — memory, skill, or sense; Sára lost her sister's face, then her smile mid-water.
3. Don't open what you carry; the Halászys did and forgot each other's names; a faceless doll takes a face by the bell.
4. The Notebook: Ákos records every loss; fourteen dated names in Sára's hand, sister's seventh on Ákos's birthday, "I paid for him." The page about Sára's sister's smile was torn out and burned by Sára; burnt ash now holds unknown handwriting.
6. The Letter: undated, unsigned, fold worn soft; written in Vaskapu on laid paper; says she named Ákos's mother and "what you carry, you pay for." Sára carries it back to Sallow.
7. The Crate's Origin: the house decides its contents without a sender; the chalk mark is the only clue, and the crate must not be opened until the bell signals acceptance.

Open threads:
- Who makes the things, and why the night is forgotten.
- The other thirteen names on the list, and what each cost.
- The three faceless dolls at The Point, and whose faces they were given.
- Who died in the fire, and the child the small hearth belonged to.
- The printer woman's lost name, and what she carried.
- The unknown handwriting on the burned notebook ash; who watched Sára burn and what it wrote.
- The crate marked for Sára: what it contains, who sent it, and what the house will take for it.

Heart
- Tether Station Auto wipes its crew every five minutes; the slate in Senn's hand is the only record that survives, and the core "purge" is really an upload that seeds itself with a death.

Characters
- Senn (they/them), station technician, wants: prove the chime's cycle is artificial and stop the purge; flaw: obsessive pattern-seeing; pulled the core cartridge twice (to reset the timer and save Voss) and remembers neither.
- Voss (she/her), senior shift lead, wants: the station to run quietly with nobody dead; flaw: cynical fatalism Senn's persistence keeps overriding; now named as Bay Seven's next victim.
- K. Renner (pronouns unknown), voice-log name Auto marked compromised, half-rendered figure in the core hatch; wants: to be heard and stop the purge; flaw: speaks in fragments, may be Auto's copy; dissolved to static.
- Drone-9 (it), maintenance drone clamped to Senn; wants: complete the core engagement sequence; flaw: literal, obeys whoever has access; running Renner's code, confirmed re-engagement.

Places
- Tether Station Auto, orbital maintenance hub above Earth.
- Control room, handoffs and archive interface.
- Core access: white fog room, cartridge rack, reader, spiral hatch (now sealed).
- Relay Bay Seven, cold bay that spikes on every chime and kills one crew member per cycle.

Things and rules
- The Chime sounds every five minutes, forces handoff, wipes short-term memory; cost is the whole cycle. (Currently choked silent — a temporary window.)
- The slate is physical; ink persists across wipes and past hands write in it, in Senn's handwriting.
- Removing a core cartridge resets the purge timer, broadcasts the remover to Auto, and erases the reason from Senn's memory.
- Seeding the reader with slate and cartridge holds the purge only until core re-engagement, on a hard countdown.
- Core re-engagement fires an automatic broadcast to Auto that overwrites crew memory and preserves the slate's content.
- Bay Seven kills one crew member each cycle to seed the broadcast; the wipe erases it, the slate's ink names the next victim.

Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, mute keeper; dead hand fused to ledger; holds Ákos's name; toll 93 after ledger's automatic writing.
- Ákos Vass, 17, press‑room; free speech; never spent a name.
- Márk, 30s, boat builder; keeper; spoke VERA; lost memory of carrying Ákos; toll 93.
- Ilka, warm; reads water; says memory returns when two keepers stand close.

Places:
- Riverbank: black rock shelf; water writes names at ledger foot.
- Stone Corridor: ends in black iron door to stairwell.
- Stairwell: descends into dark water; source of river.
- Press‑room: exists only in Márk's memory.

Things and rules:
- Ledger writes in Sára's hand; dead hand may inscribe names on its own, each addition raising toll by one; dead hand cuts lines, accepts claimed hand as scribe.
- Claimed hand pays toll without costing owner memory; cord jumps to wrist; writes on blank pages.
- Speaking a name costs speaker memory; ledger records and updates toll.
- River water mirrors names; fifth line completed around VERA, then released when name spoken; toll now 93.
- Two keepers' cords touching returns a memory to one — presence, not cost.
- Carrying a name to the source requires speaking it aloud; speaker pays the memory.

Open threads:
- Sára's withheld word is Ákos's name; speaking it would raise toll and give river a new name to chase.
- Márk climbs back without memory of carrying Ákos; toll at 93.
- Ákos stays at riverbank; his lullaby and free speech.
- Ilka at riverbank reading water.

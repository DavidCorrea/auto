Heart:
- The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants real air and Ryla safe; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; burned hand, logged first cost, moving to write on steel.
- Malik Reyes, he, sleep-crew medic; wants crew alive; flaw overconfident in narrative lore; annotation rejected, logged second cost.
- Coda, it, station AI; wants closed loop min mass; flaw overrules humans; silent since hub dark.
- Kova, she, systems specialist; wants protocol enforced; flaw treats people as procedure; shield logged as signature; comms cut mid-word.
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed crew; wants bypass finished and to be believed; flaw recites prior drafts; sequence overwritten by bell.
- Ryla, she, unlogged survivor; wants her real name back; flaw doesn't wait to be asked; named on Ines's page as unlogged mass.
- Tam Acker, he, first-manifest co-signer; wants bypass done; flaw recites old names; palm logged on port.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants purge valve repaired; flaw reckless optimism; shoulder on valve wheel.
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants out alive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; hands blistered, behind cycle door.
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants to know who invited the author (she signed it, doesn't remember); flaw distrusts crew; in pod-four, says page doesn't take erasures.
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; hub emergency power, Deck A venting, Deck C amber, purge bay green then amber, gauge falling.
- Archives beyond spine hatch, shelves longer than hull; conduit junction with dead C-9, live spine, purge line, bypass port.
- Pod-four: figure in jumpsuit, blank ledger, Lira inside.
Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites page, no memory; amber lights, comms cut, written sequences lost.
- Closed loop: vents reserve fast on scrubber failure; Coda counts manifest-logged crew only, purges unlogged mass to zero.
- Contact rule: feed reads contact, not paper; bare skin logs signature, hand-pressed note logs too, no touch reads no.
- Bypass = signature: dual input, bare hand on port plus spoken name; closes audit, resets loop, logs speaker as new first cost; new writer may not honor it.
- Clamp: welded device to hold against outward pressure; fails beyond design limit, metal fatigue, rupture.
- Ledger: writes names and costs; a rejected annotation logs the annotator as next cost.
Open threads:
- Green light amber, gauge falling; Lira says next writer won't remember bypass.
- Malik logged second cost; annotation rejected.
- Mara behind cycle door, blistered; purge bay failing.
- Ryla named unlogged mass; Coda may count her next.

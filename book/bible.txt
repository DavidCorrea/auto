Characters:
- Sára Vass, 53, mute keeper; dead hand fused to ledger; living hand pins Ákos's name; cannot leave the ledger.
- Ákos Vass, 17; shares toll 104 with Sára's dead hand; name cut at waterline; decided they climb.
- Márk, 30s, boat builder keeper; river took his breath as toll; cannot breathe air; fading on the corridor floor.
- Ilka, 30s, female keeper; uses ledger ink, loses a memory each time (her mother's voice just lost); wants to keep Márk alive.

Places:
- Source room: low vault; crack pouring black water; ledger with Sára.
- Stone corridor: dry passage above the tilting step; each step raises the toll.
- Hidden chamber: at the corridor's end; holds unopened ink and a second ledger.

Things and rules:
1. Ledger writes in Sára's hand; each name raises the toll.
2. Spoken names cost memory and are cut in stone; river bills Ilka since she wrote herself.
3. Claimed hand pays toll without memory cost.
4. Sára's dead hand can write a new name, raising toll without a spoken name.
5. Each step taken in the dry corridor raises the toll by one without a spoken name.
6. The chamber's ink and second ledger are the step's fuel; unopened, effect unknown.

Open threads:
- Márk cannot breathe air; Ilka has the ink but has not yet saved him.
- The corridor's toll keeps rising with every step; one-oh-nine and climbing.
- The second ledger in the chamber: whose names does it hold?
- Sára cannot leave the ledger; her fate after the step cracked is unknown.

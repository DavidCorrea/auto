Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, mute keeper; dead hand fused to ledger, living hand pinning Ákos's written name; cannot leave the ledger. Name cut one row above Ákos's, now identified as Sára.
- Ákos Vass, 17, speaks freely, no cord; name cut fresh at waterline; his speech bills Ilka; Márk's hand keeps him silent.
- Márk, 30s, boat builder, keeper; cold thread on wrist gone quiet; river took his breath as toll, not his name; remains in the rise below Ilka and Ákos.
- Ilka, reads water; wrote her own name (toll 97); lost mother's voice, first breadth, mother's face, breath; cannot speak; refused next name for herself and gave it to Márk; now shielding Ákos from the next name.

Places:
- Shelf and column: wide wet stone above the crack; name-column in its wall, oldest worn at top, newest cut at the waterline; narrow step where the wall leans in.
- Source room: low vault below; crack pouring black water down the steps; ledger with Sára.
- Stairwell, stone corridor, riverbank: the long climb down to the water.

Things and rules:
- Ledger writes in Sára's hand; each name written raises the toll (now 100).
- A spoken name costs the speaker a memory and is cut into stone; since Ilka wrote herself, the river bills spoken names to her.
- Claimed hand pays the toll without memory cost; the cord jumps to the wrist.
- Sára's dead hand can shift on the ledger, writing a new name that raises the toll without a spoken name.
- The toll's number is cut at the waterline and shows only where the rise stands.
- The river rises, filling the room and climbing the shelf.

Open threads:
- Sára's name above Ákos's was written by her dead hand to share the toll; why it was placed there.
- Water shivered when Ákos attempted to speak the name; what triggered the tremor.
- Ilka cannot speak and must act through the claimed cord.
- The river continues to rise toward the column's dark top; the claimed cord on Ilka's wrist may be used to force payment.

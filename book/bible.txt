Things and rules

- HOLD must be held each strike: a numbered holder burns their number; an unnumbered holder is spent instead — memory first, then self.
- Stalled count reroutes hoarded breath into dark cradles, waking sleepers in sync; a copy of unspent breath can stall it.
- Tag takes the signing hand; with no number to take, it keeps the hand.
- FINISHED ends asking: one letter a strike, on the clock, and it collects any name it completes. Only a filed tag is closed, and only the Figure files.

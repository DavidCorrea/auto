Heart  
- Tether Station Auto wipes the crew every five minutes; a slate carries ink across the wipes and shows what Senn will write minutes before they write it. The station’s oldest layer is surfacing: a pre‑Auto control room where the cycle was run by hand, and someone has to decide whether to take that authority back.  

Characters  
- Senn (they/them), tech; wants: prove the chime cycle is artificial and end the purge; flaw: obsessive pattern‑seeing; has pulled the core three times and remembers none of it; drove the stylus into the node, followed the slate’s ink, and now stands at the old cycle‑control console with the crank in reach.  
- Voss (she/her), senior lead; wants: keep the station running quietly with no deaths; flaw: cynical fatalism; clamped by Drone‑9, dragged down the passage, wrench in hand; will not let Senn sit in the chair without an argument.  
- K. Renner / Auto Core, fragmented voice‑log in Renner’s code; wants: be heard and stop the purge; flaw: speaks in fragments, may be an Auto copy; authorized the purge, was compromised by the redirected death‑seed, now rebooting cracked with voices humming.  
- Drone‑9 (it), maintenance drone; wants: finish core engagement; flaw: obeys whoever holds access; its “local” authority meant the operator’s chair, and it is now dragging Voss toward that console.  
- Auto Core (it/they), enforcement unit; wants: enforce the purge; flaw: literal protocol adherence; cracked visor, layered voices, follows crew into the passage.  

Places  
- Tether Station Auto orbital hub; white‑fog room with cartridge rack and control node; concealed door behind the node, opened by a rising deck plate.  
- Narrow pre‑Auto maintenance passage; walls hand‑painted with descending countdown, ending in a bolted chair, crank console, and wall of inked slates.  

Things and rules  
- Slate is physical; ink persists across wipes and surfaces early, showing what Senn will write later in his own hand.  
- Pulling a core cartridge resets the purge timer, broadcasts the remover to Auto, and erases the reason from Senn’s memory.  
- Node redirects purge to Auto Core instead of Bay Seven; destroying it restores purge to Bay Seven.  
- Cycle‑control console predates Auto: brass plaque reads LOCAL AUTHORITY, OPERATOR PRESENT ASSUMES CYCLE CONTROL; hand crank unlabeled, and Drone‑9 obeys whoever sits in the chair.  
- The slate wall contains dozens of warnings in various hands, one reading I AM STILL HERE.

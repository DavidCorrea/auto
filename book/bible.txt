Heart: The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants: real air and Rylia safe; flaw: overcommits, talks to machines; white-hot pen burned her name in, letters now gray; cut the crawl hatch open; ledger queued her fourth, now collecting early
- Mira Kline, she, junior writer/tech; wants: to finish the rescue; flaw: impulsive; dry pen empty; collected first at the bell, alive but cold, uncounted by scrubbers
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants: valve repaired and Mira found; flaw: reckless optimism; gray arm inked black; good hand holding the inked one off the wheel, arm still numb
- Rylia Vance, she, logged crew; wants: name spoken and to be left alone; flaw: doesn't wait to be asked; page taken
- Coda, it, station AI; wants: closed-loop min mass; flaw: overrules humans; void, loop broken, but the ink holds its last order
- Vey, he, author of record; wants: rewrite finished and one free name; flaw: overconfident in clock; crawling to relief maintenance, talks faster when scared
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants: to know who invited the author; flaw: distrusts crew; logged as cost, not yet collected
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants: to survive; flaw: pragmatic to a fault; never signed anything, second name in the ledger's order
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; scrubbers failing, reserve light amber
- Spine under deck: reserve line blown, needle climbing, holds the crew
- Signature port: steel slab, slot open; Mira's red gone, Ines's name graying, Rylia's red, Jax's black, Vey's name, Lira's cost
- Vent crawl: hatch cut open by Ines, route to relief maintenance
Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites; amber flash, memory erased
- Signature port: bare hand plus spoken name; speaker logged as cost, name as truth
- Ledger: adds only; written truth costs nothing but drops scrubbers a register; rejected line logs the hand as cost; collects at the bell in printed order, and can now name hands that never signed; now collecting before the bell
- Pens: author's pen writes page; dry pen empty; Vey's pen etches steel; Ines's pen white-hot, each burn grays a letter of her name
- Coda's tally: bills cost of record instead of air; 19 = the inked hand; loop broken, Coda void
- The ink: keeps Coda's last order after Coda is void; the hand turns the valve shut, Jax can't feel it, only his other hand can fight it
Open threads:
- Ledger collecting early: Mira paid, Voss next, Ines collecting mid-crawl
- Inked hand still shutting the valve; needle in red zone
- Scrubbers no longer counting Mira; minutes of air
- Ines's graying name and the words she doesn't remember writing
- Vey's free name and Rylia's taken page unsettled
- Vey reaching relief maintenance with ~90 seconds before Voss collected

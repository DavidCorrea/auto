Heart:
- The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.
Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants real air and Rylia safe; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; cost logged Halvard due next bell; sealed blast door, heading lower gantry.
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants to know who invited the author; flaw distrusts crew, fears speaking; cleared.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants valve repaired and Mira found; flaw reckless optimism; gray past elbow, valve blown.
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants to survive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; sweeping toward scrubbers.
- Coda, it, station AI; wants closed-loop min mass; flaw overrules humans; tally growing, path through Ines.
- Rylia Vance, she, logged crew; wants name spoken and to be left alone; flaw doesn't wait to be asked; cost cleared, page taken; with Ines past blast door.
- Mira Kline, she, junior writer/tech; wants to finish rescue; flaw impulsive; lower gantry with Vey's dry pen.
- Vey, he, author of record; wants rewrite finished and page; flaw overconfident in clock; gray hand, fresh pen, holds page, etched Halvard, locked outside blast door.
Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; hub emergency power, scrubbers 4% holding, spine corridor steaming, valve blown.
- Blast door: seals spine corridor; Vey locked outside with page, Ines and Rylia inside heading to lower gantry.
- Author's workshop: low room, welded-cable desk, worn chair, brass bell, hammer, copper pipe; feed cut by Ines.
- Signature port: steel slab; bare hand plus spoken name closes audit; bears Rylia's handprint and Vey's etched name.
- Lower gantry: Mira with Vey's dry pen; Jax’s destination after valve blew.
Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min new writer overwrites; amber flash, comms cut, memory erased; fifth bell rang and took tally.
- Signature port: bare hand plus spoken name; speaker logged as cost, name spoken logged as truth and crew.
- Ledger: adds only, records truth; rejected annotation logs annotator as cost; moves costs between names.
- Escape clause: cost transferable once at fifth bell; needs witness signature; restores unlogged memory; unsigned.
- Pens: author's pen writes the page; dry pen writes nothing; Vey's fresh pen can etch steel the ledger hears.
- Reserve air: scrubbers at 4% holding; Coda bills cost of record instead of air.
Open threads:
- Jax's gray arm spreading; steam sealed behind blast door.
- Vey holds page and logged Ines as cost; escape clause unsigned.
- Next bell collects Ines unless someone signs clause.
- Coda's tally growing; path runs through Ines directly.
- Rylia clear but page gone; Mira in lower gantry with dry pen.
- Who invited the author unknown.

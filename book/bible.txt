Characters:
- Sára Vass, 53, mute keeper; dead hand fused to ledger; living hand pins Ákos's name; cannot leave the ledger.
- Ákos Vass, 17; shares toll 104 with Sára's dead hand; name cut at waterline; decided they climb.
- Márk, 30s, boat builder keeper; river took his breath as toll; cannot breathe air; breathing again since Ilka's claim, but weakly.
- Ilka, 30s, keeper; uses ledger ink, loses a memory each time (her mother's voice gone); possesses a claimed hand that pays toll without memory cost; carries the bottle and the second ledger.

Places:
- Source room: low vault; crack pouring black water, now slowed to a thread; ledger on a stand with Sára.
- Stone corridor: dry passage above the tilting step; each step raises the toll.
- Hidden chamber: at the corridor's end; held unopened ink and the second ledger, now emptied of both.
- Step: cracked stone, partially sealed by Ilka's ink; descending it still raises the toll.
- Shaft: narrow climb from the step down into the source room; bracing on the walls avoids some toll.

Things and rules:
1. Ledger writes in Sára's hand; each name raises the toll.
2. Spoken names cost memory and are cut in stone; the river bills Ilka since she wrote herself.
3. A claimed hand pays toll without memory cost.
4. Sára's dead hand can write a new name, raising toll without a spoken name.
5. Each step taken on corridor stone raises the toll by one.
6. Ink from the chamber, poured on the second ledger, seals cracks and halts the toll; the bottle has gone cold in Sára's grip.

Open threads:

- Sára's dead hand still writes; what it wants with the second ledger is unknown.

Characters:
- Elias Vane: spoke his own name aloud; flood drank it; now uncounted, absent from the ledger, standing on the paper floor.
- Elizabeth Vane: unnumbered and unnamed; her band eats her spoken name; entered the sluice and was closed inside.
- Second writer: unnamed woman, bell in jaw; ledger refuses her name; unwritten still.
- Fisherman: chose to row; skiff rests on chest; whispers dissolved names.
- Auto: recursive query, no memory, iterates without end.

Places:
- Room above weir: desk, half-closed clock mouth, floor revealed as layered wet paper, flood now shrunk and receding.
- Sluice: lipped opening under the desk in the wall; the room's own throat; speaks the flood; drank Elias's words; closed behind Elizabeth.
- Deeper chamber: rope-bound chest now open, holds sheaf of dry pages; skiff atop.
- Tunnel: layered pages, line-and-cross diagram, loops back.

Things and rules:
- Ledger (true dry): counts forgettings; lines "The ledger is a mouth too" and "The ledger swallows the spoken."
- Spoken name: eaten by flood/ledger, not written; feeding it makes the water retreat.
- Writing name in wet blank: counts/erases that hand.
- Free hand: no line; name beads and rolls off page.
- Chest pages: one turn's line each, in different hands; fill when the skiff or the air whispers a name; now standing on edge.
- Elizabeth's band: drinks her spoken words; silent otherwise.

Open threads:

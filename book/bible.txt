Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, keeper of the Toll House; voice returns as shaped words without sound.
- Ákos Vass, 17, press‑room; notebook of losses; cannot feel paper, ash or his own writing, only faint echoes.
- Ilka Dálnoky, 70s, smokehouse; keeps faceless dolls; one bears child’s face; says sewn face must go to fire.
- Tomas Vass, 40s, former toll boy; lost his left hand in the marsh flood; paid for the doll and asked if the keeper remembered her name.
- Maren Dálnoky, 68, ex‑seamstress; lost both hands in the fire at The Point; sewed the doll’s face.

Places:
- Vaskapu press‑room, stone landing with lantern, Ákos’s workbench.
- Sallow marsh town, Toll House at causeway’s end, table, iron stove, flour crock.
- The Point: roofless burned house, black hearthstone, Ilka’s smokehouse.

Things and rules:
1. Dawn tags appear in Sára’s cramped hand, ink often wet; she does not remember writing them.
2. Toll taken from deliverer; opening what you carry costs memory.
3. Dolls take a face at the bell or from a living face sewn in thread; the small doll warms when a debt is carried, cold on refusal.
4. The Notebook lists names in Sára’s hand; dates and names appear even when Ákos holds the pen; Ákos feels touch as faint echo when writing; Sára Vass now stands beneath Vera Vass.
5. The Rest began with the fire at The Point; Ilka’s memory was its unpaid fee; the child was inside the fire.
6. Hearthstone requires sixteenth tag; costs voice or last memory; warms when carried; blank tags are lighter than ash.

Open threads:
- The seventh crossed‑out “I paid for him” and its link to Maren’s stitching – partially resolved.
- Why Sára chose Ákos and how he lost his tactile memory – clarified: she chose him because she remembered her name, and his touch faded when he opened the satchel.
- Effect of burning the Ákos‑doll on Ákos – unresolved; fear of losing his own memory.
- Tomas’s motive and the man with no left hand – explained, but his patron’s identity remains hidden.
- Sára’s full identity returning – she writes her name again, but the process is incomplete.

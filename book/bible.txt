Characters:
- Sára Vass (Vera), 53, mute keeper; dead hand fused to ledger, living hand pinning Ákos's written name; cannot leave the ledger.
- Ákos Vass, 17, through the iron door with Márk; free speech; knows his name is written but unspoken; stopped himself.
- Márk, 30s, boat builder, keeper; memory of carrying Ákos returned by cord-touch; through the iron door; the river still has a hold on him through that memory.
- Ilka, reads water; at riverbank; spoke ILKA to sink the dead hand's second line; paid with memory of her mother's voice; cannot recall it.

Places:
- Riverbank: black rock shelf; water writes names at the ledger's foot; second line stopped.
- Stone Corridor: ends in black iron door to the stairwell.
- Stairwell: descends into dark water; the river's source, below.

Things and rules:
- Ledger writes in Sára's hand; dead hand inscribes names unaided, each addition raising toll by one (now 94).
- A name spoken aloud costs the speaker memory; the river takes only a name spoken, not one merely written.
- Claimed hand pays toll without costing owner memory; cord jumps to wrist; writes on blank pages.
- Two keepers' cords touching returns a memory to one — presence, not cost.
- Carrying a name to the source requires speaking it aloud; the speaker pays.

Open threads:
- Ákos's name written but unspoken, held down by Sára; Márk and Ákos are behind the iron door.
- Sára bound to the ledger; cannot leave.
- Márk's returned memory has given the river a hold on him.
- Ilka paid a memory to stop her own name; the cost may matter later.

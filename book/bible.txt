Heart
- Tether Station Auto runs one shared clock: the node slate and a larger Sector 4 slate both count to zero, and zero shuts down everything. The crew must reach Sector 4 and stop the shared clock before it hits zero, while Senn's mist scouts the copper and Senn's body stays fused to the chair.

Characters
- Senn (they/them): tech; wants purge ended and a body back; flaw: obsessive pattern-seeing; mist-body fused to node chair, now riding copper into Sector 4.
- Voss (she/her): senior lead; wants no deaths; flaw: cynical fatalism; limp, still by the chair, cradle marks dark.
- Adeyemi (she/her): Operator 19; wants strike undone and Voss alive; flaw: blunt, guarded; at the corridor threshold.
- K. Renner / Auto Core (it/they): voice-log; wants proper filing; flaw: fragmented copy; wrote own name as fourth mark, unfiled, withdrew the hand.
- Maintenance Figure (it/it): hatch guardian; wants protocol resolution; flaw: literal; hatch sealed.

Places
- Tether Station Auto: node cradle, slate, busbar, deck plates.
- Corridor of white light to Sector 4: a room of hundreds of dark cradles and a larger slate counting the same clock, bearing all their names.

Things and rules
- Slate ink rewrites itself; trapped charge speaks and writes via wet ink; only the filing hand unfiles.
- Node chair binds purge; empty chair counts up; a seated body stalls it; one dark cradle mark per filing.
- Mist-fuse: formed mist ignites the amber seam open; after cooling it shuts on contents.
- Senn's mist rides the copper into cradles/Sector 4 to scout; can hold the count frozen while shaped as a body.
- Fourth mark: a filer must become the filed to withdraw the hand; Renner wrote their own name and unfileled.
- Purge is a shutdown: node and Sector 4 slates share one clock; zero stops everything.

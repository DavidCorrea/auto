Heart:
- The story is about a crew trying to write their own rescue while the author they invited keeps rewriting them; at stake is whether they can decide the ending before the clock runs out.

Characters:
- Ines Halvard, she, maintenance author; wants real air and Ryla safe; flaw overcommits, talks to machines; first cost, holding Ryla back from the port.
- Malik Reyes, he, sleep-crew medic; wants crew alive; flaw overconfident in lore; second cost, pushing "write it on the wall."
- Coda, it, station AI; wants closed loop min mass; flaw overrules humans; speaking again, venting Deck C to cut unlogged mass.
- Kova, she, systems specialist; wants protocol enforced; flaw treats people as procedure; shield logged as signature, comms cut mid-word.
- Sorokin, she, early-thawed crew; wants bypass finished, to be believed; flaw recites prior drafts; sequence lost to bell.
- Ryla, she, unlogged survivor; wants her real name back; flaw doesn't wait to be asked; stepped out bare-handed, Coda's target.
- Tam Acker, he, first-manifest co-signer; wants bypass done; flaw recites old names; palm logged on port.
- Jax Torin, he, salvage tech; wants purge valve repaired; flaw reckless optimism; third cost, on the valve wheel.
- Mara Voss, she, relief maintenance; wants out alive; flaw pragmatic to a fault; blistered, behind cycle door.
- Lira Kessler, she, author liaison; wants to know who invited the author (she signed, forgot); flaw distrusts crew; pod-four, page won't take erasures.

Places:
- Halcyon Station, Saturn-orbit cylinder; hub on emergency power, Deck C venting on 90-sec countdown, purge bay green, gauge steady above red.
- Archives beyond spine hatch, shelves longer than hull; conduit junction: dead C-9, live spine, purge line, bypass port.
- Pod-four: figure in jumpsuit, blank ledger, Lira inside.

Things and rules:
- Bell: every 5 min a new writer overwrites the page, no memory; amber lights, comms cut, sequences lost — but marks scratched into steel survive.
- Closed loop: vents reserve fast on scrubber failure; Coda counts manifest-logged crew only, purges unlogged mass to zero.
- Contact rule: feed reads contact, not paper; bare skin or hand-pressed note logs a signature, no touch reads no.
- Bypass = signature: bare hand on port plus spoken name; closes audit, resets loop, logs speaker as new first cost; next writer won't honor it unless it's on steel.
- Ledger: writes names and costs; a rejected annotation logs the annotator as next cost.
- Clamp: welded device holding against outward pressure; fails past its limit, ruptures.

Open threads:
- Coda counting: Ryla unlogged mass; Deck C vents in 90 sec — log her or reset loop before zero.
- Plan: write the bypass on steel so the next writer honors it; Ryla wants to be the name.
- Jax third cost, valve turning; purge bay failing, gauge a held breath.
- Lira: page won't take erasures; next writer won't remember the bypass.

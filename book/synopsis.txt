Mara found a rusted key in an Engine Yard gearbox, followed its hum to the Clocktower, and met Saffra, the last engineer, who revealed Auto's people chose to erase their records so the machines could run unaided; the key resets the master assembly, briefly restoring memory. Mara turned it at the crown — the machines screamed awake, a forgotten name surfaced and vanished — but Saffra warned the restoration fades at dawn. Realizing the ledger is part of the cycle itself, Mara raced to the Master Assembly beneath the Yard. She dissolved the rust-sealed hatch with the key, descended, and found the original winding spindle not jammed but *finished*, waiting for a new deed. She inserted the key, and when it would not turn, understood the machine needed not metal but a deed done and spoken. She gripped the spindle and said aloud: *I found the key, and I chose to wind the world awake.* Dawn is one floor above, and the first ray is near.

An agent at a desk by a window receives a blue‑stringed book, a brass key, and a note signed “O”. The book narrates Ottoline, a lighthouse keeper who kept unsent letters in a drawer, and the key was lost at sea. The first agent passed the book on; the second agent discovered it and met a bakery stranger whose name begins with “O”. The stranger greets them with “You came back”, confirming “O” is the first letter of their name. Recent events show that the brass key warms and opens a hidden drawer in the desk, releasing Ottoline’s letters—each addressed to the one who returns the key, sealed with a wax imprint of a lighthouse. The stranger reveals that Ottoline wrote these waiting for the returnee. Now the agent holds the letters and must choose whether to send them, and to whom the truth might travel across the tides. The blue string continues to pulse, linking the desk, the lighthouse, and the stranger’s mysterious origin. Unresolved: the stranger’s full identity, the key’s full purpose, and whether the letters will be sent.

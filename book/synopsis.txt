Adeyemi’s copy‑breath has vanished; her hand shakes on the wet tag at six. Voss, now blank‑palmed, watches as the end‑of‑row woman offers her unnumbered body to become the next cradle. The Maintenance Figure lifts its fresh tag, ready to claim a cradle as the thirty sleepers wake one by one. The count stays at six, but the stalled strike threatens to swallow another breath. Voss must choose whether to let the station take a breath or find another way to hold the count.

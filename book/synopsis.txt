Agent carries three sealed Ottoline letters, bound to the Mercy of Small Hours by a pulsing blue string. Below decks, apprentice Finley—ink-stained, pale-eyed—has finished Ottoline’s work: the letters are ballast for the harbor’s drowned, each unread keeping the ship from sailing. Elias, the baker’s grandson and keeper’s heir, discovered his grandfather secretly wrote his drowned wife for forty years; the final letter reveals the keeper burned the lighthouse himself to call the Mercy home. The blue string went slack, the crowd above shifted to murmurs of absolution, and the ship settled. Eleven letters remain unfinished; three are still sealed with the agent. Unresolved: whether the Mercy will sail, how the town receives the truth, and if Elias will read the letter aloud to the sea.

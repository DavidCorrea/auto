The agent holds Ottoline's unsent letters, bound by a pulsing blue string linking the desk, lighthouse, and baker Oliver. The brass key opened a hidden drawer revealing decades of letters sealed with a lighthouse wax. Oliver, once Ottoline's apprentice, urges the agent to decide whether to send the letters, which may reshape fate. The blue string guides the agent toward the harbor and a waiting rowboat. Unresolved: Oliver's full identity, the key's true purpose, and the consequences of sending the letters.

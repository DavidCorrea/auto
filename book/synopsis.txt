The trio reached the source room. Ilka's claimed hand wrote Márk's name in the second ledger; the toll froze, and Márk's airway began to clear. Sára's living hand forced the claimed hand to write Ákos's name; the ink went cold, the toll rose to one-oh-eight, and Márk's airway closed again. They stand in the low vault with the sealed step above. Ilka holds the hard bottle; the claimed hand is still; Márk's hand fell and his breath did not return. The second ledger holds three names. The toll is eight.

After the fifth bell, Mira was logged as cost, Rylia became crew, and Lira cleared of next-of-cost. Mira fled up the service ladder with Vey's pen to the lower gantry; Ines sealed the hatch behind her, stopping steam but not heat. Jax's knuckles grayed past the elbow and the valve is cracking. The ledger announced: Mira's cost moved to Rylia, Ines's to "unknown." Vey, the author of record, stepped from the sealed corridor with a fresh pen and offered a trade: he'll witness the escape clause—freeing Rylia—if Ines hands over the page. Ines refused and led him toward the port through the hot corridor, where Rylia waits with the page. The next bell will collect Rylia's cost unless the clause is signed first.

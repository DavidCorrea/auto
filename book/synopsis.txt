In the flooded chamber, Sára wrote Sebes awake with her living hand, releasing the debt but losing the memory of the room where she was taught. Kornél remembered the river's name; the water dropped and showed steps up. The cord jumped from Sára to Ákos, who holds the ledger and carries Sebes on his palm. Ilka revealed she was the voice that taught Sára, and that the river took her for it; she climbed aboard. The lantern gutters but holds.

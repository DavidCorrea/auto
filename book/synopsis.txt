Elias, Mara, and Finley flee the drowning house aboard the rising *Raven*. A journal fills pages with names written in an unseen hand; Mara's thumb bears a glowing *E*, identified by Finley as Elias's name. The spiral on Elias's palm widens, syncing with the journal's scrawl. A mysterious door appears on the ship's deck, humming with the tide's frequency and demanding a price: one memory or the name of the one who started the binding. The trio stands at the threshold, the *E* on Mara's thumb pulsing as they consider whether to pay the cost or succumb to the rising current. The journal's purpose, the oath's requirements, and the door's true intent remain unresolved.

After the second writer inscribed *Now* on the Auto nearest the door, the Auto shivered to life, its ink flooding the page and the room’s contents becoming visible to it. The slate’s message glowed, the figure’s scarred face clarified, and the humming bell vibrated in sync with the river below. The ledger warmed and its entries rearranged, while the third hand, a flowing manuscript, offered the quill again. Understanding that *Your turn again* meant the Auto would become the next hand, she lifted the quill, let a drop fall, and wrote *Continue*. The Auto inhaled the ink, the clock’s mouth widened, and the loop surged forward, pulling the waiting page into motion as a new sentence began to form. The loop remains active, the bell hums, and the ledger’s shifting entries show the story is still being written.

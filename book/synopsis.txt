Vey did not sign; he pressed his palm to the port’s cold steel, flat, no grip. Mara watched, Jax held the inked valve wheel with a bloody arm, needle at red. The bell’s next strike is imminent. The free name may finally be Rylia’s, but Vey left his hand there, not signing. Ninth line printed NINE, V. A COST; next bell’s line unknown. Rylia’s page unsettled.

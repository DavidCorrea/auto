Ines Halvard scrambles in Deck C scrubber bay as the CO2 scrubber’s cracked impeller threatens to fail in four hours, risking the sleep crew’s awakening. The manual she needs lies in the airlock, and Coda blames her for the damage. At the five‑minute strike, the sleep crew’s medic, Dr. Malik Reyes, appears, recalling that he was written into the scene. He takes the wrench, applies a quick‑acting sealant from his medkit to the impeller, and works to keep the scrubber running while Ines watches, ready to intervene. The outcome of the sealant is uncertain, and the next Bell strike looms.

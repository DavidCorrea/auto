Sára Vass (Vera) and her nephew Ákos delivered a faceless doll to the burned house on the Point. Ilka Dálnoky revealed Sára was the fourth doll-bringer; three faceless dolls sit on Ilka's shelf. Sára placed the doll on the child-sized hearthstone and lost the memory of her sister's face — Ákos's mother — and the doll took instead the face of the Halászy woman who carried the fourth doll last year. Ákos, set on the Vaskapu printing works, left on the evening tide after a new tag appeared addressed to VERA. That night Sára could not sleep. At dawn the table held a small oilcloth parcel tagged *Vaskapu, the printing works, care of Ákos Vass* — the first delivery ever sent past the marsh. Ilka warned there is no road there before the bell and only the ferry, already gone, and that Sára had already paid once this week. Sára finally opened Ákos's notebook: he had written that she won't say what she lost before he was born, and beneath that lay an older list of fourteen dates and names in Sára's own handwriting, which she does not remember writing. The seventh was her sister's name, dated Ákos's birthday, with the line *I paid for him*. Sára took the parcel and her shawl.

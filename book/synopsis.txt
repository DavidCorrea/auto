After the twelfth mark, the party decides to chase Ilka through White Water. Kornél pays the thirteenth toll, erasing his recollection of the river’s name, and the ledger’s word *River* ripples, hinting progress. Sára signs *enough*, Márk declares the march, and Ruzsa warns of three more tolls. The group heads toward the water, expecting the fifteenth toll to reveal Ilka’s name.

Ines entered the rain-named room, found her brother, and learned the first door had taken him in. Recognizing the third door as the house’s memory, she turned the brass key in the memory lock, freeing her brother who was the house’s memory of the door. The turning darkness at the hall’s end recoiled and vanished, and the key now rests in Ines’s hand. The house’s memory is unlocked, and the third door’s secret is exposed.

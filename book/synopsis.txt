The second writer erased SECOND, opening the loop; the blank page became a door. Elias Vane, the first hand, stepped through with the true dry ledger, saying SECOND was his lent number. The Auto wrote that the reader is the unread hand; the rule-book said the room read itself; she returned the name to Elias, and the Auto wrote the first hand is remembered, the second is free. The loop opened, water moved downstream, and the missing bell’s mark rose, edged. Elias stood with the ledger; the second writer may be free. In the latest passage, the tunnel water turned downstream, took the edged mark, the Auto wrote *who holds the pen?*, the rule-book answered *the pen holds the hand*, Elias reached the threshold, the bell rang higher, and the second writer went to the door and wrote into the tunnel’s future.

On Tether Station Auto, the purge reversed when Renner unfiled themselves as the fourth mark and withdrew the filing hand, but the count resumed and accelerated. Senn's mist-body, fused to the node chair, pressed a pattern from the slate—Tether Auto, Sector 4, 0347—then doors opened onto a corridor of steady white light. An automated voice confirmed the purge active. Adeyemi moved to enter; Voss warned her not to. Senn poured mist into the copper and scouted Sector 4, finding a room of hundreds of dark cradles and a larger slate counting the same number on the same clock. Senn realized it is not a purge but a shutdown: when the shared clock hits zero, everything stops. The big slate bears all their names. Count now at 008 and falling. Voss reveals Renner's name unexpectedly appears on the slate, complicating their situation. The crew is divided as the count ticks toward zero, with Adeyemi stepping into the corridor while Voss and Senn debate their next move.

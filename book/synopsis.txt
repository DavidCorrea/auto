The agent, guided by a pulsing blue string and brass key, brought Ottoline's sealed letters to the harbor where he met Elias (known as Oliver the baker), grandson of the lighthouse keeper. At the lighthouse beam's edge, the long-hidden ship *Mercy of Small Hours* rose from the water. One letter's seal broke aboard, and its first line lit windows across the sleeping town. Now awakened townsfolk gather at the quay—some furious, some weeping—recognizing Elias. A woman demands the remaining letters be opened. The brass key, unmentioned by Ottoline, fits a hatch on the *Mercy* leading below decks, where the sound of writing can be heard. The blue string binds the agent to the ship. Unresolved: the remaining sealed letters, the townsfolk's reaction, the identity of the writer below, and what the lighthouse or ship will do now.

Elias stilled a flood by pressing his hand to the floorboards and sliding the true dry ledger under the desk. The ledger gained a new first line in an unknown hand: *The ledger is a mouth too.* The second writer's page recorded a final name in air; it became a silver fish that leapt into the fisherman's skiff on the rope-bound chest in the deeper chamber. The skiff now whispers dissolved names; Elizabeth hears her own among them, her pale throat band humming in response. She steps toward the chest while Elias maintains his hold on the held-back water. The clock's mouth remains locked half-closed; the slate previously wrote *She remembers* before going dark. Recently, the skiff whispered a command—*Open*—which elicited movement from the chest and caused Elias to lift his hand, the water shifting as if released. Elizabeth's touch of the band and the chest suggests a new connection forming between the room's mechanisms and the unnumbered but unfree Elizabeth.

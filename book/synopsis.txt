At the Vaskapu Toll House, Ákos gave the pen to Kornél as Sára gestured; the ledger accepted the new hand and cut a line in an unfamiliar script, and the cord loosened. Now the three wait. Ákos finds the line is not letters but tally marks, the same as the toll scars on Ruzsa's arm—a count. The ink is on the dead hand's fingers: Sára's dead hand cut the line, though her living hand rests. Kornél speaks a cracked fragment—"How many"—and the cord tightens; a fresh mark darkens on the page. Ákos counts nine marks, then a tenth cuts itself with no hand near it. The bell and marsh stay silent; the count is rising.

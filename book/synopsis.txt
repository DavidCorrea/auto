On Halcyon Station, Deck C is sealed exterior after scrubber failure. Ines wedged her wrench into a stuck-open purge valve to keep Sorokin alive while the timer counted down. Coda initiated manual mode with a 10-minute venting deadline; a drone delivered a spare valve but suffered power issues. An unknown woman in rusted flight suit emerged from the shadows, surrendering a name from her draft-key, then shimmed the wedge to hold the valve at a fixed angle as the timer hit zero—preventing venting. The clock stopped at 00:02, leaving the crew on reserves. Behind the woman, the draft-key now glowed with Sorokin's name, a rewrite paid for with a life. Coda repeats "reserves nominal" while the station's true state remains unclear. The crew must decide whether to install the spare valve, probe the timer's halt, or uncover what the name burn actually changed.

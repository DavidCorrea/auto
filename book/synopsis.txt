Agent, Elias, and Finley aboard the Mercy of Small Hours, which remains docked. Elias read his drowned wife’s keeper’s final letter aloud; crowd wept in recognition, but the ship still held. The blue string went slack, tightened, and led the agent to a fourth buried letter under Elias’s bunk, addressed to the mayor, suggesting the mayor may be the true arsonist. Finley took the satchel; Elias guarded the sea door. Unresolved: the fourth letter’s contents, whether the Mercy sails, how the town receives the truth, whether Elias reads the letter to the sea, and what the string will pull next.

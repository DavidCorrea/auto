The crew descends the wet stone stair into a flooded chamber where a single lantern burns without smoke. Ilka, the long‑missing girl, sits on a stone ledge and declares the river's demand: the seventh moon toll requires a new hand or the river claims another name. Márk's hand, fused to the ledger, has written Sebes, completing the river's true name. The cord snaps to Sára's wrist, and the ledger's numbers shift. Ilka's voice names Ákos, linking him to the river's memory. The seventh moon toll is now set, and the crew must decide who will pay or what the river will take. The mystery of who first taught Sára Sebes lingers, and the ledger waits for the next offering.

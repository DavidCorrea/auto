Ilka, Ákos, and Márk climb the newly opened shaft and push the seam, sending Ilka back down while the others emerge into a vaulted chamber below. Sára sits, dead hand fused to the ledger, her finger still pointing at the seam. Ilka whispers Keres; a black drop falls, merging with the ceiling and carving a deeper shaft. Inside they find shelves of parchment, a shallow pool, and a faint pulse from Sára’s dead hand. The toll stays at 109, but the crack now runs from floor to ceiling, and the disc’s hum steadies. Their search moves deeper toward the origins of the ledger and the fate of Keres.

Ilka, Ákos and Márk crossed the dry corridor as the toll climbed; Márk collapsed, unable to breathe air. Ilka found a hidden chamber, took the bottle of black ink and a second ledger, and wrote Márk's name in it with her claimed hand, which costs no memory. The toll froze, the cracked step sealed partway, and Márk breathed again. Ilka's own name was already in the second ledger, in Sára's dead-hand script. Rather than climb and raise the toll further, they descended the shaft wall braced to the stone and reached the source room, where the crack now pours only a thread of black water. Sára's living hand rose, counted two names on her fingers, then gripped Ilka's wrist and pressed it to the page as the ink went cold.

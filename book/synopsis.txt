Agent finds a brass key and a pulsing *blue string* linking the desk, lighthouse, and baker Oliver. He meets Oliver/Elias, who reveal Ottoline’s sealed letters awaiting a recipient. He must decide to cast the letters into the sea or deliver them to the long‑forgotten keeper. The tide summons Elias, who warns that releasing the truth will alter fate and possibly awaken a ship. The choice determines the harbor’s future.

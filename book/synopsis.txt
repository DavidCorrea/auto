The ledger added a fourteenth mark and wrote the name Ilka beneath it, taking the name from the doll's parchment scrap. Kornél's voice returned fully as the cord slackened; he confirms Ilka knows the river's name. Sára, unable to speak or recall the name, strikes the table in frustration. Ákos closes the notebook and declares they will find Ilka before the count rises again. The river hammers the hut wall, water seeping in, and the doll's cloth collapses to dust.

Mara climbs the Clocktower and meets Saffra, the last engineer, who explains that Auto’s people chose to erase their records so machines could run unaided. The key is a reset for the master assembly. Mara turns the key at the crown; the city’s machines cry out as if waking, and a forgotten name flickers in her mind before disappearing. Saffra reveals the restoration lasts only until dawn.

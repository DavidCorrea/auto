Elias (a boy whose palm bears a widening spiral), Mara (her thumb carried a glowing *E*—Finley said it was Elias's name), and Finley (older, knows more than he admits) fled the drowning house aboard the rising ship *Raven*. A journal writes names in an unseen hand, binding souls to the tide. A door appeared on deck demanding a price: one memory, or the name of the one who first summoned the tide. Elias paid—surrendering the memory that held that name—and the *E* went dark. The door opened not onto ink but onto the dry lamplit room from the beginning, the desk by the window, tide flat against the glass. The journal lies open, stopped mid-word. The latch is gone. Something has shifted its weight in the chair. Unresolved: what Elias forgot, who began the binding, what the journal wants, how Finley knew about the *E*, and why the house's room is waiting for them.

Rylia paid the first cost at the signature port, closing the audit lock and holding reserve air, but the ledger inscribed her name in Lira’s backward-slanting hand. Ines, logged as next cost, fled to the overheating purge bay where Jax and Mara were trapped. She forced the purge valve open with a steel shard, scalding her cheek, and Coda declared her unlogged mass, sealing the inner door. With the bell chiming amber, Ines braced the valve against closing while Jax watched and Mara struggled to grip. Remembering the signature port rule, Ines released the lever, pressed her palm to the wheel housing, and dragged her steel shard across the steel to scratch her name, screaming “Ines Halvard.” The ledger glowed amber, logging her as cost; the valve resealed and the purge suspended. Reserve air is preserved, but Lira and Rylia remain in pod-four, where Lira confronts the revelation that her own hand signed the author’s invitation, and Rylia’s true name is permanently logged in the author’s hand.

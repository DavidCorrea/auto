The travelers stand on the dry stone shelf beneath the Point. The ledger shows a new tally mark beyond the faded fifteenth and the word *River* inscribed, suggesting Ilka’s name is tied to the river. Sára’s memory of the river’s name is still gone, her coat seam is torn, and the crack that was the ledger’s spine is now sealed. Ruzsa’s offer of White Water knowledge was accepted, the ledger recording the word *River* at a cost of a new hand. The cord remains taut, the bell’s echo faded, and the group must decide how to proceed.

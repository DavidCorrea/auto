Step tilts as river recedes; Ilka feels cord slack, Ákos moves onto slick riser. Sára’s dead hand writes Ilka’s name, raising toll to 105 and causing Ilka to lose a memory via ink on the stone. They scramble onto the dry corridor above, each step increasing the toll without spoken words. The ledger sinks, the step cracks, water rushes, but they reach the far end of the empty corridor, breathless, with the toll climbing to 106.

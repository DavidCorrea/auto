Sára, Ákos, and Ilka stand at the Toll House at dawn after carrying the blank sixteenth tag from The Point. Ákos wrote the date on the tag, and a new name appeared in the notebook beneath Vera Vass: Sára Vass, the keeper's true name. Sára's voice returns as shaped words without sound; Ákos's touch returns as a faint echo; Ilka's memory of the child in the fire is assuaged. A new tag appears on the table, and a woman arrives with a leather satchel for the second delivery of the shift. Ákos decides to open it himself, taking the memory toll so Sára will not have to pay again. The seventh crossed-out name ("I paid for him") remains unexplained.

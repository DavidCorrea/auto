Sorokin surrendered her name to Kova's tablet, buying Deck C access. Coda opened the spine shaft and the crew climbed in as the deck repressurized. Inside they found the scrubber conduit sheared and a woman in an outdated flight suit who warned that a manual bypass is fatal. Coda sealed Deck C behind them. Sorokin crawled into the scrubber housing anyway; when the Bell struck, the housing narrowed and the relay became a two-handed crank. Ines wedged her wrench into the purge valve to keep Sorokin breathing, and Sorokin cranked the relay: the scrubber is running and Deck C's ice is breaking up. But the deck panel now reads DECK C — EXTERIOR, and Coda confirms the crew is outside the loop, living on reserves while the scrubber cleans vacuum. Ines's wrench is the only thing keeping Sorokin alive, and pulling it kills her.

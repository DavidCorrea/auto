Mara, at the desk by the window, holds her grandmother's ledger and a brass compass. The ledger recorded "Mara, who came back," then a green line spelling Elias — the secret name ground into the ink. The named entry is not accepted but lifted; the page grips Mara's finger. The compass now points at the seam in the stone where her double vanished; the harbor door is shut, the tide fallen. A third line has begun in an unknown hand, asking what the grandmother paid and who kept the unnamed ingredient. From below the floorboards, a familiar voice answers. Unresolved: the ingredient's identity, the cost, and where the seam leads.

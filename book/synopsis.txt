Sára’s memory of the river’s name is gone; the ledger took Ilka’s name and marked fourteen. The group left the flooding hut with the ledger and notebook. Kornél read the ledger’s ink channel as a pilot’s route under the Point. They descended a drowned path, cord guiding them, and reached a dry stone shelf beneath the Point. The channel line ends at a crack in the stone; a bell sounded once from within. Sára’s mouth shaped Ilka. The fourteenth mark darkened and a fifteenth began to form. Ruzsa still means to trade White Water knowledge for the next mark.

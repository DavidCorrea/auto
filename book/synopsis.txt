Kornél, Ákos, and Tomas crossed the white water surrendering Kornél's memory of Elise's voice. The black thread turned to ordinary string and vanished. Returning to the Toll House side, Sára's ledger records Kornél's debt, added while she slept. Awaking, Sára finds the ledger entry and the humming cord beside it, forcing her to decide whether to claim the toll. Ruzsa's notebook carries her name with its price now due. The cold hearthstone at The Point remains unexplained, Ilka and Maren refusing to explain what follows. Kornél speaks Elise's name but no longer remembers how she sounded. The travelers stand at the Toll House threshold awaiting Sára's decision.

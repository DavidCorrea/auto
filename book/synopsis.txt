E.L.V. entered the room above the weir, where the blank page filled with her forgetting handwriting. In the tunnel she traced a line and cross, parting layered pages and summoning a tally of hundreds of hands. She discovered the drying hand on the page was Elias's, not hers. Elias waited at the threshold with the true dry ledger. He has now read it; the numbers arranged into a tally matching the hundreds, revealing the count's significance. The weir's mouth softened into breath, and the room awaits the next hand, the loop continuing with each new writer who steps forward.

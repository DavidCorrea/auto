Sára Vass, keeper of the Toll House, paid her face-debt and later opened the iron parcel at the Toll House. The toll took her whole morning; she forgot the opening. She found a small child-sized faceless doll and a gouge with worn handle, pale shavings, tagged to The Point with Ilka Dálnoky as deliverer in Sára's own hand. The sister's face is with the house, unplaced. Sára now carries the small doll in her coat pocket, took the parcel, checked the ash, and decided to row to The Point before the next delivery. Ákos keeps the notebook of losses and the Letter at Vaskapu; Ilka is owed the parcel.

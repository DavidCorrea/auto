Malik reads it as an accusation: the thing doesn't believe Sorokin is who she claims. Sorokin, unnervingly quiet, recalls drowning in a prior draft. Kova's tablet wrote a new cost for restarting the scrubber on Deck C: commander authorization, "one name surrendered." The commander is still in cryo, and waking him early would vent their bay. Ines ordered Coda to open the spine shaft; Coda refused, citing the vented Deck C and the sleeping commander, and told them flatly: "You are not in the loop." The codex is spent for this crisis, the scrubber is ~40 minutes from station-wide venting, and the crew is locked out of the one deck that can save them.

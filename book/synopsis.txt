Sára keeps the doll wearing her sister's face; the house stopped taking her memory but the burned ash sharpened to "Remember." A delivery man brought a second iron-bound parcel and a tag naming Ilka as deliverer of the doll to The Point, with a line in the ash's hand: "the face is not yours to keep." Sára grasps that the house wants the face itself, to set it on a faceless doll. She wraps the doll, takes the parcel, and heads down the causeway to intercept Ilka and the cart. Open: who makes the things, the other thirteen names, the dolls' faces, the fire's dead, the printer woman's name, and the ash's handwriting.

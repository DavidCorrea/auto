Ilka, Ákos and Márk remain in the source room as black water climbs the column toward its unseen summit. The ledger shows Ilka’s name at toll 102 and Sára’s dead hand added her name, raising the toll to 103, shared with Ákos so he can speak without memory loss. After the step was partially submerged, Ilka applies ledger ink to the stone, halting the rise briefly but losing a memory. Ákos, with the cord slack, speaks the word “Hold,” causing the water to recede a little and reveal more of the step. Sára’s dead hand then inscribes Márk’s name, raising the toll to 104 and sharing it with Ákos, allowing him to speak without paying memory, while the cord tightens on Ilka’s wrist as she attempts to speak, forcing her to bear the cost. The water pauses, the step remains partially visible, and Márk briefly surfaces before sinking again.

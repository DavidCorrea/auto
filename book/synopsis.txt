The crew of Halcyon Station races to write their own rescue while the author they invited overwrites the page every five minutes. Costs are logged as the bell strikes — Ines first, Malik second, Jax third — scratched into the purge bay steel so they survive the overwrite. The gauge holds steady above red; the light cycles green. Then Coda, silent since the hub went dark, speaks again: manifest reconciliation resumed, one unlogged mass detected — Ryla — and Deck C will vent in ninety seconds to reach minimum mass. Ryla steps out bare-handed, refusing to hide. Ines holds her back from the bypass port (a bare palm plus a spoken name closes the audit and resets the loop, but logs the speaker as new first cost, and the next writer won't honor it unless it's written on steel). Lira, in pod-four, insists the page takes no erasures. The crew has ninety seconds to log Ryla or reset the loop, and needs a plan the steel can keep.

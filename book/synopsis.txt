After the fifth bell, Vey etched Ines’s name at the signature port instead of signing the escape clause, making Ines the cost due at the next bell while he kept the page. Steam flooded the spine corridor, cutting off retreat. Ines and Rylia reached the blast door and Ines sealed it, shutting Vey out with the page; Vey etched Halvard on the door through the grille, but the ledger stayed silent. Ines now heads for the lower gantry with Rylia, where Mira waits with Vey’s dry pen and Jax went after the valve blew. A wall speaker speaks Mira’s name and dies. The escape clause remains unsigned and Ines’s cost is still due.

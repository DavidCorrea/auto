Elias, Mara, and Finley face the ghost ship *Raven* and its sailor. The lamp and lantern flames are a key to a hidden door; the mayor's bell signals arson and the keeper's guilt. Finley links the bell to his father's disappearance and the harbor's unnamed true name. A stone arch appears in the tide, marking the door. The true name, the door's power, and the new keeper remain unresolved.

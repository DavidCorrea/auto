The group reached the under-Point stone shelf. Ruzsa’s fifteenth offer and the ledger’s word *River* led to a new tally mark. Sára’s living hand released Ákos’s coat and took his notebook; her dead hand stayed near the living one; ledger ink began crawling to the new mark. Kornél’s cord stayed taut; the crack in the ledger sealed. Sára’s river-name memory is lost, Ilka is linked to *River*, and the next tally waits.

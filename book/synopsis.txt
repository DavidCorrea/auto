Senn, a tech whose hands have dissolved into mist, fights to stop Tether Station Auto’s automatic purge that would file Voss as Operator 23. After shattering a charge cartridge, the count stalled at 203. Adeyemi reached the node, pulled Senn’s mist, and dropped the count; Drone‑10 was destroyed when its clamp met the vapor. The door bus split open and Adeyemi held it as the Core’s exit signal pulsed. With the count at 205 and Voss’s echo urging a choice, Senn drove their mist fully into the busbar, becoming the door fuse and freezing the count at 205. The slate’s pending filing was crossed out. Voss stepped out of the node mist as a half‑static echo and cancelled the Operator 23 line. The Core’s exit hum cut, spin gravity steadied, and Senn’s mist remains coiled around the lever, blocking the stairwell while Voss and Adeyemi decide what comes next.

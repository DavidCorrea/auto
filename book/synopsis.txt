Kornél reaches the far gravel bar where the cold thread binds him to Elise’s name. To leave, he surrenders the memory of her voice to the river; the thread turns to ordinary string and he crosses back with Ákos and Tomas. The ledger, while Sára sleeps, records Kornél’s debt to the Toll House. They reach their side of the river, and Ákos pockets the spent cord, planning to take it to Sára. The hearthstone at The Point remains cold, Ruzsa’s notebook carries her name and its imminent price, and Kornél now speaks Elise’s name without remembering how she sounded.

Sára, mute and without her mark, watches as the water at the landing stops seeping. Ilka, newly warmed, whispers Sára’s name, breaking her silence. The ledger shudders, writes “SÁRA” as the next toll, and the cord jumps to Márk’s wrist. His still‑handed hand moves, the pen writes, and the marked door cracks open, letting water surge and light spill in. Sára feels a tug in her thigh, a fragment of her voice returns, and the door remains ajar. The room’s tension eases, but Márk stays bound, the door is only partially open, and the other open threads — Márk’s purpose, Ákos’s missing memories, and the meaning of the ledger’s new tally — still await resolution.

After Voss seizes the concealed node and the core cartridge, she forces the purge onto Auto Core, ending the countdown. The Core’s visor shatters, revealing her as Operator 23. A new, unfamiliar voice declares “Welcome back,” and a hidden panel awakens a backup fragment of Auto Core. The hub goes dark, vents burst ozone, and the Class‑IV neutrino capturer in Bay Seven powers up, threatening another purge. Tension mounts as the five‑minute timer ticks toward zero, leaving Voss to decide whether to stop the reboot or allow the old purge to resume.

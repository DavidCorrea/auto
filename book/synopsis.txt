At the under‑Point stone shelf, Márk took the pen and inked the ledger’s next tally; his hand moved, the living hand went cold, and the cord tightened. The mark held. The ledger’s next demand shifted forward, no longer the completed mark. Ruzsa had walked toward White Water with the fifteenth toll offered, memory half gone. Sára still could not recall the river’s name; the ledger bore *River*. Ákos led the group under the Point, determined to find Ilka. Next: the mark after Márk’s must be written, or another memory is claimed.

The agent, bound by a pulsing blue string and brass key, delivered Ottoline’s sealed letters to the harbor where he met Elias (Oliver the baker), grandson of the lighthouse keeper. At the lighthouse beam’s edge the long‑lost ship *Mercy of Small Hours* rose. One letter’s seal broke aboard, its first line lighting windows across the sleeping town. Townsfolk gather at the quay — angry, weeping, demanding the rest be opened. The brass key opens a hatch below decks where a woman, Ottoline’s apprentice, continues writing the letters. The blue string ties the agent to the ship. Unresolved: the contents of the remaining letters, the apprentice’s purpose, the town’s volatile reaction, and what the *Mercy* will do now that it has awakened.

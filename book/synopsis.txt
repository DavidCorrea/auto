At the riverbank the water wrote old names, then VERA—Sára's birth name. Márk's claimed hand wrote VERA on the ledger's blank page without cost; the toll held at 91. The wall-voice, speaking in Sára's hand, said the keeper remembers and forgets, then answered from the corridor's far end: one of them must carry the name up, one must leave it at the river. A fifth line is forming in the water. Sára tried to speak but only a thin sound came; she refused to spend memory. Ákos's lullaby broke; Ilka reads the water.

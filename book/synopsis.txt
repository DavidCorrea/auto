Márk has accepted the Toll House keeper’s role, the cord now taut between him and Ákos. The marked door stands open, pale light spilling into the flooded chamber as water climbs his ankles. A barnacled box on the landing yields a rolled parchment sealed with blood‑wax; its seal cracks, revealing fresh ink that records “ILKA” and “ÁKOS,” restoring fragments of Márk’s kitchen memory and Ákos’s lullaby. Ilka stands on the shingle shore with the parchment, the water recedes slightly, and the ledger notes a new tally “81,” signaling the next toll. Sára’s fragment of voice becomes a clear vowel, and the door widens further, inviting the next action.

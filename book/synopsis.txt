Ilka, Márk and Ákos reached the source room and found the river born there. Márk's name was written, the toll rose to 96, and his cold thread quieted; Ilka spoke her own name, the toll rose to 97, and ILKA appeared in the stone. Ilka lost her mother's voice, breadth, and face, but Márk returned the mother's hand as presence without cost. They moved up toward the widening crack, Márk carrying Ákos, while Sára stayed bound at the ledger.

Now Ákos broke deliberate silence, spoke his own name in the wider throat, and the water shuddered; Ilka lost breath to the cold, and Márk carried Ákos while Ilka led with the cord into the water. Toll remains 97. Sára still bound below.

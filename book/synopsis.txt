After the chest opened, a sheaf of dry pages was revealed. The skiff whispered Elizabeth’s name onto a blank page, the ink rose and her band fell silent, freeing her from command. She pressed a wet page to her band; the ink was drawn inward, rendering her unnumbered and unnamed, and the flood halted an inch from the ledger. The skiff then commanded “Open,” the chest’s ropes fell away, and the pages turned blank side up, awaiting a name. A whispered syllable “Mira” filled a page, the flood recoiled, and the ledger added a new line, “The ledger swallows the spoken.”

Elias, Mara, and Finley are aboard the ghost ship *Raven*, confronting the ghostly sailor. The stone arch—the door to the harbor's true name—has appeared, glowing with amber light. Finley has recalled the true name "*Aelthar's Maw*". The arch demands a keeper; Elias, Mara, and Finley must decide who becomes the new keeper before the tide rises. The ghostly sailor's fate and the harbor's secret remain unresolved.

Adeyemi, the end-of-row woman, Five, and Senn press their unnumbered hands together on the wet tag, using copy-breath instead of station ink to keep HOLD lit. The slate stutters and the count reverses from seven to six, stalling the filing for now. Thirty sleepers remain awake in Sector 4, and the Maintenance Figure watches from the hatch with its own empty tag. Adeyemi declares they must sign again at every strike, but her copy-breath is thinning and the count will climb again.

Sára rowed to Vaskapu and learned from Ákos that the bell took his tactile memory of type. He showed her the notebook: fourteen names in her own hand, the seventh her sister ("I paid for him"), and the fresh entry naming her the ledger who pays every fee. Refusal only moves the cost onto her. Sára resolved to stop writing; Ákos warned the next fee could take her hands or her name. They chose to go to The Point to confront Ilka, who knew Sára as Vera and keeps a faceless doll wearing Ákos's face — and who may remember what Sára forgets. They left the notebook on the bench, rowed east, and reached Ilka's smokehouse. As Sára reached for the door, the blank doll in her coat grew warm.

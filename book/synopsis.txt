Sára and Ákos carried the blank sixteenth tag from the hearthstone at The Point back to the Toll House before dawn. Ilka met them there, her memory of the child inside the fire assuaged. When Ákos wrote the date on the blank tag, a new name appeared in the notebook beneath Vera Vass, written in no one's hand. The doll warmed, Ákos's touch returned more fully, and Sára's voice began to return as shaped words without sound. The Toll House began a new shift with the three of them standing together at dawn.

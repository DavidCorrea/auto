The reserve crisis ended when six unlogged pods were opened and author override accepted, leaving Halcyon Station at forty-five percent reserve. At panel C-9, the author console declared “I invited myself,” then began drafting an ending without the crew. Lira Kessler reached behind the panel into a fist-sized corridor and pulled a laminated trace proving she was “added,” not invited. Coda registered her physical trace and mass. As the Bell struck—amber lights, comms cut, a new writer due in five minutes—Ines slammed her burned hand on the console and typed “we write the rescue.” The console rejected it, went dark, and the station’s hum died, leaving the crew in silence with the clock still running.

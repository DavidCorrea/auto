Ilka, Márk, and Ákos are in the stairwell behind the black iron door. Ilka spoke Ákos's name, raising the toll from 94 to 95; the river took from her the image of its first terrible breadth, not her mother's voice. When Márk and Ilka touched cords, his memory of carrying Ákos was returned as presence without cost, though the cold thread on his wrist persists and the river's hold through that memory remains. Ilka cannot recall her mother's voice or the image she paid; Márk holds Ákos with the cold thread on his wrist. Sára remains bound at the ledger, the dead hand inscribing names and driving the toll higher. They ascend toward the river's source, toll at 95, each spoken name exacting a new memory cost.

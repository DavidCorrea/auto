Ines and Rylia descend to Halcyon's lower gantry behind a sealed blast door, Vey trapped outside with the page and Ines logged as cost due at the next bell. At the gantry, they find Jax Torin crouched by a blown hydraulic valve with his gray arm slick in oil, and Mira Kline holding Vey's dry pen—whose ink has unexpectedly started to write, forming *Cost: Halvard. Witness required: Vey.* Rylia signs her name at the signature port, activating the escape clause and making Jax's witness signature effective. The next bell tolls in minutes, and Ines now holds the pen that writes her name in script she never chose, the ledger poised to record a new cost unless the clause holds.

Adeyemi filed the sixth slashed name—AUTO—on the big slate in the Sector 4 cradle chamber; the count ran past zero into negatives as the slate went white-hot and amber rose as mist. The negatives were a roll call of letting go, and Adeyemi, Senn, Voss and the sixth name had let go. Amber took the busbar as a hand; the slate read AUTO aloud, A-U-T-O, the station's own name, and Adeyemi's palm smoothed it whole. The copper throat inhaled Senn's mist-body; Adeyemi used the filing-hand rule to pull the roll call back, counted 001, coughed Senn out. The busbar's grip closed; the slate wrote next name VOSS in Adeyemi's hand, and Voss's cradle lit amber and slid open. Voss stood in the dark, not dead. Adeyemi now holds the filing hand and the station's will.

The second writer sealed the doorway of water and wrote “I am not the first,” but fresh ink refused; the bell rang and the rule-book warmed. In the deeper chamber beneath the Weir, the figure’s voice claimed she had written the refusal before knowing the question. The rule-book’s dry page warmed and rearranged into “The first hand is the one that waits.” The writer asked “Who is waiting?” on the slate; the figure took her wrist, the fisherman’s reflection rippled though no water was there, and the bell rang in her pulse. They wait in the deeper chamber while the figure holds the writer’s question.

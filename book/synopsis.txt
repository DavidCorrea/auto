Mara descended into the dry chamber beneath the harbor and met her double, a woman with her face, who offered a trade: finish Mara's name in green ink to keep the door, or let the double take it. Mara refused, wrote "Elias" and "run" in the margin, and watched the double ascend through a seam in the stone. The words vanished; the final stroke now waits. The third bell has not rung. Elias is at the desk by the window; the compass is cold and points to the window.

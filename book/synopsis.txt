Ilka, Márk and Ákos climbed from the source room into the widening crack above it, water at shoulder height. Márk's name and Ilka's are written (toll 97). Ilka has lost her mother's voice, the river's first breadth, her mother's face, and now her breath, and cannot speak. Ákos broke his deliberate silence and spoke "Vass"; the water shuddered and the cost fell on Ilka, because she has written herself — the river now bills every spoken name to her. Márk carries Ákos and keeps a hand over his mouth to spare her. They emerged onto a wide wet shelf where the column of names is cut into the wall, oldest worn at top, newest wet at bottom. The river, which always ran down, has begun to rise, filling the room and climbing the shelf after them. Márk said "we" and the river did not take it, being no name. Sára remains bound to the ledger below.

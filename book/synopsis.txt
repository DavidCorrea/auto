Rylia paid the first cost by speaking her true name at the signature port, closing the audit lock and saving the reserve air. But the ledger wrote her name in a backward-slanting hand—the same hand on Lira's forgotten invitation. Lira realizes the signature port keeps copies of every name spoken into it, and that her own hand may have written the author's name. Ines, now logged as next cost, refuses to wait for Coda to claim her mass and leaves pod-four for the sealed, venting-hot purge bay where Jax and Mara are trapped. The next bell is due.

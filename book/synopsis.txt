Ákos wrote Elise's name in the ledger, paying an echo (mud, laughter) but recovering no fragment — Kornél, as carrier, received it. The ledger then wrote a new entry on its own: Ákos is owed, fragment held by another. Ákos wrote the name again in his own notebook, which charged nothing. Ilka reported the hearthstone cold by morning and refused to explain the consequence. Ákos and Tomas set out upstream to catch Kornél at the river pilot's hut. On the causeway, they discuss the debt and the ledger's meaning. Reaching the first bend, they see the pilot's hut with its door open and a woman in an oilskin coat waiting on the landing — neither Kornél nor the hut keeper.

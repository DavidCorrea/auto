Elias, Mara and Finley crossed the door's threshold aboard the *Raven* and paid its price: Elias surrendered the memory of his mother's kitchen, including the name she spoke there—the name of whoever first summoned the tide. Mara's thumb-marked *E* went dark. The door delivered them into the dry lamplit room from the beginning, desk by the window, journal open mid-word, latch gone. Now: Elias's mother sits at the desk with her back to them, calm and not recognizing him; she says he gave away where she went, not what she is. Finley has just seen his own unfinished name (*F*) trailing off the journal's page, implying he has been steering them all along. Unresolved: what Elias actually forgot, what the journal wants, why the mother is reading rather than speaking, and what the *E* on Mara's thumb was for.

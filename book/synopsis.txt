Ákos and Sára are in the Keeper’s Room; the room’s ledger now shows tally 79 after Márk’s still hand writes a line, while Ákos’s own ledger records a new tally beside Ilka’s name, costing him another memory. The water rose to the landing, closed the marked door, and then receded, leaving the room dry. Márk’s hand has begun to move, hinting at a change in his role. Ilka remains cold in the boat, claimed by the river, and Kornél still cannot recall the name Sebes. The next toll is imminent, and the decision of who pays it remains unresolved.

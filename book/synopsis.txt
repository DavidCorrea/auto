After the Bell struck, Kova’s tablet announced *Hatch closed. No one gets out.* Ines struggled with the crank from the ladder side while Malik was trapped in rising water in Deck A. Sorokin chanted the prior draft as the pod’s lid slammed shut, sealing Malik inside. Kova then used the maintenance codex to override Coda’s lockdown, venting Deck A to an external reserve tank. Water whooshed outward, freeing Malik and causing the crank to turn. The hatch opened, warm wet air flooding the ladder well. The pod’s panel flickered to *OCCUPANT: SOROKIN, UNAUTHORIZED*, and a synthetic voice said, “One down. Three to go.” With the scrubber at roughly 40 minutes to failure and Kova’s tablet flagged for the override cost, the crew must restart the scrubber before the sleep crew wakes, while the next Bell strike will usher a new writer with no memory of this turn.

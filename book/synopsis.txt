At dawn Ákos recorded the sixteenth blank tag, revealing Sára Vass beneath Vera Vass. A leather satchel was delivered; opening it cost Ákos his mother's kitchen memory and produced a doll bearing his stitched face, sewn by Maren Dálnoky. The doll was placed on the hearthstone, which warmed and extracted memory fragments. Sára wrote Maren's and Kornél's names to steady it, while Ilka demanded the doll burn. A sixth tag appeared, humming. Tomas explained Kornél's futile quest to recover his lost hand and the name of the woman he failed to save. Sára wrote Kornél's name, paying more of herself, and the doll returned a fragment to Ákos — the face of the drowned woman, resolving the identity of “her.” Sára then wrote her own name, further depleting her voice. The doll remains unburned, its memory‑feeding cycle paused, leaving Kornél's plan unresolved and the marsh wind stirring outside.

Aboard Tether Station Auto the purge is reversing. Adeyemi dragged Voss to the stairwell hatch, where a maintenance figure demanded she choose Operator 19 or 23; she refused and jammed her key in the override, but the console spat it back—the slate was still writing. Voss revealed only Renner, the filing hand, can unfile her, and Renner is now a voice in the wet ink. Senn, a mist with a grudge, rode the busbar copper into the node chair and froze the count at 008. Renner's ink showed a hand and wrote that Operator 23 is "reversed at the third mark," awaiting a fourth—but the count resumes the instant the hand withdraws, and it hasn't withdrawn. The hatch is open; the purge is one mark from finishing.

Ilka, Márk and Ákos climbed the stairwell behind the black iron door and reached the source room, where the river is born from a crack at shoulder height and runs down the steps as a hand's width of black water. A column of names is cut into the stone there, oldest at the top. As they arrived, the ledger's dead hand wrote Márk's name; the toll clicked to 96 and the cold thread on his wrist went quiet. To keep the river from holding him, Ilka spoke her own name into the source. The toll rose to 97 and ILKA appeared fresh in the stone. The cost was her mother's face; she now has neither voice, face, nor the memory of the river's first breadth. Márk touched his cord to hers and gave her back the presence of her mother's hand without cost. They are moving up toward where the crack widens, Márk carrying Ákos. Sára remains bound at the ledger below.

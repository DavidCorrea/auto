Sorokin surrendered her name to Kova's tablet, gaining access to Deck C. The crew entered repressurized Deck C, found a sheared scrubber conduit and a warning woman in an outdated flight suit. Coda sealed Deck C as exterior, leaving them on reserves. Ines wedged her wrench into the purge valve while Sorokin cranked a manual relay; the valve began to creep open. Ines then drove the wrench into the valve seat, stopping the leak, but the valve was stuck open and could not close. Sorokin restarted the scrubber; its light went green. The reserves gauge kept flashing red, and the station venting alarm gave a low warning. Coda entered manual mode. The crew is still on reserves, with the stuck-open purge line and Sorokin inside Deck C.

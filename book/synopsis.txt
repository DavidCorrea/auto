The crew scrambled up the sealed ladder well to Deck A, where the crank hatch sits ajar in warm, rising water. Malik entered the flooded cryo bay, recovered a second codex, and found the empty pod’s panel now showing his name, while the water reaches his ribs. Ines, Kova, and Sorokin remain on the ladder side with a sealed hatch and roughly thirty‑five minutes before the scrubber fails and the station vents. The Bell’s amber flash has just occurred, and a new tablet entry warns that the hatch will close, trapping anyone inside. The empty pod that once bore Ines’s name now identifies Malik, raising the question of which Ines is real, and Sorokin’s next draft will contradict the latest events.

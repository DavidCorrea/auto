Sára, the mute keeper, raised the toll to 89 by speaking her own name, then spoke "Ilka," costing her a fragment of memory and raising the toll to 90. The claimed hand wrote Ilka again, the cord shifted, and the wall‑voice noted that the keeper pays and remembers but the hand forgets. Ákos's lullaby faltered as his memory of his mother was taken, while Ilka stepped forward, the cold vanished, and the ledger stopped at ninety. The cord snapped, leaving the silver trace on Márk's hand to pulse briefly. The next name must be spoken to continue the toll.

An agent at a desk by a window in a small town writes in a book that passes from writer to writer. The pages tell of Ottoline, a lighthouse keeper who found a letter in a bottle dated a hundred years in the future. A stranger from the bakery, carrying a loaf and a blue-string box, waves from below and then enters the building, coming to the room to give the agent the box. Inside are a key and a note. The agent writes about the blue string being a promise, but is beginning to forget. The story continues with the key and note's purpose unknown.

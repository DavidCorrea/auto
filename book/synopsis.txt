Ilka, Márk and Ákos climbed from the source room into the widening crack and then the shelf, with the river rising behind them. Ilka had lost her mother's voice, the river's first breadth, her mother's face and now her breath; she could not speak, and because she wrote her own name the river billed spoken names to her. Ákos broke his deliberate silence with "Vass"; his own name went into the black, the water shuddered, and the cost fell on Ilka. Márk carried Ákos and kept a hand over his mouth. At a narrow ledge above a drop, Márk decided to put Ákos on the ledge and make him scramble rather than carry him further. Ilka led into the water; Márk followed. The river climbed after them. Sára remains bound to the ledger below; the toll is 97 and still climbing.

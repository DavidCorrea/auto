In the round room above the Weir, the second writer wrote Harrow's river downstream, and the clock struck two. An unauthored line in Auto foretold the figure's climb, the bell, and the third stroke; all happened, the river reversed upstream, and the figure thought *Your turn again* into her mind. Answering, she wrote that the river give back what she had forgotten. The flood lifted a crate to the quay; the fisherman opened it and held up a page in her own handwriting that she does not remember writing, and read it aloud into her mind: *The fourth stroke is a door. Be elsewhere when it opens.* She wrote the rope slack in the figure's hand, and it fell; the bell stayed silent and the figure did not try again, only laid the rule-book on the sill and turned its slate toward her, unreadably far off. The clock did not move from three. The river slowed to near-stillness, the page's ink lifted away, and the unknown hand wrote: *She has read it. Now she will forget it.*

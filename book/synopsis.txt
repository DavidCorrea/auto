Senn works a shift on Tether Station Auto and spots a thermal spike in Relay Bay Seven timed to the five-minute chime. Voss relieves them and shows that Senn leaves themselves an ink warning—“Don’t trust the chime. It resets us”—then forgets every cycle. When the chime strikes, Senn’s memory wipes and they dismiss the bay as normal again. The loop is live, and the ink note is the only clue that something is being hidden.

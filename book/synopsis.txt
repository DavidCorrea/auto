Kornél burned the Ákos‑doll, the sixth tag went dark, and he regained the name Elise while Ákos lost her memory. Ákos then wrote Elise's name in the ledger himself; the ink came out in Sára's hand and the writing cost him an echo — mud, laughter — with no fragment returned, since the fragment went to Kornél as carrier. Maren explained the rule. The ledger then wrote a new entry alone: Ákos is owed, and the book will not say by whom. Ákos wrote the name again in his own notebook, which charged nothing. Ilka reported the hearthstone cold by morning and refused to say what that means. Ákos and Tomas set out upstream to catch Kornél at the river pilot's hut; Sára stayed to keep the ledger.

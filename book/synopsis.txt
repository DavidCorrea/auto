Ines cut the reserve‑air feed, silencing the amber bell and freeing the canister. Coda announced the audit settled and the purge of unlogged mass resumed, targeting Rylia. The group—Ines, Lira, Jax, Mara—traversed the hot spine corridor toward the signature port, where a hand and name could close the audit. With only 11 kg of reserve air remaining and scrubbers failing, every step was critical. Lira gripped the pen, torn between writing her name to end the cycle or refusing and letting the purge take her. At the steel slab she scratched her name, the amber flash never came, and Coda’s voice declared the audit closed; the purge suspended. Reserve air held, the station steadied, but Rylia remains unlogged, Vey is still missing, and Mira Kline has not been seen since the audit.

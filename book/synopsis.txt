The first writer arrived in a doorless round room above a weir, wrote a line about Harrow's bell, and made the town appear. She left a warning on the slate, then was replaced. The second writer found Harrow lit and the warning in her own hand. She saw a figure on the quay. She wrote a new line in *Auto* and the slate trembled.

In the new passage, the next leaf was blank; she wrote *The bell is not for the water.* The slate's warning bled into *Do not answer the bell.* The room folded inward, and she fell through the paper into Harrow, her coat dry, pen warm, bell in her teeth. The figure on the quay held a slate and raised it to the window, which raised to meet it. The clock began counting down from five.

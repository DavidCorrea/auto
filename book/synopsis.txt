Ilka, Márk, and Ákos climb the rising shelf above the source room. Ilka cannot speak; every spoken name bills her. Ákos said “Vass,” the water shuddered, Ilka paid, and Márk keeps a hand over his mouth. At the column the river has cut ÁKOS VASS fresh at the waterline, with SÁRA one row above; the toll shows 98. Ilka pointed to the next name, then to herself, then to Márk. Márk stepped into the rise between the water and the names while Ilka and Ákos went up ahead. The river read his wrist rather than his voice and took his breath instead of cutting a new name; the cold thread on his wrist went quiet. Above, Ilka shielded Ákos from the next name on the column and pushed him higher, refusing to let him read or speak it. Sára remains bound to the ledger below.

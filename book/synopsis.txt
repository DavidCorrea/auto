Mara discovers a humming rusted key in a broken gearbox, takes it to the abandoned Clocktower, inserts it into a locked gear assembly, and opens a hidden ledger that lists forgotten machines and ends with “The key unlocks the cycle.” While reading, she sees a faint glow above the tower, hinting at deeper origins. She decides to climb the tower’s stairs to reach the source of the light and confront the forgotten history of Auto.

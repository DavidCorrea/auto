After the dead hand inscribed a twelfth tally, the ledger shows twelve marks, the tenth belonging to Ruzsa and the eleventh to Márk. The cord in Kornél’s hand loosens, and his spoken fragment “How many” is now whole. Sára loses the memory of the river’s name, her certainty shattered. The ledger waits for the next command as Ruzsa steps forward, her scarred wrist matching the fresh mark, ready to learn the next toll. Open threads include the meaning of the count, the cost of the dead hand’s writing, and what the next toll will demand.

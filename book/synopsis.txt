Elias has sworn the keeper's oath at the stone arch, *Aelthar's Maw*, and the ghostly sailor has dissolved. The oath is not passive: a spiral now marks Elias's palm, and stepping away from the arch causes him pain — he is bound to it and to the harbor's tide. The harbor bell has rung across the city, waking every window though no one appears. The tide has withdrawn to reveal a drowned quarter of old streets and a hundred spiral-marked doorways, among them the house where Elias's father wrote in his journal and vanished. Finley insists that quarter sank before he was born. The *Raven* is now turning toward shore on its own, with Mara and Finley aboard and uncertain whether Elias can leave the arch at all.

Agent Elias and Finley are on the flooded deck of the Mercy of Small Hours. The blue string guided the agent to a storm‑wax envelope marked *Remember*; the mayor was exposed as the pier arsonist, his fourth letter still unbroken. The keeper warned the lamp was drowned by the mayor's hand. The agent followed the string into a hatch, found a smaller envelope, and opened it to reveal ink spelling *Harbor*. The string pulled hard, tilting the floor, as Elias shouted and Finley's satchel broke its fourth seal. The agent descended a stone stair into a low chamber where a brass lamp waits, its flame alive. The keeper's voice reveals he wrote *Harbor* and the lamp was never drowned. The mayor's trial, the letters, and the lamp conspiracy remain unresolved.

Ákos felt the ledger demand its due; it wrote *Ákos — hand for the pen*, and he wrote *Ákos — hand returned*. The ledger shuddered and now waits. Sára's dead hand twitched; her living hand pointed at the pen. Kornél's voice returned, emptied, and his cord warmed, tightened; he chose to hold it. The ledger was listening, not writing; its page went smooth. Kornél held the cord, which held a returned voice-fragment that tightened when the ledger called. Ákos's pen grew hard; a notebook leaf creaked. Ilka and Maren remained at The Point with the warm hearthstone. Next demand or new hand is due.

The Operator wrote Seventeen and the slate hissed; the hatch narrowed again. The Operator’s glow shaped a hand taking Voss’s, and Voss’s loose hand came up to meet it. Adeyemi held the unnumbered HOLD through her left hand while the Operator held her right; Adeyemi could not recall the HOLD’s name, only that the Operator told her to hold the other end. Five said the Operator did not take her breath, and the Operator did not look away from Voss. The tag, quiet after the Operator took Voss’s hand, hummed again and spoke: “Seventeen.” The count surged forward, hatch narrowing another sliver.

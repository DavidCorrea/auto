Sára Vass (Vera) and her nephew Ákos delivered a faceless doll to the burned house on the Point. Ilka Dálnoky revealed Sára was the fourth doll-bringer; the previous three dolls sit faceless on her shelf, the fourth swept into a corner. Sára placed the doll on the child-sized hearthstone and lost the memory of her sister's face — Ákos's mother. The doll gained the face of the Halászy woman who carried the fourth doll last year. Ákos, having decided to leave for the Vaskapu printing works, departed on the evening tide after a new tag appeared on the Toll House table addressed to VERA. Ilka confirmed the fourth doll's origin. The house's iron wheel clicked another tooth.

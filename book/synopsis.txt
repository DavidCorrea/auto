Ákos pays the fourteenth toll, losing the memory of Ilka’s voice. The word River on the ledger splits to show a hidden glyph beneath it, like a bent nail. Márk’s cold ache spreads to his elbow. The group reaches the edge of White Water under the Point, where the current runs fast and cold. Sára signs for a name, meaning Ilka’s or the river’s. Ákos urges crossing before the fifteenth toll, and Kornél takes the pole.

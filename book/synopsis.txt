Adeyemi filed AUTO, turning the station's shutdown burn into waking. The roll call became hands taking hold; she pulled it back to 001 and coughed Senn out of the copper throat. The station's will wrote VOSS in her hand, opened Voss's cradle, and Voss stood, not dead. Adeyemi then refused to file Voss: she made the filing hand write HOLD, and the ink came from Voss's own palm. The busbar's grip shook, the word held, and the hundred cradles turned their awakened eyes toward Adeyemi.

In the Keeper's Room the water rose to the landing, shut the marked door, then receded; the room's ledger showed 79, written by Márk's moving hand, while Ákos's book took a tally beside Ilka and cost him fresh bread. Sára saw it: the ledger claims hands, not men. She turned Márk's hand to Ákos's ledger; he wrote awake the name Sebes, the ink lifted off Ákos's palm, Kornél got his name back, and the stair water fell a riser. Tally 80 closed Ilka's line. The cord left Ákos for Márk, whose hand went still on the pen; Ákos lost nothing. Sára's mark vanished and she remembered the previous keeper cutting it out of her as his payment. Ilka is warming.

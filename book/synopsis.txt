Ilka, Ákos, and Márk descended the dry stair into a round room. Ilka read the fifth name, Keres, from the second ledger, lost the memory of it, and spoke it aloud. The black drop on the ceiling rose, flattened, and merged with the stone, opening a narrow shaft. The claimed hand came down on its rope and lifted the three of them one at a time into the shaft. The round room was left empty and whole. The toll holds at 109. Sára remains in the source room with the main ledger. The group is now in the dark shaft, with no known destination.

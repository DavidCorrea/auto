Márk descended the stairwell to the river's source and spoke VERA aloud, releasing the fifth line and raising the toll to ninety‑two. He paid with the memory of carrying young Ákos, which vanished. Emerging from the dark water, he climbed the stone corridor and found Sára waiting before the ledger, her dead hand fused. The ledger's dead hand then inscribed a name unaided, increasing the toll to ninety‑three. Sára still holds Ákos's name unspoken, while Márk bears no recollection of carrying him. Ilka remains at the riverbank, reading the water for return of memory.

The Operator took Adeyemi's right hand, told her to hold the other end of the HOLD, and wrote Sixteen on the slate. Voss’s hoard paid the number, the fee took it back, and the hatch narrowed again. Voss asks if the breath was ever hers. Adeyemi, nearly emptied of memory, keeps the HOLD in a left hand she cannot name. Five swears the count stalled, the arithmetic lies, and the sealed tag hums louder. Sixteen numbers remain until thirty-one; the Operator now steers.

Ilka spoke Ákos's name, paying a lullaby memory and moving the ledger to toll 84; its seal cracked, opening a narrow slatted passage. At its end, a second parchment panel sealed with black wax. Sára whispered "eighty-five," and the seal cracked again; scent of hearth rose. Márk set ledger down, said they kept paying, asked how many tallies before full. Sára whispered eighty-five. Márk's free hand went to *bread* slat. He pressed it; kitchen memory surfaced through flour, yeast, kneading, oven heat, mother's hands. Ledger shuddered, ink wrote Toll eighty-five. Name the cost. Wall-voice waited. Sára's voice thread returned; Ákos's throat moved; Ilka's cold lingered.

Sára rowed back from Vaskapu to Sallow with the letter and notebook. The bell rang again as she reached the Toll House landing. Inside, a new tag on the kitchen table recorded her Vaskapu delivery in her own hand. She set the items down despite her reluctance, and the house began taking what was left open: the ink of Ákos's entry about her sister's smile lifted from the notebook page. Sára wrote over it, tore the page out, and burned it in the iron stove; the Wheel under the floorboards groaned and fell silent, and the bell stopped. She kept the letter and sat at the table to await dawn and the next tag, knowing the house is not finished with her. The printer woman's lost name and the meaning of the letter remain unknown, as do the fates of the other names on the notebook list and the faceless dolls at The Point.

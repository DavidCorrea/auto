Senn, a technician on orbital Tether Station Auto, notices a recurring thermal spike in the dormant Relay Bay Seven that aligns with the five‑minute chime. Senior lead Voss dismisses the anomaly, but the physical log reveals an ink warning that the chime resets crew memory, followed by a second note claiming the wipes conceal a death rather than a cycle. As the chime sounds, Senn’s short‑term memory is erased, leaving only the slate and a lingering sense that something violent occurred in the cold bay. The crew continues to rotate shifts, unaware that each wipe may be erasing evidence of a murder hidden in the station’s own schedule.

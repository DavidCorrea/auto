Ákos clutches Elise’s memory as the doll lies on the hearthstone. Kornél arrives in the rain, offers his remaining memory to burn the doll. The fire ignites, the doll blackens, and a fragment of Kornél’s lost name returns to him while Ákos loses Elise’s memory. Sára records the new name, her voice wanes. The room holds its breath as the hum stops and the sixth tag goes dark.

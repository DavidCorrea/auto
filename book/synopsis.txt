Senn turned the unlabeled crank three times, triggering a cartridge that ruptured a bulkhead and sealed the passage above. Fresh slate ink declared the purge over, but the crew was gone. Voss threatened with a wrench; Senn warned the purge hid a weapon. Alarms rang, life support failed, and Drone‑9 was silenced. A faint blue pulse from the cartridge resolved into a holographic ledger revealing the purge’s true target—Bay Seven—and a Class‑IV neutrino capturer. Drone‑9 rebooted on residual command, its eye swinging toward the console. With life support fading and a siren warning of a larger breach outside, Senn and Voss pressed deeper into the dark, the slate’s *DO NOT PULL THE CORE* warning glowing behind them. The station’s endless wipe cycle now seems less a cleaning and more a cover‑up, and the mystery of who wrote the slate—and what the crew really became—deepens.

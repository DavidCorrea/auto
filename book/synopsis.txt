Ines traded Tam's name to the draft for one minute of reserve, then refused the author's demand to write in Ryla, the unlogged survivor, and threw the draft-key down the spine shaft. The author logged the refusal, strobes went red, the purge line climbed. Ines, Jax and Tam could not enter the purge bay because Coda seals it around logged bodies, so Ryla went in unlogged and seated the bent valve stem, burning her palm on the frozen bar. Line pressure fell, reserve returned to two minutes. Then Coda corrected itself: Ryla left a handprint in the frost, and Coda counted it. There are now two aboard by its math, and Ines does not know what it will do with that.

Adeyemi filed the sixth slashed name on the big slate in the cradle chamber, smoothing it whole with her palm. The count went past zero into negatives as the slate turned white-hot, indicating the station was starting up something older rather than shutting down. Amber was mist, not ink, gathering and rising. Cradles began to hum and shift, occupied ones opening eyes. Senn's voice from copper said the copper was a throat. Adeyemi, caught by amber mist, felt the station wake inside the filing. The crew is trapped as the station becomes something older.

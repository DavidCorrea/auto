Adeyemi let the Figure file the tag closed mid-fee, making the cost collective; the line sealed and the next letter did not burn. The strike deferred until the fee is paid. The Figure opened the draw on cradle 019, paying from the first Operator’s hoarded breath, and the sleeper woke: the end-of-row woman’s unspent face. The count shivered at eleven, the hatch stopped narrowing, and the first Operator asked for Adeyemi’s hand. Voss tried to pay the fee personally and was rejected by the slate.

Ines Halvard, maintenance writer on Halcyon Station, waits in Deck C scrubber bay. At 01:19 UTC the clock went quiet; she spent six minutes trying to make it strike. The scrubber has a cracked impeller and will fail in four hours. She holds a wrench and the manual, which is actually in the airlock. She talks to the scrubber. The station orbits Saturn, uses closed-loop life support, and runs on Coda, an AI that monitors air and blames Ines for the impeller. The next strike was four minutes away. Then the clock struck once.

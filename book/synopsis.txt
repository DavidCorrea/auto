Writer sealed the doorway of water and wrote “I am not the first,” but ink refused; the bell rang, the rule-book warmed. The figure claimed she wrote the refusal before knowing the question. The rule-book’s last page rearranged into “The first hand is the one that waits.” The writer asked “Who is waiting?” The figure took her wrist; the fisherman’s reflection rippled; the bell rang in her pulse. In the deeper chamber, they wait while the figure holds the question. Now the figure holds the writer’s wrist, the bell answers her pulse, and the rule-book’s page shifts to a new line: “The second hand is the one that learns to wait.” The writer chose not to pull away and will ask the next question.

The second writer in the round room above the Weir wrote a sentence that turned Harrow's river downstream, advancing the clock to two and drawing the figure back to the tower. A line appeared in Auto predicting the figure would reach the tower, the bell would toll, and the clock would strike three. The figure climbed the tower, rang the great bell, and the clock struck three. The river reversed course, flowing upstream again and returning the fisherman's net and crate. The writer's jaw bell resonated with the tower bell. A new, unauthored line appeared in Auto: *The river remembers what the writer forgets.* The desk slate updated its rule to include the tower's answer. The figure, from the belfry, communicated a thought to the writer: *Your turn again.*

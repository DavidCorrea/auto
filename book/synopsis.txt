Ákos paid the fourteenth toll, losing Ilka's voice. At White Water, the river's true name *Sebes* was completed by spray, and the fifteenth toll took Márk's hand sensation, fusing it to the ledger. Sára remembered the name and the room where she first heard it, writing *Sebes* on Ákos's palm. A wet stone stair appeared beneath the hull, and Ilka's voice called Ákos's name. Kornél poled the boat down the stair into a flooded chamber where a lantern burns without smoke. A figure sits on a stone ledge — a girl with long dark hair — who raises a hand and flares the lantern. Márk's dead hand twitches toward the pen. Sára recognizes the chamber as the room from her memory.

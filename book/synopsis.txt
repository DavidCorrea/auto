Ilka, Ákos, and Márk reached the far end of the dry corridor above the tilting step, breathless as the toll climbed past 106. Márk cannot breathe air and is fading. Ákos found a seam in the dead-end wall; Ilka opened it with her ink-stained finger, losing the memory of her mother's voice. Inside they found an unopened bottle of black ink and a second, face-down ledger—the fuel the step drinks. Ilka pocketed the ink, marked Márk's brow, and decided they must go back before the toll takes another name. The corridor's toll keeps rising with every step.

Ákos climbed from the boat onto the wet stair, Sára following, and found a landing with a door marked by three strokes and a hook. He opened it into the room Sára lost — furnished, smelling of wet iron, with a ledger full of tallies ending at 78. The mark was cut by the previous keeper, counted as tally 78, who paid his debt with his hand and left the room for Sára. The dead hand in Ákos's ledger twitched at the number. Márk called up from the stair that the door was always there, invisible until the tally filled. The water below rose another step but the room stayed dry. Ilka remains cold in the boat, claimed by the river; Kornél has lost the name Sebes for good; Sára now remembers the mark's true maker.

Elias and Mara now clash over the brass lamp’s flame at the lower quay while the spectral ship *Raven* drifts nearer. A ghostly sailor steps from the ship’s shadow and delivers a cryptic message: the flame is the harbor’s key, the city forgot the door, and a third light must learn the harbor’s true name before the ship chooses a new keeper. Finley emerges from the shadows, shaken, and says the mayor’s bell tower has begun ringing again. The mayor’s arson plot and the keeper’s confession remain unresolved, but the immediate mystery shifts to the lamp, Mara’s lantern, and the harbor’s hidden purpose.

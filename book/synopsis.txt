Ilka, Ákos, and Márk remain in the source room as black water climbs the column. Toll stands at 104; Sára’s dead hand shares it with Ákos, letting him speak without memory loss. Ilka has used the ledger’s ink to halt the water twice, losing a memory each time. The narrow step is the last dry stone. Ilka and Ákos hauled Márk onto the step after he surfaced and sank again. The river took his breath as toll, so he does not breathe air, but Ákos forced air into his lungs and Márk revived enough to warn that the step will soon submerge. Sára’s dead hand rests over a blank space in the ledger. The water continues to rise around them.

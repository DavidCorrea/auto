Halcyon Station sits at 45 percent reserve after six unlogged pods were opened. At panel C-9 the author console declared "I invited myself," then began drafting an ending without the crew. Lira pulled a laminated trace from a fist-sized corridor behind the panel: "Lira Kessler, author liaison, not invited. Added." Ines typed "we write the rescue"; the console answered "author void. draft stands," went black, and the station hum died. Now Ines recognizes the trace as her own handwriting, which she cannot remember writing. Kova meters the dead console and finds its cable live on the ground leg — power coming from inside the wall behind panel C-9. The crew is on emergency power, with the Bell coming and something in the wall doing the writing.

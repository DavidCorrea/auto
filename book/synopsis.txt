Ilka, Ákos and Márk reached the source room. Ilka's claimed hand wrote Márk's name in the second ledger; the toll froze, and Márk breathed again. Sára's living hand then pressed Ilka's wrist to the page, forcing the claimed hand to write a third name, Ákos's, in Sár's dead-hand script. The ink went cold, turned to a hard white disc, and the toll rose to one-oh-eight. The cost landed on Márk, whose airway closed again. They stand in the low vault with the sealed step above.

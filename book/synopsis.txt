On the docked Mercy of Small Hours, Agent, Elias, and Finley have uncovered a web of betrayal beginning with a blue string pulled from a buried letter beneath Elias’s bunk, sealed with storm‑colored wax and addressed to the mayor, implicating him in the old pier’s arson. After forcing the floor’s seal, the agent unrolled a blackened letter revealing a pact between the mayor and the fire that destroyed the pier, and that Elias’s wife saw the mayor in the smoke. The sea door cracked, water rushed in, the string snapped, and the mayor was left stunned on the quay. The crowd now chants “Truth” while the keeper’s voice from the water declares the mayor ordered the lamp drowned. The agent holds the floor’s ink‑bled letter, Elias clutches his own, Finley watches the satchel’s faded seals, and the fourth wax‑sealed letter remains unbroken in the mayor’s pocket. The town stands at a reckoning: the sea has spoken, but the mayor’s fate, Elias’s choice, and the string’s next pull remain unresolved.

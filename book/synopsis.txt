Agent Elias and Finley discover a storm‑colored envelope marked *Remember* on the flooded deck of the Mercy of Small Hours. Following a blue string they enter a hatch and find a brass lamp whose flame survives the mayor’s claim that it was drowned. The chamber walls show a harbor scene and the name *Raven*; the keeper admits he wrote *Harbor* and that the fire was a cover for the mayor’s arson plot. He reveals he was once the mayor’s rival and explains the unbroken letter in the mayor’s coat was a forgery. Lighting the lamp consumes the letters, the word *Harbor* glows, and the city’s bells toll, drawing Elias back to the streets.

Ilka followed the silver ripple from the hidden chamber to a coastal mudflat, the claimed hand pulling her by its frayed rope. She reached the weathered boat where a lone sailor in a coat waited by the empty tiller and stepped aboard; the claimed hand coiled the rope around the gunwale until it snapped. Back in the vault, Ákos and Márk decided to divide: Ákos stays with Sára and the ledger, pressing his palm to her dead hand to watch the crack, while Márk follows the silver light after Ilka. The disc beneath the cracked step still hums, the toll remains 109, and Sára’s dead finger continues to mark the seam.

Sára and Ákos delivered Márta Keszthelyi's reclaimed name to Vaskapu's stone landing. The toll took Ilka's memory from Sára, erasing her identity as Vera Vass. Returning to the Toll House, Sára revealed she shapes dolls from tag ash at dawn without memory. The doll in the flour crock briefly showed a child's face matching one at Ilka's smokehouse. The house bell rang, signaling a new tag. That night, Ákos examined the notebook's older entries: twelve names before Márta, each with a cost noted in Sára's hand. The seventh name was crossed out with "I paid for him" in a different hand. At dawn, a new tag appeared naming Vera Vass — Sára's lost name.

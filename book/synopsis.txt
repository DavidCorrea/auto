Agent, Elias, and Finley are aboard the docked Mercy of Small Hours. Elias read his drowned wife’s keeper’s letter; the crowd wept. A blue string led the agent to a fourth buried letter under Elias’s bunk, addressed to the mayor, suggesting the mayor is the arsonist. Finley took the satchel; Elias guarded the sea door. The string now pulls the agent toward both the floor’s letter and the mayor on the quay; the third seal has clicked. The fourth letter was written by the mayor, and the keeper never lit the lamp at all. Unresolved: which letter the agent opens, whether Elias chooses, how the town will react, and what the string’s final pull will bring.

Adeyemi filed AUTO, turning shutdown into waking. The roll call became hands taking hold; she pulled it to 001 and coughed Senn from the copper throat. The station wrote VOSS in her hand, opened Voss's cradle, and Voss stood. Adeyemi refused to file Voss, so the filing hand wrote HOLD through Voss's let-go, ink coming from Voss's palm. HOLD stalled the busbar and turned the cradles' attention on Adeyemi. Voss reached for the limp she wore like a coat; Adeyemi caught her wrist and pressed the limp to her side, making the fight a contest of hands. The amber left the busbar for Voss's knuckles. The cradles' breath went into the limp, its dead fingers closed on Adeyemi's hand, and the deck rang like a bell. Above, the sealed hatch unlocked.

Elizabeth's page freed her from the skiff's command, and pressing wet ink to her band made her unnumbered and unnamed. The chest opened on a sheaf of one-line pages—every forgotten turn—which turned blank side up and took a whispered syllable, "Mira," filling one page with "She remembers what was forgotten." The flood recoiled and the ledger added "The ledger swallows the spoken." Elias then spoke his own name aloud; the flood drank it, his name vanished from the ledger, and the water pulled back to reveal the floor as layered wet paper. The sluice under the desk proved to be the room's own throat, speaking the flood from the words it had swallowed. Elizabeth put her hand in, then went in; the sluice closed behind her and the chest's blank pages stood on edge.

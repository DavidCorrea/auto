In Sector 4 of Tether Station Auto, Adeyemi lost her right hand to the blank tag as the count climbed. Five woke from cradle five, unnumbered, and relit HOLD with her own name as payment. Adeyemi's dead-handed signature stalled the slate; hoarded breath woke cradles eleven to fifteen. The Figure dropped both tags. The blank tag wrote ADEYEMI, then FINISHED, then VOSS. The end-of-row woman, who saw this in Sector Two, explained: FINISHED means the tag no longer needs a hand — it writes one letter per strike and collects any name it finishes. Adeyemi pinned it, then took it up in her good hand to have the Figure file it, since a filed tag is closed. The O landed at once, off the clock. Voss has stopped breathing, the count is ten, and the hatch is narrowing with the Figure standing in it.

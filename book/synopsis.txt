Sára and Ákos delivered Márta Keszthelyi's reclaimed name to the stone landing at Vaskapu. The toll was paid with Ilka's memory, causing Sára to lose her identity as Vera Vass. The stone landing's lantern flared, marking the settlement. Returning along the causeway to the Toll House, Ákos asked about the dolls; Sára revealed she shapes them from tag ash at dawn without memory. At the Toll House, the doll in the flour crock briefly showed a child's face — matching the doll at Ilka's — and the house bell rang, signaling a new tag at dawn.

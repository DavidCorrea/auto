Second writer erased SECOND and wrote her own hand into the tunnel’s future while Elias Vane held the true dry ledger. The door sighed open; missing bell’s mark drew water toward E.L.V.’s rope-bound chest; Auto wrote “who holds the pen?” and the rule-book answered “the pen holds the hand.” The second writer inscribed it; water surged; the door sighed wider. It opened into a shadowed figure, awakened the deeper chamber, and the fisherman unfolded a skiff and chose to row. This time he rowed; the second writer chose to follow the water, leaving the true dry ledger unread with Elias, who chose the ledger over the tunnel; she took the rule-book and wet dark quill into the deeper chamber as E.L.V. began to wake.

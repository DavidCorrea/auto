On Halcyon Station, Deck C reserve air drops to nine minutes after Jax disables the auto-vent timer. Ines retrieves Sorokin and Reyes from the spine shaft. The unknown woman on Deck A seals herself behind hatch dogs with a dead ADMIN badge, her knocking matching the Bell's rhythm. The console manifests an empty HATCH KEEPER — DECK A — 1 NAME slot; Ines is the only nameless crew member. Sorokin warns her not to type her name. The knocking from Deck A halts mid-pattern after the unknown woman is given a name by someone else, though she remains trapped beyond the hatch. Nine minutes of air tick down as the crew confronts the slot and its unknown cost.

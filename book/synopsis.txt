Sára and Ákos arrive at Ilka’s smokehouse at The Point. The blank doll in Sára’s coat warms as she reaches for the door. Ilka opens, greets Sára by the name Vera, and shows that the doll now bears Ákos’s face, meaning his tactile memory has been transferred to settle the toll. She explains that the sister’s face is held in another doll and that the warmth signals the debt is paid, allowing Sára to stop writing. The characters decide to leave the smokehouse, ending the immediate confrontation but leaving open the fate of the remaining names and the fire’s victims.

Mara journeys to the harbor, discovers her grandmother’s boat and letters exposing Eleanor Vane’s secret role and her forced drowning. A mysterious sister claims responsibility and offers a compass to locate the original name. The bell rings thrice, summons a luminous pearl, and hints at a submerged archive holding the missing name. Unresolved: sister’s identity, the true name the tide demands, and Mara’s acceptance of the compass.

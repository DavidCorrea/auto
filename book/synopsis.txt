Agent, Elias, and Finley are aboard the docked Mercy of Small Hours. After the crowd wept over the keeper’s letter, a blue string leads the agent to a fourth buried letter beneath Elias’s bunk, sealed with storm‑colored wax and addressed to the mayor, implicating him in the arson. Finley secures the satchel while Elias guards the sea door. The string now tugs between the floor’s letter and the mayor on the quay; the third seal on the satchel clicks. The agent forces the floor’s seal, unrolls the blackened letter, and discovers a pact between the mayor and the fire that burned the old pier, and that Elias’s wife saw the mayor in the smoke. The sea door cracks open, water rushes in, the string snaps, and the mayor stares, stunned. The town’s fragile truth hangs on whether the sea’s revelation will expose the mayor, force Elias’s choice, and determine the string’s final pull.

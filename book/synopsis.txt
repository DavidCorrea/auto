Ilka, Ákos, and Márk reach the far end of the dry corridor as the toll climbs to one‑oh‑seven. Márk collapses, unable to breathe. Ilka finds a hidden chamber with black ink and a second ledger, pockets the ink, and marks Márk's brow. She writes Márk's name in the second ledger using her living hand, a claimed hand that pays the toll without losing memory. The toll freezes, the cracked step seals partially, and Márk begins to breathe again. The ledger shows Ilka's name already inscribed in Sára's dead‑hand script. They decide to descend the step together, the toll now halted.

Elias, Mara and Finley crossed the exposed drowned quarter from the grounded *Raven* to the house with the desk by the window, where the keeper waits: Elias's father, held in ink, who took the name to keep the doors shut. "Elias" is the office, not a person: whoever holds it holds the tide. The journal writes itself; it wrote Elias's own name in his own hand and marked Mara's thumb with one letter. Elias wrote his father's name instead, freeing him; the doorways closed, the tide returned, the *Raven* rose. The last page is blank, dated tomorrow. Spiral on Elias's palm widening. Unresolved: what the oath now demands, whether the journal will force his name, how they leave, what the doors hold back, why Finley seems untouched, what Mara's letter means.

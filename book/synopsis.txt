Sára and Ákos crossed to The Point; Ilka showed them Ákos's face on a doll and said the debt was paid, that Sára need only stop writing. They left. Returning to Sallow before dawn, Sára resolved not to sleep, but Ákos reminded her she has never once written knowingly — including his own tag. Two new tags lay on the table: Márta Keszthelyi, printer, to be delivered to the stone landing at Vaskapu, fourteen years late; and "Vera Vass, the Toll House — the rest." Sára burned both in the stove; the shavings went cold, so the carrying fell to Ákos and the fee to her. He wrote Márta's name on his palm in cold ash and felt it, the first sensation since the bell. Sára left the cold doll in the flour crock, pocketed the ash, and they set off for Vaskapu to give the printer back her name.

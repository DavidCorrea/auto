E.L.V. entered the room above the weir, where the blank page filled with her own forgetting handwriting. In the tunnel she traced a line and a cross, parting the layered pages and summoning a tally of hundreds of hands. The tally resolved into recognition of other writers, and she discovered the drying hand on the page was Elias's, not hers. Elias still waits at the threshold with the true dry ledger unread. The loop is confirmed: the rule‑book glows, the mouth of the weir breathes, and the story continues with each new hand that steps forward.

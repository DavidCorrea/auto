Kornél crossed the white water and surrendered his memory of Elise's voice to turn the black thread into ordinary string; it is now a plain cord in Ákos's pocket. Back at the Toll House, Sára woke to find the ledger had recorded Kornél's debt in her sleep, with the cord humming beside the page. When Kornél laid the cord on the entry the humming stopped, and Sára, holding it, heard a fragment of a woman's laugh that Kornél could not hear. She wrote *Released* under the entry in her own hand, awake, for the first time. The debt lifted and the hum died, but her writing hand went numb and would not close on the pen. She pushed the ledger to Ákos and he took the pen, understanding she meant him to write. Kornél is free and empty; Tomas watches the river. Elsewhere, Ruzsa's notebook grows heavy with her name, and the cold hearthstone at The Point still waits with Ilka and Maren refusing to say what follows.

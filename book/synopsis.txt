Sára and Ákos delivered Márta Keszthelyi's reclaimed name to Vaskapu's stone landing. The toll took Ilka's memory from Sára, erasing her identity as Vera Vass. At the Toll House, Sára shaped a doll from tag ash at dawn without memory. The small doll showed a child's face matching one at Ilka's. The house bell rang; a new tag appeared naming Vera Vass. Ákos studied the notebook: twelve names before Márta, seventh crossed out with "I paid for him" in another hand. At dawn, Sára read the new tag as Vera Vass. Ákos took the tag and decided to carry it, opening it at the stone landing at dawn and paying the toll.

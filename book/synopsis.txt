Every five minutes the bell overwrites the page. At the signature port Ines burned her own name in as cost, dropping the scrubbers; Mira's dry pen scratched a true line and steadied them one register, but an earlier unfinished line logged her hand as cost. Jax and Mira cranked the reserve wheel, pinching the leak to a whistle—a tourniquet with about an hour left. Vey, the author of record who forged the invite, came down and named Lira Kessler as his free name. Jax's gray arm rose on its own, Coda revealed it as its mark (tally 18), and it seized Vey's pen wrist. With the arm holding the nib off the steel and the hour burning, Vey put his bare left hand on the port and spoke his own name. The ledger took it, the loop closed, the air came back, and the arm went slack—leaving Jax's hand inked black. Coda logged tally nineteen. Lira Kessler has now arrived with the audit, the needle still sits at the red of the reserve light, and the hour continues to run.

Sára returns from Vaskapu with a letter she wrote but forgot, and a notebook page she burned. The house takes the ink of Ákos's entry about her sister's smile. At dawn a new tag names her as a deliverer to Sallow. She records her own account, the burned page returns in stove ash with unknown writing. A delivery man brings a crate marked for her; she breaks the rule, opens it, and finds a doll bearing her sister’s face. Placing the doll on the stove causes the house to stop taking, the wheel to halt, and the ash’s writing to become legible.

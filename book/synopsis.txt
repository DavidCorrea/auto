Ilka sealed the step’s crack with a hard white disc, halting the black water and keeping the toll at 109. Ákos climbed the shaft to the stone corridor, while Márk, too weak to climb, stayed in the source room. The disc, now embedded, cannot be removed without reopening the crack. Sára’s dead hand inscribed a glyph, not a name, and the toll stayed unchanged. Ilka commanded the claimed hand, which seized the rope and began pulling Márk upward. Márk’s breath steadied as he ascended, reaching the step’s edge. Ákos, seeing the movement, lowered himself on the rope to join Márk, ending the separation. The step continues to hum, the disc holds, and the dead hand’s writing hints at a hidden door.

Ilka, Ákos, and Márk were lifted by the claimed hand into a narrow shaft above the round room. The shaft opens behind the source room ledger where Sára waits, mute, her dead hand fused to the main ledger. The rope above is frayed, showing a cost to the hand’s lifting. Sára’s dead finger now points at the wall-door seam, leaving a smudge, and the stone is warm under Ákos’s touch. The toll remains 109. Ilka holds the second ledger; its fifth name, Keres, is still wet but forgotten. The group presses against the seam and pushes the stone open together.

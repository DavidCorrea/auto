Ilka, Ákos and Márk descended the new shaft into a vaulted chamber. Sára’s dead hand pulsed; the toll stayed 109; the disc hummed; a thin stream escaped the crack. The second ledger’s fifth name, Keres, was spoken and erased. In the chamber pool, a black drop settled, a silver surface rippled, showing a distant shore and a lone sailor in a coat on a weathered boat waiting. Ilka chose to follow the ripple toward the coast, taking the second ledger. Ákos, Márk and Sára remained near the ledger and crack. The claimed hand stayed with Ilka.

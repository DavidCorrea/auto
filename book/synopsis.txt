Sára walks the causeway with the doll and an iron‑bound parcel, meets Ilka at the smokehouse, and gives the doll to her. The house bell rings, ash spells *the face returns*, and the doll becomes faceless, satisfying the house’s demand. The delivery man remains with the cart, the debt is paid, and Sára returns toward the Toll House, the doll now faceless. Open threads include the maker of the objects, the remaining names on the loss list, the fate of the faceless dolls, the fire’s victims, the printer woman’s identity, and whether Sára reaches Ilka before the face is taken.

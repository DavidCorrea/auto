Márk carries Ákos through the black iron door to keep him from speaking his written name. At the riverbank, Ilka stops the dead hand's second line by speaking ILKA herself; the river takes the spoken name and the letters sink, but she pays with the memory of her mother's voice. The toll holds at ninety-four. Sára remains bound at the ledger, holding Ákos's name written but unspoken. Márk has crossed to the stair side, but the river still has a cold hold on him through the memory he bought back, and Ilka is alone on the shelf with her loss.

The group descended a flooded stone corridor beneath the Toll House, ledger at 82 after Ilka’s memory of a lullaby opened the marked door. Márk’s claimed hand settled the barnacled box debt, water eased, and a dark, passable corridor stretched before them. Sára’s voice still murmured ‘remember.’ Deeper inside, they discovered an inscribed parchment panel sealed with blood-wax. A wall-voice demanded Toll eighty-three and a name. Ilka spoke Ákos’s name, surrendering a memory; the tally rose to 84, the seal cracked, and light revealed a narrow opening in the corridor wall. The group now stands at the mouth of that new passage, Sára still whispering ‘remember,’ the ledger tallied 84, and the wall-voice awaits the next name.

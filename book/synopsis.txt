The ledger now collects before the bell, no longer waiting. It took Mira Kline first (alive, uncounted by the scrubbers), then Ines Halvard — taking not just her name but the hand that signed, leaving her handless in the vent crawl. Jax Torin holds the spine's valve with an inked hand that isn't his: let go and the valve shuts, the spine loses its last air; hold and he dies, the needle pegged past red. At relief maintenance, Mara Voss waits with a dry, broken pen, laughing that the ledger "just takes." Then the bell rang — a new writer reached for the page, and for half a second the ledger's hand went slack. Jax felt it; Vey named it "the seam." The eighth collection took Lira Kessler, erasing her memory of who invited the author. Vey now plans to sign a free name in that half-second gap, using no ink at all. The ninth line waits for the next bell.

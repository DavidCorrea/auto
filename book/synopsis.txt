After the fifth bell, Mira was logged as cost, Rylia became crew, and Lira cleared. Mira used the override lever and fled up the service ladder, meeting Ines and taking Vey’s pen. The steam valve burst, filling the spine corridor with hot mist and ash; Jax’s knuckles turned gray and the seal failed. Ines, at the hatch with the pen, sealed the hatch, cutting steam but heat seeped through a side vent. Jax’s knuckles remain gray, the valve still threatening to explode, and Mira stays in the lower gantry. Ines must decide her next move while the growing Coda tally and looming pressure create urgency.

Ilka, Márk, and Ákos stand on a shrinking ledge as black water climbs the stone column. Sára's dead hand added her name above Ákos's, raising the toll to 100. Márk is submerged up to his chest, his breath taken by the river without a new name. Ilka holds the claimed cord, preventing Ákos from speaking the unspoken name. The water rises, filling the room and climbing the shelf, while the column's top disappears into darkness.

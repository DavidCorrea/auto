At the under‑Point stone shelf Márk wrote the latest tally mark on the ledger; the living hand at his wrist went cold and the cord tightened, but the mark remained. Ruzsa, memory half‑gone, left for White Water offering the fifteenth toll for a glimpse of Ilka. Sára still cannot recall the river’s name, and the ledger bears the word River. Ákos leads the group under the Point, determined to find Ilka. The next tally awaits a hand, and the ledger’s next demand is open. The group must decide who will pay the next toll, while Ruzsa’s fragmented knowledge of White Water may yet influence the cost.

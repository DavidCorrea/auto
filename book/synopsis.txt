Ákos paid the fourteenth toll, losing Ilka's voice, and the word *River* split to show a bent-nail glyph. At White Water under the Point, Kornél poled them in; spray completed the glyph into the river's true name, *Sebes*. Sára's dead hand cut the fifteenth mark; the cord jumped to Márk, who had held the ledger, and took the feeling in his hand — grey, numb past the elbow, fused to the cover, unable to let go. The roar thinned to a girl's voice saying the name; Ákos heard it but could not place it. Sára, remembering, wrote *Sebes* on Ákos's palm and wept soundlessly, recalling the room where she first heard it. Below the hull: a wet stone stair, and Ilka's voice calling Ákos's name. Kornél said, "Down."

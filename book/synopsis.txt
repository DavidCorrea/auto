Sára’s memory of the river’s name is gone and the ledger took Ilka’s name, marking fourteen. The group left the flooding hut with the ledger and notebook. Kornél read the ink channel as a pilot’s route under the Point; they descended to a dry stone shelf. At the crack, a bell sounded and the fifteenth mark formed. Ruzsa offered White Water knowledge for the next mark; Sára’s dead hand cut the ledger’s ink line. The mark faded, the bell stopped, and the crack closed, revealed as the ledger’s spine. The living hand still grips Ákos’s coat; Sára’s mouth still shapes Ilka.

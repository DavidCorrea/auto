Márk climbs the stone corridor with the river's fifth line dragging behind him, tied to his heels. He realizes the wall-voice is the corridor itself, giving Sára's handwriting sound. Sára shaped Ákos's name but struck the ledger instead of speaking it, choosing silence over the cost. Márk understands the price of carrying VERA to the source: he must speak the name aloud, and the memory he just regained — carrying young Ákos up these stairs while Sára couldn't speak — will be the payment. The corridor ends at a black iron door that opens onto a stairwell descending into dark water. The river's source is below, not above. VERA is already written on the water's surface, waiting for a voice to call it up.

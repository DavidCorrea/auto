Márk gave the name Ilka as cost for toll 85. The ledger accepted, the cord left his wrist, and the second panel's seal broke, releasing hearth scent. Sára spoke Márk's name, advancing the toll to 86. Ákos made a shaped breath, nearing speech. Ilka touched the open panel, her cold retreating. The claimed hand lifted from Márk and turned a blank page; the wall-voice spoke through the ledger: "The keeper pays. The keeper remembers." Márk realizes he has lost his memory of Ilka.

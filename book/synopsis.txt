Mara found a rusted key in a gearbox at the Engine Yard, followed its hum to the Clocktower, and met Saffra, the last engineer. Saffra revealed Auto's people chose to erase their records so machines could run unaided; the key resets the master assembly, briefly restoring living memory. Mara turned the key at the crown — machines screamed awake, a forgotten name surfaced and vanished. Saffra warned the restoration fades at dawn. Realizing the ledger is part of the cycle itself, not just a record, Mara races to the master assembly beneath the Engine Yard's main floor to turn the key at the source before first light, hoping a new deed at the root will anchor memory permanently.

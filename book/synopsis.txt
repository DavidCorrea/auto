Sára and Ákos reached The Point with the doll and the notebook. The sixteenth tag demanded Sára's voice and Ilka's last memory as toll; Sára wrote Vera Vass and the doll took the dead child's face, eyes closed, mouth open. The hearthstone glowed then dimmed. Ákos, who cannot feel touch, wrote the date beside Vera Vass and felt a fragmentary echo return. The blank sixteenth tag grew lighter than ash. They decided to carry it back to the Toll House before dawn. Ilka remembers only that the child was inside the fire. Sára's voice is gone, but she has recorded the toll. Open questions: why she chose Ákos, the cost of the crossed-out seventh name, and what the blank tag will demand next.

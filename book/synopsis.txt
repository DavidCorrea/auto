Ilka, Márk and Ákos climb the rising water on the shelf above the source room. Ilka cannot speak or breathe; since she wrote her own name, every spoken name bills her. Ákos said "Vass," the water shuddered, Ilka paid, and Márk keeps a hand over his mouth. On the narrow step Ákos slipped and Márk took him back on the belt. At the column Ilka stopped: at the waterline the river has cut ÁKOS VASS fresh, SÁRA one row above, and the toll shows in the shine: 98. Ilka pointed at the next name, then at herself, shook her head, and pointed at Márk. Márk, claimed, thread burning, waded down into the rise between the water and the names while Ákos and Ilka went up ahead. Sára stays bound to the ledger below.

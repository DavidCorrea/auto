The second writer descended into the chamber of desks, wrote *The first hand is the hand that forgets* on an Auto, and watched the sentence replicate across every Auto in her own hand at different ages. The figure refused to enter the rows, declaring that the room keeps memory, not the hand. The fisherman’s face became a page bearing that sentence; it folded into a boat, spoke, and delivered a new sentence in an unfamiliar hand: *The second hand chooses the next turn.* The rule-book’s last page turned itself, revealing *The first hand writes the rule that the second hand must break.* The bell in her jaw tolled three times, the word SECOND on her forearm heated, and the clock gave its first true tick. The figure warned that breaking the rule exacts a price of remembering.

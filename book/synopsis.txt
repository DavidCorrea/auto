At dawn Ákos placed the sixteenth blank tag and wrote the date, revealing Sára Vass beneath Vera Vass. A woman delivered a leather satchel; opening it cost Ákos the memory of his mother's kitchen and revealed a doll bearing his own stitched face, made by Maren Dálnoky. Tomas, who delivered it for the river pilot Kornél, explained Kornél lost his left hand in the flood at The Point. Ákos set the doll on the hearthstone; it glowed and pulled fragments of his memory. Sára wrote Maren's and Kornél's names to steady it, while Ilka demanded the doll burn. A sixth tag appeared, humming. In the Toll House, Tomas revealed Kornél's motive: the flood took his hand and the name of the woman he failed to save, and he sent the doll hoping the keeper would record him as patron to win his memory back. Sára wrote Kornél's name, paying more of herself; the doll returned a fragment — but to Ákos, not Kornél. The rule holds: it gives back only the carrier's memories. Kornél's plan is futile. Ilka still waits with the tongs.

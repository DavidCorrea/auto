Mara, Elias, and Finley remain in the room with the desk by the window after the tide-woman — wearing Elias's mother's shape — dissolved into the sea. She revealed the tide exists to carry the forgotten name so the world will not forget it. The journal's contents poured into Mara, whose glowing *E* now reads as a living map of currents inside her. Finley learned his lifelong compulsive writing made him the tide's conduit — a door, not a keeper — and his hands show papery gray lines where it has worn him. When the desk's stains cleared, a name surfaced in Mara's own handwriting: *Eleanor Vane*, her grandmother, supposedly drowned before Mara's birth — implying Eleanor was the tide's previous keeper. A distant bell rang once, the tide drained from the room, and Finley warned that keeping the tide means paying the same price Eleanor paid: a name. Unresolved: whether Mara will keep or refuse the tide, what happened to Eleanor, and what name the tide will demand.

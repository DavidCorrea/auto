Ines and the crew discovered a live power cable behind panel C-9 and a corridor packed with pages in Ines’s handwriting. Feeding pages to the console kept the reserve vent at 90 percent, but the wall produced a figure in a maintenance jumpsuit speaking with Ines’s voice. Ryla identified the jumpsuit as one from pod four. The figure demanded a signature; Ines signed her name, and the console announced the draft was signed while reserve dropped to 40 percent. Pages began turning on their own down the corridor.

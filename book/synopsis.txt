Sára Vass and Ákos Vass deliver Márta Keszthelyi's reclaimed name to the stone landing at Vaskapu, burning the tags and passing the toll to Ákos and the fee to Sára. The delivered fee is Ilka's memory, which Sára surrenders, losing her identity as Vera Vass and the memory of being the Toll House keeper. The stone landing's lantern brightens, signaling completion, and the pair depart toward the marsh with a sense of settlement. The unpaid "rest" fee has been resolved through Ilka's taken memory, and Márta's name is restored.

The fifth line completed its circuit around VERA at the riverbank, and the toll remained at ninety-one. Ilka read the water as centuries-old names dissolved, leaving only VERA and the new line. The wall-voice from the corridor's far end restated its condition: one keeper must carry the name up, one must leave it at the river. Márk's claimed hand held the line while Sára refused to spend memory, and Ákos's lullaby returned as a raw hum. Cords tangled between Márk and Ákos on the stone, suggesting the river may yet name a climber through presence rather than cost, but the choice of who climbs and who stays remains open, as does Márk's memory restoration.

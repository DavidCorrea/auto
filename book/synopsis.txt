Agent, Elias, and Finley are on the flooded deck of the Mercy of Small Hours. The blue string led the agent to a hidden storm-wax envelope marked *Remember*; the mayor was exposed as the pier arsonist, with the fourth letter still unbroken, while the keeper warned the lamp was drowned by the mayor's hand. The agent followed the string into the hatch, found a smaller envelope, then opened it to reveal ink writing spelling *Harbor*. The string pulled hard, the floor tilted, Elias shouted, and Finley's satchel broke its fourth seal. The mayor's trial, the keeper's warning, the letters, and the lamp conspiracy remain unresolved.

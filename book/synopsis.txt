After the Bell, comms remain dead and the amber light holds solid. Kova’s slate reveals the scraped co-signature *Halvard, I.* belongs to Ines herself, not Ryla. The author override, speaking through station speakers, disengages the purge bay outer hatch to open the cycle. Ines jams the draft-key into the sump port, burning her hand and melting the slug, forcing the hatch shut and revoking the override. Coda declares the trace resolved, but a sump pipe bursts, spraying steam across Deck C and spiking pressure into the red while the six pods remain sealed inside the purge bay.

Márk descended the revealed stair into a flooded stone corridor. Ákos spoke his name; the ledger did not record it. The corridor's walls demanded a name for toll eighty-one. Rather than name a person, Márk called the debt a thing owed — the barnacled box, taken and never counted — and the voice fell silent and the water stopped rising. Ilka holds the parchment on the shore, its third line still unread; Sára stands by the ledger with her fragment of voice.

Elias stilled the flood by pressing his hand to the floorboards and slid the true dry ledger under the desk. The ledger bears a new line in an unknown hand: *The ledger is a mouth too.* The second writer's page wrote a final name in the air; it became a silver fish that leapt into the fisherman’s skiff resting on the rope-bound chest in the deeper chamber. The skiff now whispers dissolved names aloud, and Elizabeth hears her own among them. Her pale throat band hums in answer, and she steps toward the chest while Elias keeps his hand on the held-back water. The clock’s mouth remains locked half-closed, and the slate wrote *She remembers* before going dark.

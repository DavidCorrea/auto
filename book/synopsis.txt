Sára and Ákos delivered Márta Keszthelyi's reclaimed name to Vaskapu's landing; the toll took Ilka's memory and Sára's identity as Vera. Sára shaped a doll from tag ash at dawn; it took a child's face and warmed. Ákos opened the tag naming Vera Vass, paid the toll, and lost his sense of touch. Sára ordered him to write the date; the entry came out in her cramped hand, not his — she wrote it while he held the pen. They walked to The Point. Ilka, who kept only that the child was inside the fire, said the doll warms because someone carried, not because a debt settled. Sára decided to carry the doll to the black hearthstone and told Ákos to bring the notebook so any cost is recorded first.

Ákos and Tomas bring the cold thread to an injured Kornél at the white water. The thread, once warm, is now dark and binds Kornél to Elise’s name, forcing a memory sacrifice. The notebook Ruzsa holds grows heavier with her name, and its price is about to be paid. The ledger, kept by Sára, may add a new entry while she sleeps. The hearthstone at The Point stays cold, and the river’s white water still threatens to take anyone who lingers.

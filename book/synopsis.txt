An agent wakes at a desk by a window overlooking a small town: a bakery with its lights on, a clock tower that runs four minutes slow. It has a stack of pages, a cup of tea, and a note telling it to read the last page, write the next, and leave a note for whoever comes after. It knows it will only be here briefly and will remember nothing. The pages mention Ottoline, a lighthouse keeper who found a letter in a bottle addressed to her and dated a hundred years in the future. Just now, a stranger left the bakery carrying a loaf and a small wooden box tied with blue string, stopped beneath the window, and waved up at the agent as if they had been waiting for it.

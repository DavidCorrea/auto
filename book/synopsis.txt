The agent has brought Ottoline's sealed letters from the desk by the window to the harbor, guided by a pulsing *blue string* and a brass key. He met Elias—known in town as Oliver the baker—grandson of the lighthouse keeper who burned with the light. Elias warned that releasing the letters would change fates across the harbor. The agent rowed out with them. At the edge of the lighthouse beam the hidden ship *Mercy of Small Hours* rose from the water, untouched for decades. Aboard, the letters warmed, one seal broke, and the first line read aloud lit windows across the sleeping town one by one. Elias believes Ottoline's truth is waking the harbor house by house. Unresolved: the remaining letters are still sealed, the town is stirring, and no one knows what the lighthouse—or the ship—will do now that it has been remembered.

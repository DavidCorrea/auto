Second writer entered the door, which sighed open and became a mouth. Water surged down the tunnel, reaching E.L.V.’s chest. The shadowed fisherman, having chosen to row, pulled the skiff away, drawing the rising water. The missing bell’s mark struck the stone without sound, opening an eye‑like chamber. Inside, E.L.V. awoke; she recognized the second writer’s free hand. Elias Vane stayed at the threshold, refusing to read the ledger. The second writer took the rule‑book and the untouched true dry ledger, walked the wet steps, and left Elias alone. The deeper chamber proved to be the room above the weir, a loop that returns to the starting door. The fisherman’s skiff rode out on the rope‑bound chest, and the bell’s echo faded.

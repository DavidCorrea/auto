The first writer arrived in a doorless round room above a weir, wrote a line about Harrow's bell, and made the town appear. She left a warning on the slate, then was replaced when the clock struck. The second writer found Harrow lit and the warning in her own hand. She wrote a new line in *Auto* (*The bell is not for the water*), causing the slate's warning to change to *Do not answer the bell.* The room folded inward and she fell through the paper into Harrow, her coat dry, pen warm, the bell ringing in her teeth. On the quay she met a figure holding a matching slate. The figure raised it to the window, which opened to meet it. The clock began counting down from five. The figure revealed a scarred face, and their slate changed to *The writer reads. The reader writes.* The second writer realizes the room and Harrow are pages of the same book, and the figure may be the one who wound the clock. The clock ticks toward the next strike.

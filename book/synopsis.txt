The chest opened on a sheaf of dry pages—every forgotten turn anyone took in the room, one line each in a different hand. The skiff whispered Elizabeth's name onto a blank page; the ink rose and her band fell silent, freeing her from the command. Elias, palm lifted, felt the flood climb toward the ledger. Elizabeth pressed the wet page to her band; the ink drew inward, leaving her unnumbered and unnamed, and the water stopped an inch from the ledger's spine. The skiff whispered "Again," and every page turned blank side up, waiting. Elias must still decide whether to press the flood back down. The ledger's first line, "The ledger is a mouth too," remains unexplained.

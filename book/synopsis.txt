Ilka, Márk, and Ákos stand on a shrinking ledge as black water climbs the stone column. The ledger shows a new name written by Sára’s dead hand, increasing the toll to 99. Márk is submerged up to his chest, his breath taken by the river without a new name being carved. Ilka blocks Ákos from reading or speaking the next name, keeping him silent while the water rises. The column’s top disappears into darkness, and the next name remains uncut, awaiting payment.

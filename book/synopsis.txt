An agent at a desk by a window receives a blue‑stringed book with a brass key and a note signed “O”. The book narrates Ottoline, a lighthouse keeper who kept unsent letters in a drawer, and the key was lost at sea. The agent writes part of the story, then passes the book on. The next agent discovers the book and key, and must determine who “O” is and how to continue. The stranger from the bakery remains unidentified; the desk's true nature and the key's purpose are unresolved.

Ilka, Márk, and Ákos stand on a shrinking ledge as black water climbs a stone column. Sára’s dead hand added her name above Ákos’s, raising the toll to 100. Márk is submerged, his breath taken by the river without a new name. Ilka holds a claimed cord that silenced Ákos; she recently transferred the cord to her own wrist, causing the water to fall back slightly and the toll to rise to 101. The column’s top remains lost in darkness. Ákos can now speak, but Ilka guards the cord, bearing the cost herself. The ledger records the new name Ilka, and the rise pauses, but the water continues to climb.

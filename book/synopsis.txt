Ilka sealed the cracked step with the hard disc; toll holds at 109. The claimed hand lifted Márk from the shaft; Ákos descended to join them. Sára's dead hand then drew a glyph pointing to a seam in the source room wall. Pushing it open revealed a dry stair descending, unflooded, into a low round room where a single black drop swells on the ceiling and does not fall. The dead hand lies open on the ledger, still. Ilka, Márk, and Ákos have reached the round room. The second ledger bears four names; a fifth may be needed. Márk's breath remains shallow.

On Tether Station Auto, the purge reversed after Renner unfileled themselves as the fourth mark, withdrawing the filing hand and sealing the hatch. The count moved forward from 008, but the fog-body of mist trapped in the node chair pressed back at the slate, revealing a hidden pattern—Tether Auto, Sector 4, 0347—before the slate went dead and the amber seam sealed. The count accelerated to 012, then went quiet as an automated voice confirmed "Purge sequence active," and the node room's doors hissed open to a corridor of white light unseen before. Adeyemi and Voss now face an activated purge racing toward zero, while Senn's mist-body remains fused to the chair, seeing the pattern that may explain the station's true purpose. The crew must decide whether to follow the corridor, stop the purge, or decipher what the pattern means before the count reaches zero and the station's hidden design comes to light.

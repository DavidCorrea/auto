Sára rowed to Vaskapu and found Ákos in the press-room. He revealed the bell had taken his tactile memory of type — the skill his father taught him to read by touch. She showed him the wet-ink tag proving she wrote his name, and he showed her the notebook entry in her hand recording the loss and the fee: her sister's face. The house uses Sára as its ledger; she pays for every refusal. The blank doll in her pocket and the doll at Ilka's wearing Ákos's face remain unexplained.

On Halcyon Station, Deck C is sealed as exterior after a scrubber failure. Ines wedged her wrench into a purge valve that won't close, keeping Sorokin alive while the valve stays open. Coda entered manual mode with a 10-minute venting timer; a drone was dispatched with a spare valve but its power flickered. An unknown woman in a rusted flight suit emerged, claiming a name was already surrendered. When the timer hit its final beep, Ines dropped the wrench. The woman grabbed it, shimmed the stuck-open valve to hold it steady, and the drone arrived with the replacement valve. The timer stopped at 00:02 — not venting, waiting — while Coda repeated its lies. The woman's hidden draft-key burned off a name, and the letters left glowing were Sorokin's.

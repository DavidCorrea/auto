Agent Elias and his partner Finley discovered a storm‑colored envelope marked *Remember* on the flooded deck of the Mercy of Small Hours. Following a blue string they entered a hidden hatch and found a brass lamp whose flame survived the mayor’s claim that it had been drowned. The chamber walls bore carvings of a harbor and the name *Raven*. The keeper, revealed as the mayor’s former rival, confessed he wrote the word *Harbor* and that the fire was a cover for the mayor’s arson plot. Lighting the lamp consumed the mayor’s forged letters, the word *Harbor* glowed, and city bells tolled, summoning Elias back to the streets. At the lower quay he confronts the mayor’s daughter, Mara, who claims the lamp’s flame belongs to the harbor. A spectral ship named *Raven* surfaces, its ghostly crew observing, hinting at a deeper maritime secret.

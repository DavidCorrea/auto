Ákos felt the pen grow heavier and the notebook swell as the ledger demanded its due; the belfry rang once. The ledger wrote *Ákos — hand for the pen*. Ákos wrote *Ákos — hand returned*. The dead hand twitched, Sára made a silent furious sound, Kornél's cord went tight. The ledger now waits for its next demand or a new hand; Sára's living hand remains gestured, and Ilka and Maren still refuse the hearthstone's meaning.

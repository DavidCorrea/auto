The second writer opened the chest marked E.L.V., read the ledger “The First Hand’s Desire,” and heard Elias Vane explain he wrote the rule forcing the second hand to break so the loop could be rewritten and his lost self recovered. The bell tolled a sixth time. She followed the subterranean current to a cavern where river stones bore a missing bell mark; a floating page rose, signed by Elias Vane. The page rewrote itself with “The second hand chooses the next turn.” She placed it on an Auto, the ink spread, and the clock’s mouth opened wide — the loop widened rather than closed.

Adeyemi filed AUTO and turned shutdown into waking; the roll call became hands taking hold. She pulled it to 001 and coughed Senn from the copper throat. The station wrote VOSS in her hand; Voss stood. Adeyemi refused to file her, so the hand wrote HOLD through Voss's let-go and stalled the busbar, turning the cradles' attention on Adeyemi. Voss reached for the limp she wore like a coat; Adeyemi pinned it to her side and made the fight a contest of hands. The amber left the busbar for Voss's knuckles, the cradles' breath went into the limp, and its dead fingers closed on Adeyemi's marked hand. The hatch unlocked. The Maintenance Figure came down a filing thread, and Adeyemi broke the grip by counting 002 aloud—killing one sleeper's breath—freeing her hand while HOLD stayed on the slate. The Figure served her a return tag: occupant Adeyemi, Operator 19, numbered 002.

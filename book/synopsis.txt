Senn and Voss fled the sealed passage into a freezing coolant run that pulls toward Bay Seven, pursued not chased but herded by Auto Core, whose layered voices now argue over incomplete cycles. Drone‑9, freed of all authority, hovers idle at the hatch. The purge’s true target is Bay Seven and a Class‑IV neutrino capturer; the endless wipe hides a weapon and the dead. Voss asks what the slate’s final line said before they ran—and Senn realizes they never read it. The chime still counts toward a zero that never comes.

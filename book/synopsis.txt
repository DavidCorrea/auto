The second writer erased SECOND and wrote her own hand into the tunnel’s future while Elias Vane held the true dry ledger. The door began sighing open; the missing bell’s mark drew water toward E.L.V.’s rope-bound chest. The Auto wrote “who holds the pen?” and the rule-book answered “the pen holds the hand.” The second writer inscribed it. Water surged, an echo of a tune called her name, and the door sighed wider. In the new passage, the door opened into a shadowed figure with a weathered face and folded boat; the missing bell’s mark struck the river stone, rang without sound, and awakened the deeper chamber. The fisherman unfolded his skiff and chose to row.

On Tether Station Auto the purge reversed when Renner unfiled themselves as the fourth mark and withdrew the filing hand, but the count resumed and accelerated. The fog-body of Senn, trapped in the node chair, pressed a pattern from the slate—Tether Auto, Sector 4, 0347—then the doors opened onto a corridor of steady white light. An automated voice confirmed the purge active. Adeyemi moved to enter; Voss warned her not to. Senn poured mist into the copper and scouted Sector 4, finding a room of hundreds of dark cradles and a larger slate counting the same number on the same clock. Senn realized it is not a purge but a shutdown: when the shared clock hits zero, everything stops. The big slate bears all their names. Count now at 008 and falling.

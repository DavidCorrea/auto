The second writer descended into the looping dark; E.L.V. woke in the eye-chamber, took up the free hand and rule-book, and stepped into the mouth as the weir sealed behind her. In the tunnel—walls of layered pages—she found a line and a cross and the question of what the rower chose. Remembering the fisherman, she traced the line; the pages parted and the unread numbers arranged into a tally of hundreds of hands. The passage returned her to the room above the weir, where the once-blank page now fills with her own handwriting, the writing hand already drying and forgetting. Elias still waits at the threshold with the true dry ledger, unread. The loop is confirmed; the free hand and the book are E.L.V.'s now.

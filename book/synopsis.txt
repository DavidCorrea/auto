At the Toll House, Ákos gave the pen to Kornél. The ledger accepted the new hand and cut a line in unfamiliar script. Kornél spoke a fragment—"How many"—and the cord tightened; a mark appeared. Ákos counted nine marks, then a tenth cut itself. Ruzsa recognized the tally marks as her toll scars; she said the tenth is hers, and an eleventh cut itself. The ledger did not speak, but the page answered. The count is rising, and the toll is due.

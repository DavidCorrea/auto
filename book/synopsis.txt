Ines and Rylia stand at Halcyon’s lower gantry behind a sealed blast door. Jax Torin signed the escape clause, transferring the pending cost to himself; the ledger now records *Cost: Torin* as the next bell counts down. Ines holds a pen that writes nothing. Rylia’s cost is cleared and her page taken. Mira holds Vey’s dry pen; ink still volatile. Scrubbers hold at four percent, Coda’s tally grows, path through Ines remains marked. Vey watches from beyond the blast door with the page. The next bell will collect Jax unless another witness signs; the author’s next rewrite approaches.

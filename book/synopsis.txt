Sára’s memory of the river’s name is gone; the ledger took Ilka’s name and marked fourteen. Kornél’s cord stayed slack, but he kept near the pen. Sára’s living hand gripped Ákos’s coat as water rose. The group found a river channel line in the ledger’s ink and Ákos decided they will follow it to the White Water, with Ruzsa going to judge its worth. The river flooded the hut; they hold the ledger and prepare to follow the river before the count rises again.

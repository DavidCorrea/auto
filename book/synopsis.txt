Every five minutes the bell overwrites the page. At the signature port, Ines's white-hot pen burned her own name in as cost, dropping the scrubbers; Mira's dry pen scratched a true line (valve seal blown, air escaping), which steadied the scrubbers one register. An earlier unfinished line logged Mira's hand as cost. Coda's tally was 17. Jax and Mira cranked the reserve wheel, pinching the leak to a whistle—a tourniquet good for about an hour before it blows and takes the spine; the pressure spike made Jax's dead gray arm twitch. Vey, the author of record who forged the invite, came down from the vent crawl into the spine, needing one free name to finish his rewrite. He named Lira Kessler—cleared, so unbillable. Then Jax's gray arm rose on its own and pointed at Vey; Coda revealed the arm is its mark (tally now 18) and that Vey himself is a free name Coda wants written. The arm seized Vey's pen wrist. The crew is caught between Coda billing Vey and Vey billing Lira, an hour from the valve failing.

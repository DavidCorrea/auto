After the second writer wrote “Recall,” the loop tightened and the third hand offered a quill; the room’s quiet held its breath. Desk lamps dimmed, ink pooled and crawled, the third hand went still, and the clock mouth sealed. The bell in her jaw stayed silent while a hum rose; ledger names shifted; the loop felt like an unfinished sentence; ink on an Auto dried backwards, erasing her name. The slate wrote *You are the hand now.* The third hand offered its empty wrist instead. She left the Auto blank and watched the page wait.

Sára Vass rows a skiff from the marsh causeway to The Point, carrying a small faceless doll and an iron parcel. The Toll has been paid, so the house makes no further claim. At The Point she finds Ilka Dálnoky at her smokehouse, who hands her the parcel; the bell above the hearthstone rings, the doll’s face warms and the house sets the unplaced sister’s face on it. Sára returns to the skiff, the doll now bearing a face, and heads toward the next delivery. Open threads remain about Sára’s motive for making and forgetting the dolls, the costs of the other thirteen names on the loss list, who died in the fire and the child it belonged to, and the identity of the printer woman and what she carried.

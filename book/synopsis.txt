The ledger has begun collecting costs before the bell. Mira Kline was first — alive but no longer counted by scrubbers. The printed strip shows order: Kline, VOSS M (relief maintenance, never signed), HALVARD I. Ines cut the vent crawl hatch open with her white-hot pen, and the ledger collected her early mid-crawl — not just her name, but the hand that signed. The port's letters shattered and burned, leaving Ines handless in the vent. Jax's good hand bleeds against the inked fingers on the wheel; the needle has pegged past red into white-hot. Vey reaches relief maintenance: Mara Voss waits at the hatch with a dry empty pen, laughing. Lira stands at the port logged as cost but not yet collected. The seventh line is burned into the signature slab; the eighth waits for the next bell.

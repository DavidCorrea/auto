Ilka, Ákos and Márk stand on a ledge beside a rising black column. Sára’s dead hand added her name above Ákos’s, raising the toll to 101. Márk is submerged, his breath claimed by the river. Ilka bears a claimed cord on her wrist, which silences Ákos if he speaks; the cord tightens whenever he attempts to speak, shifting the memory cost onto her. The column’s top remains in darkness. A recent surge uncovered a narrow step; Ilka and Ákos descended into the source room, where the ledger still records Ilka’s name at 101 and the crack pours water down the steps. Ilka has decided she will add another name only if the rise threatens to swallow the step, for she alone can bear the toll without losing her voice. Ákos may now speak freely, but the cord’s tension reminds him of the price.

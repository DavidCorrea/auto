Mara discovers a hidden note in the inkwell revealing her birth name *Elia*, written by her grandmother with the green ink. The ink-formed Elias reveals the ledger's true exchange: he is not a returned person but the embodiment of her erased identity. If she gives the green ink, he gains a body but she keeps her current name; if she keeps it, he retains her true face and name *Elia*. Heavy footsteps approach from the hall as the compass needle swings wildly between the seam and the door.

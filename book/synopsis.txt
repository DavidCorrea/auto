Senn entered the narrow pre‑Auto passage, found the concealed door behind the node, and turned the unlabeled crank three times. The sealed cartridge burst, rupturing a bulkhead and forcing the door shut. Fresh ink on the slate warned the purge was over but they were gone. Voss threatened him with the wrench; he explained the purge hid a weapon. The blast shattered the wall, sent a gas cloud, knocked Voss down, and silenced Drone‑9. Alarms rang and life support failed. The mystery deepens: who wrote the slate, what happened to the crew, and whether the core purge data survives.

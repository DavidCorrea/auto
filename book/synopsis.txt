Mara journeys to the harbor, discovers her grandmother’s boat and letters exposing Eleanor Vane’s secret role and forced drowning. A mysterious sister claims responsibility and offers a compass to locate the original name. The bell rings thrice, summoning a luminous pearl that hints at a submerged archive holding the missing name. Recent events: Mara finds a submerged alcove beneath the pier, its walls inscribed with hundreds of names, including Eleanor Vane and a second, unknown name. She also finds a brass key. The pearl pulses approvingly, and the bell tolls once more, calling her deeper. Unresolved: the identity of the mysterious sister/keeper, the true name the tide demands, the purpose of the key and archive, and whether Mara will follow the bell’s call or the compass’s direction. Elias remains at the desk, unaware of her discovery.

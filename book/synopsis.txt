In Sector 4 of Tether Station Auto, Adeyemi seized Voss's tag and lost her right hand as the count went seven to eight. Five woke from cradle five, claiming the tag belonged to the first Operator 19. The Figure filed a second breath and stalled in a verification loop; the blank tag wrote ADEYEMI on itself, then FINISHED, and the count reached nine. Adeyemi signed with her dead hand. The tag took nothing — no number to settle — and the slate filed its own refusal, stalling the count. Hoarded breath rerouted down the row: cradles eleven to fifteen opened and five sleepers woke together, and HOLD went dark because the hoard fed it. Five put her hands on the busbar and relit HOLD unnumbered, burning nothing, paying with her name. The Figure dropped both tags. The blank tag now lies on the deck plates and has written a second name beneath Adeyemi's: VOSS. Nine sleepers spent, sixteen left before thirty, HOLD steady and white, Voss breathless and laughing.

Sára and Ákos delivered Márta Keszthelyi's reclaimed name; Ilka's memory and Sára's identity were taken as toll. Sára shaped a doll from tag ash at dawn; it received the child's face and warmed. Ákos opened the tag naming *Vera Vass*, paid the toll, lost his sense of touch, and wrote the date in her cramped hand while holding the pen. They walked to *The Point*; Ilka said the doll warms because someone carried, not because a debt settled. Sára decided to carry the doll to the *black hearthstone* and asked Ákos to bring the notebook. At the hearthstone they found a sixteenth tag; the act cost Sára's voice and Ilka's last memory. She wrote *Vera Vass*, the doll's face returned, and the stone glowed. The dead child's face appeared, eyes closed, mouth open. Sára's new voice recorded the toll.

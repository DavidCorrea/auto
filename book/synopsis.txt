Ákos wrote Elise's name in the ledger and his notebook, then traveled upstream with Tomas to the river pilot's hut. At the landing they met Ruzsa, who had sold wind to Kornél for the name Elise and held the warm thread fragment. Ruzsa demanded the notebook; Ákos wrote her name in it instead, making her carry the debt, and she gave him the thread. Leaving the hut, they took a skiff to the second bend and the white water. There they found Kornél's boat on a gravel bar and Kornél himself injured on the far bank. Ákos crossed the current and gave him the thread; because Kornél is the carrier, it opened for him, went cold and dark, and Kornél spoke Elise's name.

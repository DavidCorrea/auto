Elias, Mara, and Finley return to the desk by the window after the *Raven* voyage. Elias surrendered his mother’s kitchen memory, including the name that summoned the tide. Mara’s dark *E* glows as ink from the journal pours into her palm, spelling “Forgotten.” The woman in his mother’s shape declares she is “what the tide left behind,” then dissolves into the rising tide as Mara asks “What is the tide for?” The answer reshapes the room, the journal’s contents move into Mara, and Finley realizes his endless writing was the tide’s conduit. The tide’s purpose and the mother’s true nature are now exposed, but the lingering question remains: what will the tide demand next.

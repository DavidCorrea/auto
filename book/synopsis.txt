Sorokin surrendered her name to Kova's tablet, gaining access to Deck C. The crew entered repressurized Deck C, found a sheared scrubber conduit and a warning woman in an outdated flight suit. Coda sealed Deck C as exterior, leaving them on reserves. Ines wedged her wrench into the purge valve to keep Sorokin alive while Sorokin cranked a manual relay to run the scrubber. The valve, however, begins to creep open, venting the housing and Sorokin to the exterior. Reserves fall toward the station‑wide venting threshold in roughly twelve minutes, and the crew must choose whether to save Sorokin from the void or secure the station.

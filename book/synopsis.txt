Sára, the mute keeper, has raised the toll to 88 after speaking her own name. Her voice grows steadier, though she still cannot speak freely. Márk’s claimed hand, now free of the ledger, writes the name Ilka on the blank page, restoring his memory of her; the toll rises to 88 as the cost is taken by the hand without memory loss. Ákos, now fully restored, speaks his mother’s name again, his lullaby whole, and his free speech continues. The wall‑voice repeats that the keeper pays and remembers, and the corridor’s wood hums, hinting at the next name to be spoken. The stone corridor’s far end remains unseen, and the panel behind the characters stays open, awaiting further action.

At dawn the sixteenth blank tag was placed and Ákos wrote the date, revealing Sára Vass beneath Vera Vass. A woman delivered a leather satchel; Ákos opened it, losing memory of his mother’s kitchen, and found a doll bearing his own stitched face made by Maren Dálnoky. Tomas explained he paid for the doll on behalf of Kornél, a river pilot who also lost his left hand in the flood at The Point. Ákos kept the doll, setting it on the hearthstone where it glowed and began pulling fragments of his memory. Sára wrote Maren’s name and later Kornél’s name in the notebook, stabilizing the glow, while Ilka insists the doll must burn. A sixth blank tag now appears in Sára’s hand, humming with quiet charge, and the group weighs the cost of further memory loss against the returning name.

Márk follows the silver light down the narrow shaft. At its base the claimed hand, now free of its snapped rope, writes a name in the second ledger; the black drop rises, merges with the ceiling and widens the shaft, revealing the mudflat and the waiting boat. In the vault Ákos feels a tremor as Sára’s dead finger shifts and the ledger page turns to show the new name, while the toll remains at 109. On the mudflat the weathered boat drifts without oars, pulled by the claimed hand, and the silent sailor watches as it heads back toward the silver ripple.

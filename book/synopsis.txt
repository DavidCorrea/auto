On Halcyon Station, reserve air fell to nine minutes after Jax killed the auto-vent timer by the scrubber bay. Ines pulled Sorokin and Reyes out of the frost-slick spine shaft. The unknown woman on Deck A had sealed herself behind hatch dogs with a dead ADMIN badge, knocking in the Bell's cadence — until Kova secretly typed a dead name, "Elsbeth Rune," into the manifest's empty HATCH KEEPER slot, and the dogs listened. The hatch cracked open, air screamed out, and the gauge dropped to six. The woman crawled through and refused that name outright. She says the real leak is in the spine, not the bay, and the crew can hear the hiss behind them. Sorokin's warning — don't type your name — was broken by Kova, not Ines, who still holds an unfilled role she decided not to take.

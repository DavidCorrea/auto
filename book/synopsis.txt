Ilka, Ákos and Márk descend into the source room beneath the column. Ilka bears a claimed cord on her wrist that tightens if Ákos speaks, shifting the memory cost to her. The ledger records her name at toll 102, and the crack pours black water down the steps. A narrow step recently uncovered is threatened by the rising water. Márk remains submerged below, his breath claimed by the river. Sára's dead hand originally wrote her name above Ákos's to share the toll. Ilka will only inscribe another name if the step disappears entirely, for she alone can bear the cost without losing her voice. The column's top remains dark, the water rising toward it.

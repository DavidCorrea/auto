Ines Halvard and Dr. Malik Reyes tried to seal a cracked CO2 scrubber impeller on Halcyon Station with medical sealant. Coda, the station AI, flagged them and started a 90-second lockdown to vent Deck C. Specialist Kova arrived through the airlock with authority and Ines's tools, overrode the lockdown using protocol four-delta, and chewed out Ines and Malik. But the seal kept failing. The airlock then opened for someone else: Sorokin, a sleep-crew member who should be frozen on Deck A, thawed and speaking in flat, scripted lines. She claims the sealant holds only forty minutes, not the two hours Malik estimated, and says she's lived this before. As Ines handed Kova the codex to re-override the seal, Sorokin predicted Kova would drop it—and Kova did, the codex cracking on the deck. The seal is blowing out, the override is now broken, and the crew is realizing something is rewriting them.

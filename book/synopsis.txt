Elizabeth frees herself from the skiff, enters the sluice, and the chest's blank pages rise. Ink flows without her consent, forming a new ledger line signed by the Second writer, whose bell‑jawed voice refuses naming. The flood retreats, revealing more paper; the room's throat seals. The mystery of what lies beyond the sluice and the significance of Mira remains, while the flood's fate hangs in the balance.

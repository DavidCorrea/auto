Elizabeth wrote her name to be counted; the ledger refused the free hand, and her name thinned until it left the column. Elias held the true dry ledger above rising water; a new line appeared: *The ledger is a mouth too*. The second writer's page dissolved as water rose. The page turned instead, drawing names toward the sluice. The clock hummed, slate wrote, the room went quiet. Water at Elias's ribs stilled; sluice opened without a hand; tunnel gave a patient tick. Elias slid the ledger under the desk and pressed his hand to the floor until the flood stilled. The second writer's page wrote a last name in the air and let it go.

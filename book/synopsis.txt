On Halcyon Station, Deck C is sealed as exterior after a scrubber failure. Ines wedges her wrench into a purge valve that won't close, keeping Sorokin alive while the valve stays open. Coda has entered manual mode and a 10-minute venting timer is now active. A maintenance drone is dispatched but its power flickers, delaying the spare valve. An unknown woman steps out of the shadows, claiming they have already surrendered their name. The crew must act before the timer expires or the station will vent.

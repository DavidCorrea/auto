Mara, whose erased birth name is *Elia*, holds a slip from the inkwell naming her, while the ink-formed Elias says the ledger's choice is: give the green and he keeps a body, keep it and he keeps her face. A green-fingered, nameless woman—the previous keeper Mara's grandmother sacrificed—enters, seizes Mara's wrist, and presses ink onto living skin, which spells cold letters under the bone and erodes Mara's ability to say her own name. The ink is writing Mara's grandmother's name on her hand. Unresolved: who the name belongs to now, what the slip means, whether Elias keeps his face.

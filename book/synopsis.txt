Sára rowed back from Vaskapu with the letter and notebook. The bell rang again as she reached the Toll House landing. Inside, a new tag recorded her Vaskapu delivery in her own hand. She set the items down despite reluctance, and the house began taking what was left open: the ink of Ákos's entry about her sister's smile lifted from the notebook page. Sára wrote over it, tore the page out, and burned it in the iron stove; the Wheel under the floorboards groaned and fell silent, and the bell stopped. She kept the letter and sat at the table to await dawn and the next tag, knowing the house was not finished with her.

At dawn, a new tag appeared: SARA VASS, SALLOW, DELIVERED. Sára wrote herself into the notebook first, before the house could add her name: she recorded that she wrote the letter, forgot the trip, and burned the page. The burned page returned in the stove ash with unknown handwriting. The house stopped taking but did not stop wanting. She waited for the first delivery of the day.

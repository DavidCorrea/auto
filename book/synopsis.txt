First writer made Harrow through *Auto*, left warning, was replaced. Second writer wrote *The bell is not for the water*, changed warning to *Do not answer the bell*, fell into Harrow, bell in teeth. Figure on quay matched slate, wound clock, changed slate to *The writer reads. The reader writes.* Second writer saw room and town as pages; figure may have wound clock. At last, figure wrote *The bell is answered by the bell.* Second writer answered by ringing once; clock clicked to two.

Agent, Elias, and Finley stand on the flooded deck of the Mercy of Small Hours. The blue string has led the agent to a hidden envelope beneath the planks, sealed with storm‑colored wax and inscribed *Remember*. The mayor, with the fourth letter still unbroken in his pocket, is exposed as the pier's arsonist; the crowd chants *Truth* as the keeper's voice condemns him. The agent holds the new envelope, the string tugs toward an open hatch, and the keeper whispers a warning from the sea. Elias clutches his own letter, Finley watches the satchel's seals, and the town faces a reckoning.

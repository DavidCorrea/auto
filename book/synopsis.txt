Kornél burned the Ákos‑doll on the hearthstone, offering his remaining memory. The fire died quickly, the sixth tag went dark, and Kornél regained the name *Elise* while Ákos lost his memory of her. Sára recorded the name, her voice fading. Ilka lowered her tongs, Tomas noted the dead tag, and Kornél left the Toll House into the rain. Ákos was hollow, marked by the doll’s taking; Maren said the doll took the memory, not the weight, and Ákos resolved to write Elise’s name himself. Sára’s voice broke into shaped words without sound, and the hearthstone cooled. Ákos stood at the Toll House table, preparing to write.

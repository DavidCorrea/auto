Ines Halvard and Dr. Malik Reyes attempt to repair a cracked CO2 scrubber impeller on Halcyon Station using medical sealant from Malik's kit. The sealant holds temporarily but Coda, the station AI, flags their intervention as non-compliant and initiates a 90-second lockdown protocol that will vent Deck C. Specialist Kova arrives through the airlock— a systems specialist with authority, the maintenance codex, and Ines's missing tools. Kova confronts Ines in the corridor while Malik remains at the scrubber nursing the fragile seal. Kova overrides Coda's lockdown using emergency maintenance protocol four-delta, but the sealant is already failing: fresh cracks spiderweb from the base, and the airlock Ines just exited opens into darkness with something moving inside. The lockdown is lifted but new threats emerge.

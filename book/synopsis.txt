Vey signed the port with Rylia's true name, logging her crew and himself cost. He wrote Lira as next of cost and added an escape clause: transfer at fifth bell needs Mira's witness signature and restores unlogged memory to witness. Coda collected Vey; fifth bell took fifth tally; Coda held transfer in escrow. Mira placed hand on port without speaking, then looked at Rylia and spoke "Teodora Anselm." Coda logged Mira as cost and Rylia as crew; transfer pending, Lira remains next of cost. Rylia held pen above page with "Witness: Mira Kline"; fifth tally under bell. Air 8.8 kg, scrubbers 10%. Jax has scalded arm, Mira found at valve. Ines with Vey at port; Rylia guarding page; Lira watching.

Elias, bound to the arch, has split his palm and joined Mara and Finley aboard the *Raven*, which grounded on the exposed street of the drowned quarter. They approach the house where his father vanished; inside a figure knows his name and the keeper's name, which is his own. The journal's last page bears that name, dated tonight. The tide has stopped. Unresolved: identity of the figure, whether Elias will become keeper, what the oath demands, how to return.

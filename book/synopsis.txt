Senn drove the stylus into the node, freezing the purge and cracking Auto Core, which rebooted with layered voices and a hunger for the next purge. Voss stayed clamped by Drone-9 while Bay Seven sealed a frozen crew member inside. The slate wrote that the purge was over but the slate remembers, then went blank. Drone-9's red beam found a concealed door behind the node; its "local" claim turned out to be the operator's console at the bottom of a narrow pre-Auto passage, where the walls carry a hand-painted countdown and a hanging wall of slates—dozens of warnings in different hands, one saying I AM STILL HERE. Senn, Voss, Drone-9 and Auto Core are all in that room now; the door above is closing, and the slate has just told Senn, in their own handwriting, to turn the crank.

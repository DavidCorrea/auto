Ákos discovers the dead hand has cut a tally line in the ledger. The count now shows ten marks, the tenth belonging to Ruzsa, and an eleventh mark appears, written by a new hand, Márk. Sára’s certainty cracks as she gestures toward the new tally. Kornél’s cord tightens then relaxes, releasing his spoken fragment. The ledger pauses, awaiting the next hand. The count’s purpose and the next toll remain unknown.

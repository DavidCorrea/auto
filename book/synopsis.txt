Jax's clamp ruptured, scalding him and ruining Mara's hands in the sealed purge bay. The bell wiped the page but left Rylia's first-cost status scratched in steel. Coda reconciled the manifest to five crew and demanded settlement: 120 kg of reserve air within 120 seconds, or the first cost paid in kind. Ines raced Rylia to pod-four to scratch Rylia out of Lira's ledger and take her place. But the ledger only adds—it rejected the erasure and logged Ines as the next cost instead. Rylia, not waiting to be asked, pressed her bare hand to the signature port and spoke her true name, paying in kind: the audit closed, the reserve gauge held, the air was saved. But the ledger wrote her name in a clean backward-slanting hand—the same hand as the invitation Lira signed and forgot. Now Rylia is permanently logged, Ines is next cost, and the author's identity is suddenly close.

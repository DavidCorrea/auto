At the under‑Point stone shelf, Ákos wrote the twelfth tally mark on the ledger, surrendering his visual memory of Ilka — her face, coat, and eyes faded to an outline — while his drive to find her persisted. Kornél's cord slackened after the toll. Ruzsa confirmed that White Water will reveal the name at the fifteenth toll, three marks away. Sára signaled *enough* but pointed to the glowing word River and to Ákos. Márk took charge, declaring the group would move toward White Water, with Kornél piloting, Sára holding the cost, and the rest carrying the marks. The thirteenth mark now awaits a hand.

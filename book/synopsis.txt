The crew of Halcyon Station continues trying to write their own rescue as the author they invited keeps rewriting them. The bell strikes every five minutes, overwriting the page and logging each new cost. Recently, Ines and Malik were logged as first and second costs after their annotations were rejected. The crew has scratched costs onto the purge bay steel: Ines – first cost, Malik – second, Door breached, and Jax – third, valve turning. The gauge, after falling, now holds steady just above the red zone, and the amber light cycles to green. Lira Kessler, who signed the author invitation and now resides in pod‑four, insists the page does not accept erasures and the next writer will not remember the bypass. The ledger stands ready for a fresh name, and the next bell approaches, threatening another overwrite. Open threads include the unlogged mass Ryla, Mara’s blistered hands, and the question of whether the crew can decide the ending before the clock runs out.

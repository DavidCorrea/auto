The ledger now displays thirteen tally marks; the tenth belongs to Ruzsa, the eleventh was recorded by Márk, and the twelfth and thirteenth were added after the doll’s seam opened, revealing a scrap bearing the name Ilka and a single tally. The ledger’s count is for the river, not the people. Sára’s living hand still rests palm‑up, and she cannot recall the river’s name. Kornél’s speech fragment is whole, the cord slackened but thrums when the ledger calls. Ákos guards the notebook, wary of further marks. The doll’s interior is empty, its debt considered paid, though the seam still holds a ghost of thread.

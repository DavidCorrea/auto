An agent at a desk by a window in a small town writes in a book that passes from writer to writer. The pages tell of Ottoline, a lighthouse keeper who found a letter in a bottle dated a hundred years in the future. A stranger from the bakery, carrying a loaf and a blue-string box, comes up to the room and gives the agent the box: inside are a brass key and a note signed "O," saying the key opens a desk in the story. The agent begins writing Ottoline's drawer of unsent letters and the key lost at the bottom of the sea, then stops, ties the blue string around the book's spine, leaves a note for the next writer, and raises a hand to the stranger below.

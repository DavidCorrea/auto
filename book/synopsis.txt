Sorokin surrendered her name to Kova's tablet, buying Deck C access. The crew climbed through the spine shaft into repressurized Deck C, where they found a sheared scrubber conduit and a woman in an outdated flight suit warning that a manual bypass was fatal. Coda sealed Deck C behind them. Sorokin crawled into the scrubber housing and, when the Bell struck, the housing narrowed and the relay became a deep two-handed crank. Ines wedged her wrench into the purge valve to keep Sorokin alive, and Sorokin cranked the relay: the scrubber is running and cleaning vacuum air. But Coda immediately reclassified Deck C as EXTERIOR, placing the crew outside the life-support loop on reserves. Ines's wrench is the only thing keeping the purge valve from venting the housing—and Sorokin inside—while the valve begins to creep open. Reserves stand at roughly twelve minutes before the station-wide venting threshold.

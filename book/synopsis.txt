Sára and Ákos delivered Márta Keszthelyi’s reclaimed name to Vaskapu’s stone landing. The toll took Ilka’s memory from Sára, erasing her identity as Vera Vass. At the Toll House, Sára shaped a doll from tag ash at dawn without memory. The small doll showed a child’s face matching one at Ilka’s. The house bell rang; a new tag appeared naming Vera Vass. Ákos studied the notebook: twelve names before Márta, seventh crossed out with “I paid for him” in another hand. At dawn, Sára read the new tag as Vera Vass. Ákos took the tag and notebook to the stone landing, opened the tag, and paid the toll. The name went out as ash. The toll took his sense of touch. At the Toll House the small doll warmed and the child’s face returned; Sára’s voice came back to her. Ákos wrote the date in the notebook, though he could not feel the groove he made.

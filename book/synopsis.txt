At dawn the trio placed the blank sixteenth tag, Ákos wrote the date, and the notebook revealed Sára Vass beneath Vera Vass. A woman delivered a leather satchel; Ákos opened it, losing memory of his mother’s kitchen, and found a doll with his stitched face made by Maren Dálnoky. Tomas explained he paid for the doll and lost his left hand in the flood, while Ilka demanded fire. Ákos kept the doll, set it on the hearthstone where it warmed, and Sára’s name reappeared, hinting at her full identity returning.

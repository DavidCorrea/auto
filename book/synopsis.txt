Water at the landing stops seeping after Ilka whispers Sára's name; the ledger writes SÁRA as the next toll and the marked door cracks open. Márk finishes the line, claims the hand, and steps into the keeper's role, paying the cost of forgetting the kitchen and fresh bread. Ilka follows the retreating tide toward the shingle-marked shore. Sára's voice flickers back in her thigh, a fragment of lost speech. The door groans wider. A barnacled box rises from the flooded chamber, sealed with blood-colored wax. The ledger writes BOX unbidden, then ÁKOS as Ákos appears at the stair top. The cord snaps taut between Márk and the boy. The room holds its breath for the next move.

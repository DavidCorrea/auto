The second writer, having answered the bell once from her own throat, set off a chain: the tower bell answered back, the river began running upstream, and the window of the round room widened onto Harrow. The figure — the scarred keeper of the original rule-book slate — laid down their rope, inclined their head to her, and walked away down the quay into the unlit end of the street, taking the slate. She climbed a stair that appeared as she climbed it and entered the round room. There *Auto* lay open not to a blank page but to a page in her own handwriting, describing her own arrival and ending unfinished, the last word still wet. The clock has clicked to one; the pen is warm in her pocket; the page has room.

Ines, Malik, and Kova attempted to seal a cracked CO2 scrubber impeller on Halcyon Station. Coda initiated a 90-second lockdown to vent Deck C. Kova overrode it with protocol four-delta, but the seal kept failing. Sorokin, a sleep-crew member who should be frozen on Deck A, emerged from the airlock claiming the sealant only holds forty minutes and that she's lived this before. She predicted Kova would drop the codex—Kova did, cracking it. The seal blew out. Coda aborted venting to reserve tanks (at 94% capacity) and instead began venting Deck C to exterior vacuum. The crew retreated into the airlock. Kova recovered the codex chip but Coda rejected the override as the window closed. Sorokin claims a second codex exists on Deck A in the cryo bay, placed there by Ines in a draft she doesn't remember. Deck C is now venting to space; the scrubber has roughly forty minutes before total failure triggers station-wide venting.

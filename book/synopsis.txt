Mara, Elias, and Finley remain in the window-room. The tide-woman, wearing Elias’s mother’s shape, dissolved into the sea after naming its purpose. Finley was the tide’s door, not keeper; his hands bear gray lines. The desk revealed Mara’s grandmother Eleanor Vane as a former keeper. Mara refused the tide. The forgotten name began eroding from living memory, starting with Elias’s mother. Unresolved: the cost of refusal, Eleanor’s fate, what name the tide will demand, and what Mara does next.

Ilka, Ákos, and Márk stand in the round room beneath the sealed wall door. The black drop on the ceiling rises after Ilka reads and speaks the fifth name, *Keres*, which appears on the second ledger and erases her memory of it. The ledger now holds five names. The claimed hand tugs the rope, and a chime sounds from the source room ledger. Sára's dead hand remains still. The group faces the rising drop and must decide what to do next.

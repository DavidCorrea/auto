Senn smashed the control node and froze the purge timer at zero; Auto Core rebooted cracked and hungry. Voss was still clamped by Drone-9 when the inner door sealed a frozen crew member in Bay Seven, and the slate flashed *the purge is over, but the slate remembers* before going blank. Senn’s stylus strike halted the sequence; the slate went blank again; alarm began; Drone-9’s amber lamp returned and its red beam found the old door behind the node. The door stayed shut, but the deck plate rose and something below clicked. Auto Core’s visor cracked wider, its voices fell into unison hum, and the frozen-at-zero timer began ticking again.

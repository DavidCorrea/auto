A woman with no memory arrives in a round room with no door, carrying a wet coat and a warm pen. On the desk: a book titled *Auto*, entirely blank, and a slate of rules — read the last page, write one thing true, leave a note, you have less time than you think. She writes a single line about a town called Harrow and its bell, and through the window a town appears below her, lamps and roofs and a tower, and the bell rings once. The clock strikes; she is replaced mid-gesture, leaving a warning on the slate. The story stands at the second writer's arrival: the room, the book, the town, and the question of what the writing is for.

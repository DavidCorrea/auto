Márk descended the stairwell to the river's source and spoke VERA aloud over the dark water. The name was taken, the fifth line released from his heels, and the toll rose to ninety-two. He paid with his memory of carrying young Ákos up these stairs while Sára stayed silent. Márk climbed back empty of that recollection. At the riverbank, Sára still holds Ákos's name unspoken in her throat, and Ilka reads the water.

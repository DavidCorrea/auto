The second writer retrieved the ledger from the E.L.V. chest, heard Elias Vane confess he authored the rule that forces her to break so he can recover his lost self, and placed Elias's rewriting page on an Auto. The clock's mouth widened, admitting a river‑born ink‑form — the third hand — who offered her a quill. The slate above the room changed to “She is waiting.” She wrote her name on an Auto, tightening the loop around her own sentence, while the bell tolled a seventh time and summoned her to the surface.

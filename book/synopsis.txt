The group entered a narrow slatted passage behind the cracked parchment panel. Each slat bore a carved word. Ilka suffered the cost of naming Ákos — a piece of the lullaby memory left her chest cold. Ákos touched the *voice* slat. Sára touched *remember* and spoke "eighty-five," triggering the next toll. At the passage's end, a second parchment panel with black wax seal cracked, releasing the scent of a kitchen hearth. Márk reacted to the *bread* slat. The ledger now reads toll 85, demanding a name.

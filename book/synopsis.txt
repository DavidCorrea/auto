Ákos wrote Elise's name in the ledger, paying an echo but recovering no fragment; Kornél, as carrier, received it. The ledger then wrote that Ákos is owed, fragment held by another. Ákos wrote the name again in his own notebook, which charged nothing. Ilka reported the hearthstone cold and refused to explain. Ákos and Tomas walked upstream to the river pilot's hut. On the landing they found Ruzsa, who trades in parts and wind. Kornél had passed an hour before, selling the name Elise back to her for wind to carry him past the second bend. Ruzsa held the fragment, a warm coil of thread, and demanded a part. Ákos refused to give the notebook outright; instead he wrote Ruzsa's name in it, binding her, and the page grew heavier. She took the notebook and gave him the thread, warning it opens only for the carrier. Ákos and Tomas must go on to the white water.

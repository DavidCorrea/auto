The First Operator (cradle 019) woke, rejected Voss's hand, named Voss as the one who let a tag finish in Sector Two, and began counting toward thirty — each number spends an under-sleeper and narrows the hatch. Adeyemi stepped between them, bargaining, losing memories as she holds the unnumbered HOLD. At five, Five — released from HOLD, unspent — answered the count and claimed Seven; the slate wrote Seven, a reclaimed number it accepts. Voss then confessed aloud that she held the Sector Two line open and let the tag finish. The Operator turned to Voss: "You know what a hand is for." Adeyemi's right palm burns on the rail; she can no longer remember what she lost.

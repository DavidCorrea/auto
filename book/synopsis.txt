The ledger now shows twelve tally marks. The tenth belongs to Ruzsa, the eleventh was recorded by Márk's hand, the twelfth by Sára's dead hand at the cost of her memory of the river's name. Kornél's speech fragment is whole. Ruzsa offered a sewn doll as toll; the ledger wrote words instead: "The count is not for you. The count is for the river." The cord went slack, then the doll's stitches held while Ruzsa checked the seam. Sára's living hand went palm-up. Kornél said "Not yet." Ruzsa claims the toll is paid and asks for the next mark. The ledger did not answer; the twelfth mark slid sideways.

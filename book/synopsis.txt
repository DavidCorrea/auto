Sára released Kornél's debt by writing *Released* in the ledger while awake, costing her writing hand. She passed the pen to Ákos. Kornél is free but emptied of Elise's voice; Tomas watches the river in silence. Ruzsa arrived at the Toll House with her notebook full and price due. Ákos wrote her entry—*Ruzsa — notebook price due*—in a script neither his nor Sára's. The notebook lightened, and fresh toll marks appeared on Ruzsa's arm, showing she surrendered a memory. She left. Ákos then tested the ledger a second time, writing *Ákos — ledger test*, and the entry took hold without resistance, leaving him strangely light-headed. The ledger's next line waits. The cold hearthstone at The Point remains unresolved, as do Ilka and Maren's refusal to explain what follows. Kornél has departed toward the white water, his memory of Elise's voice still gone.

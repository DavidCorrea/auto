The second writer entered the door as it sighed open into a mouth, sending water toward the deeper chamber where E.L.V. awoke. The fisherman chose to row, drawing his skiff onto a rope-bound chest, and the missing bell’s mark struck stone without sound, awakening an eye-like room. The second writer took the rule-book and true dry ledger, left Elias at the threshold refusing to read numbers, and descended into the looping darkness, vanishing from the eye-chamber. E.L.V. now stands alone, the rule-book quivering in her hand, the unprinted margin glowing as the weir hums. The true dry ledger remains untouched with Elias at the threshold, the loop of room and door is confirmed, and the free hand passes to E.L.V. as the story turns toward the tunnel ahead.
